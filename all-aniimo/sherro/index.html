No. 055

Sherro星骑士

Water水 DPS输出 D

Sherro has 3 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.星骑士 共有 3 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Sherro is the payoff of the water line and the species that makes the map part of its kit: Torrent Blade grows its reach and its damage when cast near water, and around it sit three slams in water, light and lightning plus a 70-power Flowing Water Slash. Where its relatives spend puddles for defence or for energy, this one spends the water on reach.星骑士是这条水线的兑现者，也是把地图变成技能组一部分的物种：激流刃在水边释放时范围和伤害都会变大；围绕它的是水、光、雷三种各一的重击，加上一发 70 威力的流水斩。它的亲戚花水滩去换防御或能量，而这一只把水换成了攻击距离。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Tidal Heart逐浪之心

In water terrain, or after absorbing water, its Water damage is up 25% for 20 seconds — the stronger of the two cards carrying this name; NO.053 and NO.054 give 20% for 10. Same name, different numbers, so compare the cards rather than the label.在水环境中或吸水后，水元素伤害提高 25%，持续 20 秒 —— 这是同名两张卡里更强的一张；NO.053 和 NO.054 给的是 20%、10 秒。同名不同值，要比的是卡，不是标签。

Skills技能

Split the way the official page splits them: Combat holds the skills you cast, Innate the basic attack. The list is the species', not the form's — a form does not change it.分法和官方页面一致：战斗技能是你会主动放的那几个，固有技能里是基础攻击。这份表属于物种，不属于形态 —— 换形态不会换掉它。
