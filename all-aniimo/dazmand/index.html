Dazmand has a single form recorded so far. Its stats, type, habitat and evolution requirement are below.霹雳蜥 目前只记录到一个形态。它的数值、属性、栖息地和进化条件都在下面。

Overview简介

Dazmand is the lightning ending of the Pomegg line — a species that starts grass and finishes as a lightning support, so its typing tells you nothing about where it came from. The kit is four lightning physical cards, including a 150-power Lightning Storm that costs nothing and an Electricity Shock that paralyzes only a target already carrying more than five debuff stacks.霹雳蜥是拉拉蛋那条线的雷系终点 —— 一个从草系起步、最后变成雷系辅助的物种，所以它的属性完全看不出它的出身。技能组是四张雷系物理卡，其中有一发不耗能量的 150 威力闪电风暴，以及一发只对「身上已有五层以上减益」的目标才造成麻痹的电击突袭。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Spotlight Moment高光时刻

The first skill it casts within 5 seconds of entering battle is free, on a 15-second cooldown. The window is the whole trait — you have to switch in and press something almost immediately, which rewards leading with it rather than bringing it in to react.登场后 5 秒内释放的第一个技能不消耗能量，冷却 15 秒。那个时间窗就是这个特性的全部 —— 你得把它换上来之后马上按技能，所以它奖励的是「用它先手」，不是「换上来应对」。
