About This Site关于本站

AniimoData is an unofficial guide to Aniimo, kept by people who play it. What is here, where it comes from, and what it is not.AniimoData 是一个非官方的 Aniimo 攻略站，由玩这个游戏的人自己维护。这里有什么、数据从哪来、以及这里不是什么。

Who We Are我们是谁

AniimoData is an unofficial fan site, written and maintained by players of Aniimo. We are not Pawprint Studio and have no connection to them or to anyone who works on the game. This began as notes we were keeping for ourselves, and it is still those notes — they simply have addresses now.AniimoData 是一个非官方的粉丝站，由 Aniimo 的玩家撰写与维护。我们不是 Pawprint Studio，与他们以及任何参与这款游戏开发的人都毫无关系。这里最初只是我们自己给自己记的笔记，到现在也仍然是那批笔记 —— 只是它们有了网址。

What We Do我们做什么

One aim runs through all of it: the game's own numbers, findable without having to open the game first. Today that means:所有内容都围绕同一件事：把游戏自己的数字整理成不用先打开游戏就能查到的东西。目前包括：

  • A page for every Aniimo — all 86 of them, each with stats, type, evolution line, habitat and tier每只 Aniimo 一个页面 —— 全部 86 只，各带数值、属性、进化线、栖息地和强度
  • A page for every form — 120 of them, because a form changes a creature's type and habitat, and one page per creature would have to be wrong about one of the two每个形态一个页面 —— 共 120 个；形态改的是属性和栖息地，按物种建页会把这两样里的一样的说错
  • Reference tables — items, bosses, the homestead, and which creatures carry which team buffs查询表 —— 物品、BOSS、家园，以及哪些物种携带哪种队伍增益
  • An interactive map — the world's regions, and what lives in them互动地图 —— 世界各地区，以及每个地区里有什么
  • Quests and codes — the main story in order, and the redeem codes we could corroborate across more than one source任务与兑换码 —— 主线顺序，以及能在多个来源上互相印证的兑换码
  • 50 answered questions — the ones people actually arrive with, including a section for the ones we could not answer50 条已作答的问题 —— 玩家真正带着来的那些，其中还单列了一节「我们答不了的」

Where the Numbers Come From数字从哪来

The official Aniimo wiki is the source we trust first, because it is the only one that has not been through somebody else's copy of it. When a second source disagrees, the first-party one wins; where nothing reliable exists, we leave a gap rather than guess at it.我们最信任的来源是官方 Aniimo 维基，因为它是唯一一个没有经过别人转手的来源。遇到第二来源与它冲突时，以第一方为准；两边都查不实的地方，我们宁可留空，也不猜一个填进去。

That rule is visible on the pages. The creatures the official record gives no habitat for are left blank here instead of filled in, and the gap is meant to be seen. The FAQ keeps a section for questions we cannot answer, and no page claims more than the sources behind it support.这条规矩在页面上是看得见的。官方记录没给栖息地的那些物种，这里就空着，这个缺口是有意露出来的。常见问题里单列了一节我们答不了的问题，也没有任何一页声称超出它背后来源能支撑的东西。
