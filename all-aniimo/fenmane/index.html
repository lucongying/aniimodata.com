Fenmane疾雷耶

Electric电 DPS输出 A

Fenmane has 2 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.疾雷耶 共有 2 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Fenmane is where the field starts paying off: it gets a second way to place one in Thunder Imprint, adds a 35-power Radiant Dart in light, and finishes with a 100-power Thunderfeather Volley at no energy cost. Everything in the kit is built around making lightning land twice, which is why a card printed at 1 power sits in the middle of a damage rotation.疾雷耶是电场开始兑现的地方：它多了一种布置电场的手段（雷印），多了一发 35 威力的光系辉光飞镖，并以一发不耗能量的 100 威力雷羽齐射收尾。技能组里的一切都是为了「让电系打两次」—— 这就是为什么一张写着 1 威力的卡会待在输出循环的正中间。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Scurry疾冲

A short forward dash. Short is the operative word — this is a repositioning tool, not travel. Use it to close a gap or break a line, not to cross a map.向前快速冲一小段。重点在「一小段」：它是位移手段，不是赶路手段 —— 用来贴身或者拉开距离，不是用来跑图的。

Trait特性

Thunderbond空雷契约

Every attack that connects adds a stack of Thunderbond, and at 30 stacks the next skill or Ultimate it uses puts it into Thunderwing Stance for 10 seconds. Thirty is high enough that this is a trait built up across a fight rather than one that fires early, and which button springs it is your call.每次攻击命中叠 1 层【空雷契约】，叠到 30 层时，接下来释放的技能或终结技会让它进入【雷翼姿态】，持续 10 秒。30 层不算少 —— 这是一个要在战斗里慢慢攒的特性，不是开局就亮的；而由技能还是终结技来触发，是你自己决定的。

Skills技能

So it multiplies lightning damage rather than dealing damage of its own — and because it travels with the enemy, walking away is not an option.一片持续跟随目标的雷电场，存在 8 秒；期间电场内目标每受到一次电元素攻击，就会额外再挨一次电元素攻击并叠一层【电元素异常】，最多每 0.2 秒触发一次。所以它是对电系伤害的放大器，本身不是伤害来源 —— 而且它跟着敌人走，躲是躲不掉的。
