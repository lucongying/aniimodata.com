Aniimo's Strongest Electric Team: Three of the Four Are There to Set Up Fulmintis《伊莫》最强电系阵容，四只里三只都在给电刺螳螂铺路

A four-Aniimo Electric party built around one damage dealer: what each of the other three does before Fulmintis comes in, and the order to switch them in.一套四只的电系阵容，全部围绕一个输出位搭：电刺螳螂进场之前，另外三只各自负责什么，以及换人的顺序。

The writer calls this the strongest Electric party going right now, and the division of labour inside it is unusually clean. Blazen takes the boss's break gauge down, Dazmand lifts the whole team's Electric crit rate, Luminelle pushes the Electric damage bonus to the cap, and then Fulmintis comes in, holds its attack button down and pours the damage out.这套阵容叫「永动电锯流」，是现在电系配队里最能打的一种。四只伊莫分工非常干脆：雷光貂去削 BOSS 的韧性，霹雳蜥把全队的电元素暴击率抬起来，吊灯水母把电系增伤拍到顶，最后电刺螳螂进场，按住 Q 把伤害全灌出去。

Only Fulmintis is really the lead. The other three spend the whole fight clearing a path for it, and that is where this party is comfortable to play: each of them does one errand and leaves, so there is nobody to babysit. By the time Fulmintis is on the field the crit rate is where it needs to be and the Electric bonus is at its ceiling — all that is left is to keep the skill from stopping.全场只有电刺螳螂是真正的主角，另外三只干的活都是给它让路。这套打法最舒服的地方也在这里：前面三只各做各的一件事，做完就退场，你不用分心去照顾谁；等轮到电刺螳螂的时候，场上已经是一个暴击率拉满、电系增伤见顶的局面，它要做的只剩一件事 —— 让技能别停。

Open with Blazen: take the gauge down first开局交给雷光貂，先把韧性拆下来

The first thing you do to a boss is always the same — take its break gauge down. Blazen (Electric · Break) is the one built for it: switch it in and keep hitting the break button, and do not think about anything else for this stretch.打 BOSS 的第一件事永远是拆韧性。雷光貂（Blazen，电 · 击破）就是专门干这个的，上场之后无脑击破就行，不用想别的。

One thing to watch: do not empty the gauge in one go — leave the last segment on it. The two supports behind Blazen need a window to come in on, and a boss that is still down is that window. While it is sitting there you can switch them in one at a time and lay the buffs down at your own pace instead of racing a timer.这里要留个心眼：别一口气把韧性条打空，留下最后一段。这么做的目的是给后面两只辅助腾出进场的时间 —— BOSS 还瘫着的时候，你才能从容地把它们一个接一个换上来，把增益铺好。

Dazmand carries it on and lifts the crit rate霹雳蜥接棒，全队暴击先抬一档

The moment the boss breaks, Dazmand (Electric · Support) comes in and casts Thunder Sweep, which hands the whole team +20% lightning crit rate for 20 seconds.BOSS 一断，霹雳蜥（Dazmand，电 · 辅助）立刻换上，直接放霹雳横扫，给全队拉起 20% 的电元素暴击率，持续 20 秒。

Twenty percent on an ordinary team-mate is a modest lift. On Fulmintis it is a different thing altogether, and that comes back around when Fulmintis comes in.这 20% 落在普通队友身上只是一点增伤，落到电刺螳螂身上就完全是另一回事 ——等它上场那一段再讲。

Luminelle puts the Electric bonus at the ceiling吊灯水母一拍，电系增伤见顶

Luminelle (Electric · Support) follows, and this is the Electric party's old familiar. It drops its field, which leaves +20% lightning damage underfoot for 30 seconds — thirty seconds is far longer than the effect that places it, so the field is the part you are buying, and it outlasts the rest of the fight.电队的老熟人吊灯水母（Luminelle，电 · 辅助）紧接着进场，直接开大：它在脚下留下一片电场，站在里面电元素强化 +20%，持续 30 秒。30 秒远长于放出它的那一下，所以真正买到的是那片电场，它比这场仗剩下的时间还长。

It also recharges Electric team-mates specifically — +27% basic attack damage and +16% BREAK efficiency for 20 seconds — and the break efficiency is what lets it take the last of the boss's gauge off on its way through.它同时给电元素队友充能：普攻伤害 +27%、击破效率 +16%，持续 20 秒。其中的击破效率，就是它顺手把 BOSS 最后那点韧性一并收掉的原因。

That is the setup complete. The boss is down, the Electric bonus is up, and the crit rate is on.到这一步，场面就齐了：BOSS 跪着，电系增益满着，暴击率也上来了。

Fulmintis comes in, and then it is one button轮到电刺螳螂，按住 Q 就行

Fulmintis (Electric · DPS) is the only damage dealer in this party and the only one you actually play. Switch it in, start Lightning Rush (电刃乱舞), and from there the job is to hold the button and not let go.电刺螳螂（Fulmintis，电 · 输出）是这套阵容里唯一的输出位，也是全场唯一要你真正上手操作的一只。切出来，开启电刃乱舞，剩下的就是按住 Q不松手。

What makes it work is that its buffs pay for themselves. Its trait, Electro Stash, gives back 4 energy every time one of its skills lands a critical hit — and energy is exactly what keeps Lightning Rush running, so the loop feeds itself. It also carries Lightning Surge (强化电刃), a flat +35% skill crit rate for 20 seconds. Add Thunder Sweep's 20% and the 10% base every species starts from, and the three stack to 65% — enough to put every skill it casts high up the damage it can deal.它强在这一身增益能自我循环。电刺螳螂的特性是电力积蓄，技能只要造成暴击就直接回 4 点能量，而回能正是它技能不停的前提；它自己还带一张强化电刃，技能暴击率固定 +35%，持续 20 秒。再加霹雳蜥铺下的那一档 20% 和 10% 的基础暴击率，三档叠起来就是 65% —— 这个数字足以把它每一次技能的伤害都抬到很高的位置。

High damage, fast energy return, and a loop that pays for the next hit with the last one: it keeps going until the boss stops moving.高额的伤害、迅猛的回能，加上这套自我循环，它能一直输出到 BOSS 倒下，上演血条消失术。

The order, start to finish上手就照这个顺序排

There is nothing extra to do in a fight, and the order does not change: Blazen takes the gauge down and leaves the last segment; Dazmand comes in and lifts the crit rate; Luminelle drops the field, caps the Electric damage and finishes the gauge off; and then Fulmintis, Lightning Rush, button held down.打起来其实没有多余的操作，顺序固定下来就四步：雷光貂先拆韧性，留最后一段；霹雳蜥换上抬暴击；吊灯水母开大，把电系增伤拉满、顺手收掉最后一点韧性；最后换电刺螳螂，开电刃乱舞，按住 Q 不放。

The reasoning is not complicated. Three of the four each handle one quick errand, finish it and step off, leaving the stage to the only lead in the party. By the time Fulmintis arrives the crit and the damage bonus are both already where they need to be, and its whole job is turning that into damage that actually lands.这么排的道理不复杂：前面三只各自负责一件速办的事，做完全部退场，把舞台让给唯一的主角；等电刺螳螂上场时，该给的暴击、该给的增伤都已经到位，它只管把这份优势换成实打实的伤害。
