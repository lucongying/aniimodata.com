Squashel烈拳尾

Fire火 Break击破

Squashel has a single form recorded so far. Its stats, type, habitat and evolution requirement are below.烈拳尾 目前只记录到一个形态。它的数值、属性、栖息地和进化条件都在下面。

Overview简介

Squashel is this line's only stage-3 form and it carries the largest single number in the family: a 209-power Blazing Rush that costs no energy, beside a 30-energy 85-power fist barrage. It is also the species whose name appears inside its own skill text — Blazing Rush names Squashel in the description instead of saying "the user".烈拳尾是这条线上唯一的三阶形态，并且带着这一家子里最大的一个数字：一发不耗能量的 209 威力烈焰突进，旁边是一次 30 能量换 85 威力的连拳。它也是那种名字出现在自己技能文本里的物种 —— 烈焰突进的描述里直接写了「烈拳尾」，而不是写「自身」。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Raging Fire烈火

Cast a skill, then land a basic attack within 10 seconds: that attack skips straight to its final hit, and the Power on it goes from 4 to 25. You get the payoff of a full basic-attack chain without standing there through the whole chain.放完技能后 10 秒内的下一次普攻，会直接打出普攻的最后一段，那一段的威力从 4 提到 25。等于不用把整套普攻打完，就能拿到最后一段的收益。
