Party队伍

Team Building队伍搭配

Four Aniimo go into a fight and the game sorts every creature into one of five roles, so a party is always missing one of them. This page is about that arithmetic, what each role's bucket actually looks like in the official stats, and the skill cards that pay the rest of the party instead of themselves.一场仗带四只 Aniimo，而游戏把每个物种都归进五种定位之一，所以任何一支队伍都必然缺一整种定位。这页讲的就是这道算术、每种定位在官方数值里到底长什么样，以及那些「不给自己的、给全队的」技能卡。

The best party for each element最强属性配队：每种属性一支 (Last updated: October 24, 2026)（最后更新：2026 年 10 月 24 日）

Eight parties, one per element, four seats in each. The list is the author's, put together over several hundred hours in the game — seats were changed in play rather than assembled from a stat table, so where the paper answer and the fight disagree the list keeps the fight's. The role under every name is still the one the official record files that creature under, and the lists were not filtered by it: stacking a role is a choice, so the Electric party here is one DPS and three Support. A seat marked or has two answers on this site; either takes it.八种属性各一支队伍，每支四个位置。这份名单是本站作者在游戏里打了几百个小时之后排出来的 —— 格子是在实战里换过的，不是照着数值表拼的，所以有些地方纸面算是一回事、打起来是另一回事，名单留的是打起来那个。每只名字下面印的还是官方记录里它归的那个定位，而名单没有按定位筛过：叠同一种定位本来就是选择，所以电系那队是一个输出加三个辅助。标着或的位置有两个答案，用哪个都行。

What each role brings, in numbers每种定位带来什么，用数字说

Each row is every species filed under that role, averaged across the six stats the game prints. The range beside each average is the spread inside the bucket, and it is printed because that spread is often wider than the gap between two buckets — a mean over five creatures reads as more precision than it has. The averages cover the 88 resident species of the publisher's main index. The Whisperwake Isles dex — 29 more, kept under its own numbering — is not in these numbers yet, and neither are the three Season 1 keepsake species (Hoptail, Hypnyto, Jaguaraun) added with the 2026-10-29 update; their rows are in the All Aniimo table and on their own pages.每一行是归在该定位下的全部物种，按游戏印的六项数值取平均。每个均值旁边那个区间是这一档内部的极差；之所以要印出来，是因为它经常比两个档之间的差距还大 —— 五只生物算出来的均值，看起来比它实际有的精度要准。这里的平均只算发行方主图鉴那 88 只常驻物种。 潮音群島另一套编号下的 29 只还没进这些数字，2026-10-29 那次更新加进来的三只赛季典藏物种（蹦蹦貂、迷瞳翼、奔雷豹）同样不在内 —— 它们的行都在「全部 Aniimo」表里，各自的页面也都在。

The DPS card prints one creature per element — Electric, Fire, Grass, Water, Dark, a creature's element being its first type — the strongest of each filed under that role. The other four cards print five picks for that role instead, strongest first: their buckets are too thin to split (Break 26, Support 19, Regen 8, Heal 5), and split by element the Heal card would show one tile out of five. Either way the ranking is the stat the card leads with, and the chip on a tile is just the creature's first type.输出那张卡按元素各放一只 —— 电、火、草、水、暗，元素按物种的第一个属性算 —— 取的是归在输出下、该元素里最强的一只。其余四张卡不按元素分，印这个定位推荐的五只，由强到弱：它们的池子薄得分不开（击破 26、辅助 19、回能 8、治疗 5），真按元素分，治疗那张卡五格只填得上一格。两种排法比的都是卡片第一句拿的那项数值；每格上面那颗元素标签只是物种的第一个属性。

DPS输出

The only role that leads on ATK, and the widest bucket in the index at 30 species. It is also the lowest for BREAK and the lowest for both defences: the ATK ceiling of 130 is the highest of any role and the HP ceiling of 104 is the lowest. That is what the label buys and what it costs, not a verdict on how long a DPS lasts in a fight.唯一在 ATK 上领跑的定位，也是全图鉴最大的一档，30 个物种。同时它是 BREAK 最低的一档，也是两个防御都最低的一档：ATK 上限 130 是全定位最高，生命上限 104 是全定位最低。这就是这个标签买到的东西和付出的代价，而不是「输出在场上能活多久」的判决。

Heal治疗

Five creatures, and the toughest of them on paper. Heal has the highest HP mean (102.6) and the highest M.DEF mean (87.4), and it is the only role whose ATK ceiling stops below 100. Five of 88 is the scarcest label by a wide margin — the next smallest is Regen at eight — and that scarcity is the practical form of the four-and-five problem: the role you are most likely to be missing is the one the index carries fewest of.五只生物，而且是纸面上最硬的一档。 治疗的生命均值最高（102.6）、魔防均值最高（87.4），也是唯一一个 ATK 上限低于 100 的定位。88 分之 5 是最稀缺的标签，差得很远 —— 下一档是回能的 8 只 —— 而这种稀缺就是「五定位四位置」的实际形态：你最容易缺的那种定位，恰好就是图鉴收得最少的那种。

Filling the four把这四个位置填满

Four decisions, in the order they come up. The short version is the rule the FAQ already gives in one sentence — one of each role, and a second creature of a role rather than a fifth damage dealer. What follows is the arithmetic behind that and the two places where the roster decides for you.四个决定，按它们通常出现的顺序。短版本就是常见问题页已经用一句话给过的那条规则 —— 每种定位各一只，要加就加同定位的第二只，而不是第五个输出。下面讲的是那条规则背后的算术，以及名单替你拿主意的两个地方。

  • Fill the four fighting roles first. DPS for the damage, Break to open the window, Support for the buffs and Regen to keep energy moving, with Heal in the last seat if Support cannot hold the party up. Each of those four leads the index on a different stat in the table above, which is what makes the rule a defensible default rather than an arbitrary one.先把四种战斗定位填上。 输出负责伤害、击破负责把窗口打开、辅助负责增益、回能负责让能量转起来，最后一位留给治疗 —— 前提是辅助撑不住全队。上面那张表里，这四种各自领跑不同的一项数值，所以这条规则是站得住的默认解，而不是随便定的。
  • The scarce roles are Heal and Regen. Thirteen of the 88 species carry one of those two labels — five and eight. If the creature you want does not exist in your collection yet, that is the shape of the index rather than a gap in your play, and it is why Heal is the seat most often left empty.稀缺的是治疗和回能。 88 个物种里只有十三个挂这两个标签 —— 五个和八个。如果你想要的那只还没进你的收藏，那是图鉴的形状，不是你玩得不好；也正是这个原因让治疗成为最常空着的那一格。
  • Stacking one role is a trade the game charges for in energy. Two DPS and no Regen looks like more damage; the FAQ puts the cost down to energy, and we cannot show that budget — we hold every card's energy cost and no source gives us a recovery rate to weigh it against. What the skill table below does show is where the energy comes from: the cards that hand a teammate energy or energy recovery belong to Regen and Heal creatures, and none of them is a DPS.叠同一种定位，游戏收的过路费是能量。 两个输出不带回能，看起来伤害更高；常见问题页把代价归到能量上，而这份预算我们给不出来 —— 每张卡的能量消耗我们都有，但没有任何来源给过回能速率来和它对账。下面那张技能表能证明的是能量从哪来：把能量或回能交给队友的卡都挂在回能与治疗的物种身上，一张输出的都没有。
  • The elements are a supply problem before they are a tactics problem. Nine elements spread across 88 creatures is a very uneven split, and the strip below is the whole picture. One line of it is short enough to decide a party on its own.属性首先是供给问题，然后才是战术问题。 九种属性铺在 88 只生物上，分布得非常不匀，下面那条带子就是全貌。其中有一行的短，短到能自己决定一支队伍。

Reading a candidate看懂一只候选

Everything above is about the party. This is about the one creature whose page you have open, and the two traps in the way the game names things.上面讲的都是队伍。这一节讲你正开着的那一只，以及游戏起名方式埋的两个坑。

The six numbers这六项数值

  • HP and ATK are what they look like, and P.DEF and M.DEF are physical and magical defence.生命和攻击就是字面意思，物防和魔防是物理防御和魔法防御。
  • BREAK is a stat, not the Break role. It decides how fast a target's break gauge fills, so it matters most on the creatures whose job is opening that window — which is why the Break bucket leads it and the DPS bucket trails it.BREAK 是一项数值，不是「击破」这个定位。 它决定目标的韧性条涨得多快，所以它最要紧的地方正是「负责把窗口打开」的那些生物身上 —— 这也是击破档领跑它、输出档垫底的原因。
  • REGEN is a stat, not the Regen role. It governs how much energy the creature returns, which is why the Regen bucket leads it.REGEN 是一项数值，不是「回能」这个定位。 它管这只生物回多少能量，所以回能档领跑它。
  • None of the six is a rating. No source we hold gives damage as a formula over them, so nothing on this site adds them up or ranks a creature by its total. The role table above is per-stat arithmetic, and it stops there.六项里没有一项是评分。 我们手上的来源没有一份给出「以它们为自变量的伤害公式」，所以站上任何地方都不把它们加起来、也不按总和给生物排名。上面那张定位表是逐项算术，它就停在那里。

Two names, used twice两个名字，各用两次

  • Break is a role and BREAK is a stat. A creature can be filed under Break and carry a low BREAK number; the two sit on the same page and are unrelated.Break 是定位，BREAK 是数值。 一只生物可以归在击破档里而 BREAK 数值很低；两者印在同一页上，但互不相干。
  • Regen is a role and REGEN is a stat. Same shape, same trap.Regen 是定位，REGEN 是数值。 同一个形状，同一个坑。
  • In Chinese the collision is total. The Break role and the BREAK stat are both 击破, and the Regen role and the REGEN stat are both 回能 — the same two characters, with nothing in the words to tell them apart. The English at least capitalises the stat.中文侧这两个名字撞得比英文彻底。 击破既是定位也是 BREAK 数值，回能既是定位也是 REGEN 数值 —— 同样两个字，词语本身没有任何东西能把它们分开。英文至少还能靠大小写区分数值那一侧。
  • Where each one shows up. On a species page the role is the tag beside the name; BREAK and REGEN are two of the six numbers in the stat readout underneath.各自出现在哪。 在物种页上，定位是名字旁边那个标签；BREAK 和 REGEN 是下面数值栏六项里的两项。

This page has no damage model and neither does the rest of the site. Nothing above converts a stat into damage, because no source we hold has the formula.这页没有伤害模型，站上其它页面也没有。上面没有任何东西把数值换算成伤害，因为我们手上的来源没有那份公式。
