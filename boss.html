Fights战斗

BossesBOSS

“Boss” is a word Aniimo uses for two very different fights, and most bad boss advice is two people comparing answers that were never about the same one. This page is the map of which is which — then where every Alpha and Omega stands, what the fight asks of you, and what a boss is actually for.在 Aniimo 里「BOSS」这个词指两场很不一样的仗，而大部分关于 BOSS 的烂建议，都是两个人在拿根本不指同一场仗的答案互相比。这一页先把两者分清楚，再列每一只 Alpha 和 Omega 在哪、这场仗要你做什么，以及打 BOSS 到底是为了什么。

Two kinds of world boss, one word两种世界 BOSS，共用一个词

Most bad boss advice is two people comparing answers that were never about the same fight. Alphas are world bosses you walk up to — seventeen species, each one patrolling a fixed patch of a region. Omegas are set-pieces at the end of a quest chain: three of them, one per region, and not on your map at all until that quest is done. They share a reward vocabulary and very little else. There is a third thing people mean by “boss” in this game, the Sanctums, and those are not fights at all — they have their own section further down this page.大部分关于 BOSS 的烂建议，都是两个人在拿根本不指同一场仗的答案互相比。Alpha 是走上去就能打的世界 BOSS —— 十七个物种，各自在某个地区的一小块固定范围里巡逻。Omega 是任务链尽头的定点演出：三只，每个地区一只，那条任务没做完之前，地图上根本没有它。两者只共用一套奖励词汇，别的几乎全不一样。这个词在这个游戏里还有第三种指法 —— Sanctum —— 而那个连仗都不是，它在本页下面单独一段。

Price is the other half of the difference. An Alpha claim costs 30 Primegy, and it comes out of the same meter that pays for crafting and for spare Aniipods back at your base — so what an Alpha run really costs you is the crafting you did not do that day. Omegas are claimed out of that meter as well.差别另一半在代价。一次 Alpha 领取要 30 Primegy，而且是和家园做装备、换备用 Aniipod 用同一个池子 —— 所以一趟 Alpha 真正的成本，是你那天少做的那件装备。Omega 也从同一个池子里扣。

They walk a fixed patch of one region rather than spawning on a timer, so finding one is a matter of going where it walks and looking for the largest thing on the horizon — Alphas are the same animal, bigger. The levels are the least certain numbers on this page. They are the band a boss has been recorded at by the people keeping the map, not something the game prints on screen. Use them as the order to take the seventeen in, and nothing finer than that. The Type column is back, and it settles an argument. It was missing for a week, because the per-boss element lists in circulation disagreed with our own species table on three of these: the boss databases gave Geoclaw Ice, Ignitis Dark and Sherro Water, where our table said Grass-and-Earth, Fire and Electric, and nothing we had could break the tie. The publisher's own wiki does. The boss databases were right on all three and our table was wrong — Geoclaw is Ice, Ignitis is Dark, Sherro is Water — and those rows have been corrected. So the column is the official record's per-species value, read the same way as the types in the Aniimo table. It is worth knowing that we lost that argument, and that the loss is the reason this column can be here at all. Two of the pins are hidden at first, as well: Geoclaw and Minespine do not appear on the map until the Branch has been given a 120-Amber blessing, the Earthwake Prayer. A region that looks empty is not always an empty one.这一页最不确定的数字就是等级。 那是维护地图的那批人记录下来的区间，不是游戏自己在屏幕上写的。把它当成「这十七只按这个顺序去打」，别再细。属性这一列回来了，它了结了一场争论。 之前空了一周，因为在能核对的那几只上，流传的逐只属性表和我们的物种表是对不上的 —— 那些 BOSS 资料库给晶壳蟹冰、给淬刃螳螂暗、给星骑士水，而我们的总表给的分别是草土、火、电，当时手上没有任何东西能判定谁对。发行方自己的维基能。三只上 BOSS 资料库全对，我们全错 —— 晶壳蟹是冰、淬刃螳螂是暗、星骑士是水 —— 那几行已经改过来了。所以这一列现在是官方记录的逐物种值，读法和 Aniimo 总表里的属性一样。值得记住的是我们输掉了那场争论，而正因为输了，这一列今天才放得上来。另外有两颗图标一开始是藏着的：晶壳蟹和簇晶脊龙，要等 Branch 收到那份 120 Amber 的祝福，Earthwake Prayer，才会出现在地图上。看着空荡荡的地区，不一定是空的。

Omega Aniimo: three fights behind questsOmega Aniimo：三场锁在任务后面的仗

An Omega is not a rarer Alpha. It is a set-piece sitting at the end of a quest chain, one per region, and until that quest is finished the boss is not on the map for you at all — which is why two players at the same level can sincerely disagree about whether a given Omega exists.Omega 不是更稀有的 Alpha。它是摆在任务链终点的一场压轴戏，一个地区一只，而且那条任务没做完之前，它对你来说根本不在图上 —— 所以两个同等级的玩家，会真诚地为某只 Omega 到底存不存在吵起来。

Three species, four fights. Every list you find will hand you a different number here, and both of them are counting something real. Three Omega species exist. The count of four comes from Sherro, which has a second and older encounter during the main story, in Ancient Sacred Fragrance, before the proper Omega fight — the same animal on a different script, and the databases that list both of them call the two Elderly Omega Sherro and Young Omega Sherro at the same level band. If you have beaten “an Omega Sherro” and the region has not cleared, you have almost certainly beaten the wrong one.三个物种，四场仗。 你找到的每份清单都会给你一个不一样的数字，而两个数字都在数真东西。Omega 物种是三个；四这个数来自星骑士 —— 它在主线 Ancient Sacred Fragrance 里还有一场更早的遭遇战，在正式的 Omega 之前，同一只动物、另一套脚本 —— 把两场都列出来的资料库，管它们叫 Elderly Omega Sherro 和 Young Omega Sherro，等级区间一样。如果你打过「一只 Omega 星骑士」但地区没清，那你打的几乎肯定是错的那一场。

The prompt is the mechanic提示就是机制

Every scripted boss announces itself the same way: a line of text appears on screen mid-fight and names the thing that has just changed. Reading that line is the fight. The wipe is nearly always somebody who kept attacking through the prompt instead of doing what it said.Aniimo 里每一场有脚本的 BOSS 战都用同一种方式通知你：打到一半屏幕上一行字弹出来，点名刚刚变了的东西。读那行字就是这场仗。团灭基本永远是把提示当背景音、继续输出的人。

  • Turbo蓬蓬羊 — Floats out of reach and calls in Nimbis. Lure it into sticking to two of them, and it becomes too heavy to stay up — the crash is the opening. The Nimbis are the fight, not a distraction from it.飘到打不着的高度，叫出 Nimbis。把它引过去粘上两只，它就重得浮不起来 —— 摔下来那一下才是输出窗口。所以 Nimbis 是这场仗本身，不是干扰。
  • Leafy莲冠龙 — Covers itself in Lotus Essence, and the prompt that follows says BREAK the Shield. Breaking it is what stops the health coming back. That line is not a hint, it is the instruction.用 Lotus Essence 把自己罩起来，紧跟着的提示就是 BREAK the Shield。打碎它才能止住回血。那句不是暗示，是指令。
  • Grizbo暴睡熊 — Enters an Enraged state and attacks without stopping, with a timer beside its health bar. Dodge until the timer runs out. When Enraged ends it stands there unable to act, and that is the window the whole fight is built around.进入 Enraged 状态，不停手地打，血条旁边挂着一个计时器。计时器走完之前只管躲；Enraged 一结束，它会站在原地不能动，整场仗就是围着那个窗口搭的。
  • Magmarex熔岩暴龙 — Heat climbs through the fight and the prompts land at 50% and 80%, each one telling you to cool it with Water or Ice attacks. Let it reach the top and it overheats: its attack rises sharply and its Water resistance collapses.整场仗热量在涨，提示落在 50% 和 80% 两处，每一次都是让你用水或冰属性攻击给它降温。真让它冲到顶就会过热 —— 攻击大幅提高，水抗大幅下降。
  • Ignitis淬刃螳螂 — Splits into clones of itself. Only one of them carries the health bar, and hitting that one destroys the rest. It also only appears at night.分裂出自己的分身。只有一只身上挂着血条，打中那一只其余全消。另外它只在夜里出现。
  • Glynsera霜锋狼 — Sends ice blades sweeping across the arena, and Fire skills destroy them — the blades are a mechanic with an answer rather than damage to walk out of.放出冰刃横扫场地，而火属性技能能把冰刃打掉 —— 冰刃是有解法的机制，不只是要躲开的伤害。
  • Luminelle吊灯水母 — Smaller Luminelles power the Alpha up, and defeating all of them is what interrupts it. A second warning lands when it is about to go overcharged.更小的 Luminelle 在给它充能，全部打倒才能打断。它快要过载时会再弹一条警告。
  • Blazen雷光貂 — Goes Overcharged.进入 Overcharged 状态。
  • Minespine簇晶脊龙 — Drives seismic waves out across the ground.贴着地面推出地震波。
  • Tuckin埋埋 — Summons Hummins, and they feed their health into it. Kill them quickly — once they are down, Tuckin goes vulnerable and the prompt tells you to hit it with a [Hammer].召出 Hummins，它们会把自己的血喂给它。尽快打掉 —— 它们一倒，埋埋会进入可击破状态，提示会告诉你用 [Hammer] 打它。
  • Sherro星骑士 — Raises a Shelly shield that reflects damage straight back at you. The answer is to kill Shelly first; hitting harder only kills you faster.架起会反弹伤害的 Shelly 盾。解法是先打倒 Shelly；打得越狠，只是死得越快。
  • Infergon炎铠龙 — Drops Fire Element Crystallites that are about to explode. Shatter them to carve out safe ground, so that the shock wave has somewhere to pass you by.丢下即将爆炸的 Fire Element Crystallites。打碎它们清出安全地面，好让冲击波来的时候有地方站。

Grizbo's phase is worth singling out because it is the one that reads as a bug when you meet it cold: a boss that suddenly cannot be out-damaged, a visible countdown, and then a stretch where it cannot fight back at all. Nothing has gone wrong. The damage you were doing was never going to matter until the timer said so.暴睡熊那一段值得单独拎出来，因为它冷着遇见时最像 bug：一只突然怎么都打不动的 BOSS、一个看得见的倒计时，然后是一段它完全没法还手的时间。什么都没出错。你之前打的那点伤害，本来就要等计时器同意才算数。

What a boss is actually for打 BOSS 到底是为了什么

The rewards are not the point. Seventeen of the twenty world bosses are the last step of an evolution — beat one and you have unlocked the final form of a species you have probably been carrying since the first region. The three that gate nothing are Rookey, Glynsera and Ignitis.奖励不是重点。二十只世界 BOSS 里有十七只是某条进化线的最后一步 —— 打掉一只，你就解锁了一个大概从第一张图就一直带着的物种的最终形态。什么都不卡的三个是盔卫士、霜锋狼和淬刃螳螂。

  • The material comes off the kill, not the boss. Evolution materials are the Alpha and Omega drop, and how much you get scales with your Character Rank and Badge level — so the same boss is worth more to you later, and grinding it early is the least efficient way to do it. The game will name the boss you need: pause, then Institute Guide, Aniimo Challenge, Alpha Challenge Evolution Material.材料是打出来的，不是 BOSS 本身。 进化材料来自 Alpha 和 Omega 的掉落，拿多少随你的 Character Rank 和 Badge 等级走 —— 所以同一只 BOSS 对你越往后越值钱，早刷是效率最低的刷法。游戏自己会告诉你缺哪只：暂停，然后 Institute Guide、Aniimo Challenge、Alpha Challenge Evolution Material。
  • The kill is only one of the conditions. Several evolutions want something else as well, and this is where most people get stuck with a material in hand and nothing happening: a level, the [Tenacious] personality, acquired BREAK Potential at 6 or above, a Perfect Potential rating, the passive talent [Peculiar Rock Ball] learned, a twenty-second dig at Sanctum: Twisted Roots, a soak in a hot spring, standing on the lotus leaf Alpha Leafy lives beside, or simply the hour — some of these complete only at night.打掉只是条件之一。 有几条进化还额外要别的东西，大多数人卡在手里拿着材料却什么都不发生就是卡在这里：某个等级、[Tenacious] 性格、已获得 BREAK Potential 6 以上、Perfect 的 Potential 评价、学会被动词条 [Peculiar Rock Ball]、在 Sanctum: Twisted Roots 挖二十秒、泡一次温泉、站到 Alpha 莲冠龙守着的那片莲叶上，或者干脆只是时间 —— 有几条只在夜里才完成。
  • Badges are what a boss does for the region. The Silver Badge wants 70% collection in that region and that region's Alpha defeated, and it is what lets you infuse a Lumin Amber at a Branch at all — weather, and Prismana Flow. That is the real reason to clear the map's Alphas rather than the drops.徽章才是 BOSS 对地区的意义。 Silver Badge 要该地区 70% 收集度加上该地区的 Alpha 已击败，而它是你能不能到 Branch 注入 Lumin Amber 的前提 —— 天气、Prismana Flow 都靠它。这才是清地图上那些 Alpha 的真正理由，不是掉落。

Sanctums, which are not bossesSanctum —— 它不是 BOSS

Seventeen rooms across the Breezy Plains map — fourteen solo and three that need two players — and they are the third thing people mean by “boss” in this game. Two things about them are usually got wrong. They are not gated by level: what opens a Sanctum is how much of its region you have caught. And you cannot bring your own Aniimo inside one — the room is solved with the mechanisms in it, not with your team.Breezy Plains 这张地图上一共有十七个房间 —— 十四个单人、三个要两个人 —— 它们就是这个游戏里「BOSS」的第三种指法。关于它们，最常见的两个误解是：它不按等级开，开一个 Sanctum 的条件是你在这个地区抓到了多少；以及你带不进自己的 Aniimo，房间是靠里面的机关解开的，不是靠你的队伍。

  • A first clear pays 500 EXP and one Lumin Amber of that region. A repeat visit keeps only the transport pin. There is no farm here, which is the argument for doing the rooms as you pass through rather than saving them up for later.首次通关给 500 经验，加上该地区的一颗 Lumin Amber。重复进去只保留传送点。这里没有可刷的东西 —— 这就是「路过就顺手打掉」比「留着以后再说」更划算的理由。
  • The chest grade is the reward, not the room. Basic pays 5 Glimmer, 5,000 Credits and 5 Sparkling Crystals; Standard pays double that plus one Rare held item; Premium pays 20 Glimmer, 20,000 Credits, 15 Sparkling Crystals and one Epic held item. The fifty-four chests are spread across the seventeen rooms, and fourteen of them are inside the three that need two players.奖励看箱子等级，不看房间。 Basic 给 5 Glimmer、5,000 Credits、5 颗 Sparkling Crystal；Standard 给双倍，外加一件 Rare 携带道具；Premium 给 20 Glimmer、20,000 Credits、15 颗 Sparkling Crystal，外加一件 Epic 携带道具。五十四个箱子分散在这十七个房间里，其中十四个在那三个要两个人的房间里。
  • Four of the seventeen have no visible entrance. The game does not mark them, each is blocked by something different, and one of them sits in a region you would not be levelled for yet.十七个里有四个没有可见的入口。 游戏不给它们做标记，每个被挡住的原因都不一样，其中一个所在地区，等级根本不是给你现在去的。
  • Expect other checklists to use names the game no longer prints. The September 2026 relaunch renamed fourteen of the seventeen rooms: what the older guides call Sanctum: Water to Ice is Frigid Waters now, and Chasing Clouds was Mistrider. A guide that sends you to a name you cannot find is not necessarily sending you to the wrong room.别的清单上的名字，游戏里已经印不出来了。 2026 年 9 月的重启改了十七个里十四个房间的名字：老攻略说的 Sanctum: Water to Ice，现在叫 Frigid Waters；Chasing Clouds 原来叫 Mistrider。一份把你指到一个游戏里找不到的名字的攻略，不一定就是把你指错了房间。
  • Clearing rooms is its own badge track. Sanctum Delver badges land at 3, 5, 10 and 15 rooms cleared, and the rooms also feed Field Notes, which is one of the places Research points come from.清房间本身也是一条徽章线。 Sanctum Delver 徽章在清掉 3、5、10、15 个房间时各给一次；这些房间同时也是 Field Note 的完成条件之一，而 Research 点就是从那里来的。

One number here is disputed, and we print the better-supported one. Two independent lists agree on 17 — fourteen solo plus three co-op — while a third-party database prints 18 and one page description prints 19. The three co-op rooms are Lightning Gale, Blazing Wings and Courageous Wit, and they hold those fourteen chests between them.这里有一个数字有分歧，我们印支持更充分的那个。两份互相独立的清单都写 17 —— 十四个单人加三个双人 —— 另有一个第三方资料库写 18，一处页面描述写 19。三个双人房间是 Lightning Gale、Blazing Wings 和 Courageous Wit，那十四个箱子就在它们里面。

After the fight: catching the thing you just killed打完之后：怎么把刚打完的那只收走

This is the part that catches people out, and it is different for the two kinds of world boss.这是最容易踩坑的一段，而且两种世界 BOSS 的规则不一样。

  • An Alpha cannot be caught standing, and barely after. The catch rate is 0% while it is up, and 6% once it is down — so the throw is a coin-flip you lose most of the time, and the reward you are actually guaranteed is the material.Alpha 站着抓不到，倒下后也几乎抓不到。 它血还在的时候捕捉率是 0%，倒下之后是 6% —— 所以那一掷是大多数时候都会输的硬币，真正保底的奖励是材料。
