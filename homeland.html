Homeland家园

The base on the RV you unlock at level 20: what it produces, who to station in it, how it levels up, and the two versions of how it unlocks.20 级解锁的那辆房车基地：它产什么、该派谁去、怎么升级，以及两个版本的解锁路线。

What the Homeland is家园是什么

The Homeland is the base that comes with the RV, and it opens at level 20. It is a production system rather than a decorating one: everything the game crafts is made here, Aniipods included, and it keeps producing while you are logged off.家园是随房车一起给你的基地，20 级开放。它是一套生产系统而不是装修玩法：游戏里所有要造的东西都在这里造，Aniipod 也是，而且你下线之后它照样在产。

Stationing Aniimo派驻 Aniimo

Aniimo do the work. Each one has Homeland abilities, and the ones you station cannot come with you — an Aniimo working a facility cannot be in your battle team or in the open world at the same time. Want a species in both places and you need a second copy of it.干活的是 Aniimo。它们各有家园能力，而派驻的不能带出门 —— 在设施上上班的 Aniimo 不能同时进你的战斗队，也不能在大世界里跟着你。想同一只两边都用，你得有第二只。

The nine element jobs九个元素分工

  • Fire — cooking and smelting火 —— 烹饪与熔炼
  • Grass — farming草 —— 耕作
  • Water — brewing and watering水 —— 酿造与浇水
  • Rock — tilling and mining土 —— 翻土与采矿
  • Electric — power电 —— 供电
  • Ice — cooling冰 —— 制冷
  • Wind — processing风 —— 加工
  • Dark — harvesting, chopping and curing暗 —— 采收、伐木与腌制
  • Holy — lighting光 —— 照明
  • Not every job is an element. Hauling — moving goods between collection and storage — is the non-elemental ability the sources we read directly name, and it is not the only one.岗位并不全是元素能力。搬运（Hauling） —— 在收取点和仓库之间搬东西 —— 是我们读到的来源里直接点名的非元素能力，而且它不是唯一一个。

Where the extra output comes from多出来的产量从哪来

  • Element level. If a worker's element level is one step above what the facility asks for, its work efficiency goes up 20%.元素等级。 工人的元素等级比设施要求的高一级，工作效率 +20%。
  • Personality. Every Aniimo hatches with four MBTI-style traits, and each trait matches certain facilities. A worker in a matching facility gets another 20% — so one Aniimo can collect up to four of these bonuses at once.人格。 每只 Aniimo 孵出来时带四条 MBTI 式人格，每条对应一类设施。工人待在匹配的设施里再拿 20% —— 所以一只身上可以叠到四条这种加成。
  • Fixed facilities. A few facilities only take one specific Aniimo; the Dewy House and the Tidewhisper Sandcastle are the two named. The evolved stage works faster than the base one — a Fragrancier in the Dewy House produces Aromathyst faster than a Dewy does.指定设施。 少数设施只收特定 Aniimo，来源里点名的是露屋（Dewy House）和低语沙堡（Tidewhisper Sandcastle）。进化后的形态比基础形态快 —— Fragrancier 在露屋里产芳晶就比 Dewy 快。

Take both into account when you assign: the element the facility asks for, and whether the worker's traits match it. The base's ability distribution screen — the exclamation mark beside it — lists the elements it currently needs, which is faster than working it out facility by facility.派人时两件事一起看：设施要的属性，以及工人的人格对不对得上。基地里那个能力分布面板（旁边有个感叹号的那个）会直接列出它当前缺哪些元素，比一个个设施去对要快。

Keeping them working让它们一直干活

Workers eat, get sick, and stop.工人会饿、会生病、会撂挑子。

  • Total Food Energy is the base-wide food pool. Keep it topped up: below 100% efficiency everything takes longer, crops included.Food Energy 总量是整个基地的食物池。保持满格：掉到 100% 以下效率就降，所有工序都变慢，种地也算。
  • Wheatmeal is the cheap answer. Grinding Wheat makes it, Wheat is free to grow, and one Wheatmeal is worth 9,370 food energy against the 1,000 a purchasable Energy Bite gives.小麦糊（Wheatmeal）是最省的做法。 磨小麦就有，小麦是免费种的，一份小麦糊顶 9,370 食物能量，而商店买的 Energy Bite 只有 1,000。
  • A sick Aniimo stops working and has to be given medicine in person, which means going back to the base to do it.生病的 Aniimo 直接停工，而且得本人回去喂药 —— 也就是说你必须回一趟基地。
  • Visit once a day. Workers can slack off, get sick or get injured if you are away too long, and the Warehouse needs restocking.每天回来一次。 离开太久工人会偷懒、生病或者受伤，仓库也需要补货。
  • Buy seeds before you log off. A facility keeps running while you are away as long as it has its inputs, and you collect the finished goods when you next log in.下线前把种子囤够。 只要输入材料够，设施在你离线时继续运转，下次上线直接收成品。

The last line is the one worth acting on. A base that ran out of seeds overnight produced nothing, and no amount of levelling fixes a night of that.最后一条是真正要动手的。一晚上没种子的基地等于什么都没产，而且这不是升级能补回来的 —— 那一夜就是没了。

Orders and Home Coins订单与家园币

Orders are the base's income.订单是基地的收入来源。

  • Daily Orders are the main source of Home Coins, the currency the base itself runs on. Home Coins buy plots and facility upgrades; selling Home materials at the Co-op Store is the other way to get them.日常订单是家园币的主要来源，而家园币就是基地自己运转用的货币。家园币用来买地块和设施升级；把家园材料卖到 Co-op Store 是另一条拿法。
  • They refresh twice a day, at 4:00 AM and 4:00 PM server time.每天刷新两次，服务器时间 4:00 和 16:00。
  • How many you get depends on your RV level, and later levels raise the cap.能拿几个取决于房车等级，后面几级会提高上限。
  • Reroll what you cannot make. An order for something you are not producing is dead weight; refreshing for something you can produce is the better move.做不了的就刷掉。 一条你现在根本不产的订单就是废纸；换成能产的才是正解。
  • Refreshing unlocks at RV level 4 (see the table above).刷新在房车 4 级解锁（见上表）。

Keep the base staffed and producing even when you are not chasing a level. The orders are the reason to fill the element roles rather than treat the base as decoration.就算你这一阵不冲等级，也让基地一直有人干活、一直产。订单正是「把属性岗位填满」的理由，别把基地当成装修。

Getting to the next RV level怎么推到下一级房车

What actually blocks an upgrade.卡住升级的到底是什么。

  • Woodland and Mine byproducts, not Home Coins. The materials an RV upgrade wants are a byproduct of harvesting trees and mining, and early on they are the limiting resource.卡的是林地与矿场的副产物，不是家园币。 房车升级要的材料是伐树和挖矿的副产物，前期它就是那个瓶颈资源。
  • Run the highest-level trees and ore you have. Higher-level plots return more of the byproduct the upgrade is asking for.跑你手上最高级的树和矿。 等级越高的地块，副产物出得越多。
  • Unlock plots. A new plot comes with debris you and your Aniimo clear for Wood Blocks and Mineral Sand — the two upgrade materials — plus the space itself.开新地块。 每块新地带着碎石，你和 Aniimo 清掉它换来 Wood Block 和 Mineral Sand —— 正好是那两样升级材料 —— 外加这块地本身。
  • Plant Growth Tonic and Energizing Work Meal finish a job instantly. Useful both for an order deadline and for running Woodlands and Mines purely for byproducts.Plant Growth Tonic 和 Energizing Work Meal 能立刻完工。 既救订单的截止时间，也用来纯刷副产物。
  • Modification Toolkits unlock recipes. Premium recipes make an item worth more than the plain version; Quick recipes make Farmlands, Woodlands, Mines and the Well yield more.Modification Toolkit 解锁配方。 Premium 配方做出的东西比普通版值钱；Quick 配方让农田、林地、矿场和水井产得更多。
  • Index Score is where the late toolkits come from. Spending Bud Tickets on furniture raises it, and it pays out more Dream Sparks and Modification Toolkits. Furnishing is not only cosmetic.后期工具包靠 Index Score。 用 Bud Ticket 买家具就能抬它，而它给更多 Dream Spark 和 Modification Toolkit。摆家具不只是好看。

Toolkits get rarer the higher your RV goes, so the Index stops being optional — later on it is the supply.房车等级越高，工具包越稀，所以 Index 这块迟早绕不开 —— 到后面它就是你的进货渠道。

Layout: three temperatures from two buildings布局：两栋建筑，三种温度

Once you have the Heat Furnace and the Cooling Unit, crops and trees start asking for a temperature: Scorching, Warm, Cool or Freezing.有了加热炉（Heat Furnace）和制冷机（Cooling Unit）之后，作物和树就开始挑温度了：极热、温暖、凉爽、严寒。

  • Overlap two zones and you get a third. A plot inside a Scorching area and a Cool area at the same time reads as Warm, so two buildings cover three growing conditions.两个区重叠就出第三种。 一块地同时落在极热区和凉爽区里，读作 温暖，于是两栋建筑管住三种生长条件。
  • The two extremes overlap into neutral. Freezing plus Scorching gives a temperature-neutral zone, not a hotter or a colder one.两个极端叠出来的是中性。 严寒加极热得到的是温度中性区，不会更热也不会更冷。
  • A plot only has to be touched. Any part of a plot inside a temperature zone carries that temperature for the whole plot, so the placement does not have to be precise.碰到一点点就算。 地块只要有任意一角落在温度区里，整块地就按这个温度算，所以位置不用摆得很精准。

Getting this wrong does not kill the crop. It grows slower, which is the same thing as losing the time.摆错了不会把作物弄死。它只是长得慢 —— 而长得慢和浪费掉那些时间是同一件事。
