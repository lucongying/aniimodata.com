Tuckin埋埋

Grass草 Break击破

Tuckin has 2 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.埋埋 共有 2 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Tuckin is the BREAK ending of the Hummin line and the species that actually commands the Hummins: Gravel Strike teleports every one of them onto a target, Draw Nourishment immediately restores 50% HP to each of them while rooting itself in place, and Breaking Roar refills their number to maximum before it roars. Three of its six cards exist only because the Hummins do, so on this species the summon management is the kit.埋埋是走调草那条线偏向击破的终点，也是真正在指挥走调草的那一只：砂石突袭把它们全部瞬移到目标身上，汲取养分在扎根的同时瞬间为每一只回复 50% 生命，而破音怒吼会先把数量补满、再吼出去。六张卡里有三张是因为走调草才存在的 —— 所以在这个物种身上，召唤物的管理就是它的技能组本身。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Prank Master恶搞大王

Two effects on one card. Every 1,000 max HP adds 2% BREAK damage — the same scaling NO.020's Energized carries. And on entering battle it summons one Hummin to fight alongside, then another every 10 seconds, to a maximum of 3. ⚠️ The official English card is broken here: it reads "BREAK damage by % for every 21,000 max HP" and drops the summoning altogether. The figures above are the Chinese card's, which is the coherent one.一张卡上有两个效果。每 1000 点最大生命值提高 2% 击破伤害 —— 和 NO.020【充满能量】用的是同一套成长。另外，登场时会召唤 1 只走调草参战，此后每 10 秒再召唤 1 只，最多同时存在 3 只。⚠️ 官方英文卡这一条是坏的：写成「BREAK damage by % for every 21,000 max HP」，而且把召唤那半整个漏掉了。上面的数字取自中文卡，中文卡是通顺的那一边。

Fifty percent at once is an enormous heal — but it only touches Hummins, so what this card is worth is set entirely by how many exist, and this kit carries another card whose whole job is refilling that number.牢牢扎入土壤，自身和走调草获得 +20% 击破效率，瞬间为所有走调草回复 50% 生命值，随后自身和附近走调草每秒回复 2% 生命值。瞬间 50% 是极大的治疗量 —— 但它只落在走调草身上，所以这张卡值多少，完全由「存在几只」决定；而这套技能组里还有另一张卡，专门负责把那个数量补回来。
