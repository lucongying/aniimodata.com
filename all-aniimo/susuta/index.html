Susuta羞羞獭

Water水 Break击破

Susuta has 2 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.羞羞獭 共有 2 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Susuta is a three-card water BREAK species: a 65-power cannon, a 74-power dash, and the basic attack. Its line forks at the second stage rather than the first — Popota is the one that splits into two stage-3 forms — so the question of which ending you are heading for arrives later here than it does almost anywhere else in the index.羞羞獭是一个三张卡的水系击破物种：一发 65 威力的水炮、一次 74 威力的冲撞，加上基础攻击。它这条线的分叉在第二阶而不是第一阶 —— 是泡泡獭这一个点分成两个三阶形态 —— 所以「你到底要往哪边练」这个问题，在图鉴里几乎没有任何地方比这里来得更晚。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Appeal出风头

On entering battle, if there is an opposite-sex Susuta family member on the team, it turns pink and gains +30% BREAK efficiency for 15 seconds, on a 20-second cooldown. The team requirement is the entire gate — without a family member of the right sex, the trait does nothing at all.自己登场时，如果队伍里有不同性别的羞羞獭家族成员，它会变成粉色并提高 30% 击破效率，持续 15 秒，冷却 20 秒。队伍条件是唯一的门槛 —— 没有符合条件的家族成员，这个特性等于不存在。
