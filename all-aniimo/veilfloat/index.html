Veilfloat灯纱水母

Electric电 Support辅助

Veilfloat has a single form recorded so far. Its stats, type, habitat and evolution requirement are below.灯纱水母 目前只记录到一个形态。它的数值、属性、栖息地和进化条件都在下面。

Overview简介

Veilfloat carries the same three cards as Bulbly, so the second stage is a stat step again — and it sits in the middle of a three-stage line whose last stage is where everything distinctive lands. This is a family where very little changes until the end, and then rather a lot does.灯纱水母带的卡和灯泡水母一样，所以第二阶又是一级数值台阶 —— 而它处在一条三阶线的中段，所有特别的东西都落在最后一阶。这是一个「前面几乎什么都不变、到最后突然变很多」的家族。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Glow发光照明

Light comes off it from above and lights the area it is standing in. Unlike Luminous Seed this one travels with you, so it is the one for still moving through somewhere dark.光从头顶散出来，照亮它所在的那一片。和照明种子不同，这个光是跟着你走的 —— 还在摸黑赶路的时候用它。

Trait特性

Arc Surge弧光瞬爆

A teammate who receives one of its skill buffs also gains 12% Lightning damage for 20 seconds. (The official English card prints "12%%" — a stray second percent sign. The Chinese card says 12 as well, so the figure is not in doubt.)拿到它技能增益的队友，额外获得 12% 电元素强化，持续 20 秒。（官方英文卡写成「12%%」，多了一个百分号。中文卡也是 12，所以数字本身没有疑问。）
