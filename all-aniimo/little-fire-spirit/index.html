Little Fire Spirit小火人

Fire火 Support辅助

Little Fire Spirit has 2 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.小火人 共有 2 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Little Fire Spirit is one of the five specially-numbered creatures, so it sits outside the evolution tree entirely. Its kit is the most team-facing in the index: Inspiration and Treat Feed are support cards, Leaping Sparks makes a target take 100% more damage for as long as it is shielded, and Keep the Fire Bolt going together! gives nearby allies +25% fire damage for 30 seconds.小火人是五个特殊编号物种之一，所以它完全在进化树之外。它的技能组是图鉴里最面向队友的一套：鼓舞和投喂点心都是辅助卡，跃动火花让目标在护盾期间额外受到 100% 伤害，而 Keep the Fire Bolt going together! 给附近友方 +25% 火元素强化，持续 30 秒。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Reliable Fire Companion靠谱火伴

In a party it gives everyone +15% Fire Elemental Boost, rising to 20% while its own energy sits above 40. The condition is on its own meter rather than on anything the team does, so keeping this one charged is the whole of the job.在队伍里给所有人 +15% 火元素强化，自身能量高于 40 时提高到 20%。条件看的是它自己的能量条，不是队伍做了什么 —— 所以「让它保持有能量」就是它全部的工作。

    A heal worth 8% of its own HP to one ally, rising to 12% if that ally is below 50% HP and 16% below 25%. It therefore scales in the opposite direction from most healing: the worse the target's situation, the more this gives — which makes it a card you hold rather than spend early.掏出食物治疗一名队友，回复量为自身生命值的 8%；如果该单位生命值低于 50% 则改成 12%，低于 25% 改成 16%。所以它的缩放方向和大多数治疗相反：目标情况越糟，它给的越多 —— 也就意味着这是一张该留着的卡，不是早早花掉的卡。
