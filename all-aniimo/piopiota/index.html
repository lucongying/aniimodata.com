Piopiota漂漂獭

Water水 Support辅助

Piopiota has 2 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.漂漂獭 共有 2 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Piopiota is the support ending of the Popota fork: a Dark Tide that gives 15% BREAK damage reduction and gains a stack for every puddle absorbed, up to 4 at once, a Sea in Wrath that gives teammates +20% water damage for 30 seconds, and a 100-power free finisher. Its own cannon costs half what its predecessors' did — the clearest sign that this is the casting-heavy end of the line.漂漂獭是泡泡獭分叉里偏辅助的那一端：一发提供 15% 击破伤害减免、每吸一个水滩多叠一层、一次最多吸 4 个的暗潮；一发给队友 +20% 水元素强化、持续 30 秒的怒海；以及一发不耗能量的 100 威力收尾。它自己的水炮只要前两阶的一半能量 —— 这是「它是这条线上偏持续释放的那一端」最清楚的信号。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Radiant Charm魅力四射

Raises Water damage by 16% on every opposite-sex Aniimo in the party. A team of one sex gets nothing from it, so this is a reason to build the party around it rather than a bonus that tags along by itself.队伍里所有不同性别的伊莫水元素伤害提高 16%。全队同性时它一点用都没有 —— 所以这是「围绕它组队」的理由，不是顺手白拿的加成。

Skills技能

Split the way the official page splits them: Combat holds the skills you cast, Innate the basic attack. The list is the species', not the form's — a form does not change it.分法和官方页面一致：战斗技能是你会主动放的那几个，固有技能里是基础攻击。这份表属于物种，不属于形态 —— 换形态不会换掉它。
