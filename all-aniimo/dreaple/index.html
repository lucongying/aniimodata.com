Dreaple眠眠羊

Dark暗 Support辅助

Dreaple has a single form recorded so far. Its stats, type, habitat and evolution requirement are below.眠眠羊 目前只记录到一个形态。它的数值、属性、栖息地和进化条件都在下面。

Overview简介

Dreaple is the dark ending of the Nimbi line and the one that becomes a sleep specialist: Nimbi Attack sends a summoned Nimbi through the enemy line applying Drowsiness, Hypnotic Cloud Shot and Nightmare Devourer work the same theme, and an 84-power Dream Blast finishes for free. It drops the wind element its predecessors had entirely, so this stage is a change of plan rather than an upgrade.眠眠羊是云朵羊这条线的暗系终点，也是变成「睡眠专精」的那一只：Nimbi Attack 让召唤出的小羊穿过敌人、叠加【睡意】，催眠云射和噬梦者走的是同一个主题，最后一发 84 威力梦境爆破不耗能量。它把前两阶的风属性整个丢掉 —— 所以这一阶与其说是升级，不如说是换了思路。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Counting Sheep数羊时刻

Every 10 seconds the next skill adds a stack of Drowsiness; at 3 stacks the target drops into Nightmare for 20 seconds. Inside it they lose 27% Dark Resistance and are held — unable to move or cast — for 3 seconds, though any damage breaks the hold. So the lock is short and fragile while the resistance shred runs the full 20: the control is the setup, the 27% is the payload.每 10 秒，下一次技能给目标叠 1 层【睡意】；叠到 3 层时目标进入【噩梦】，持续 20 秒。噩梦期间暗元素抗性降低 27%，并且 3 秒不能移动、不能放技能 —— 但只要受到伤害就解除。所以控制又短又脆，真正持续 20 秒的是那 27% 减抗：控制是起手，减抗才是收益。
