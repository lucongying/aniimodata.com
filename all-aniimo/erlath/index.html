Erlath沐羽鸟

Water水 Regen回能

Erlath has a single form recorded so far. Its stats, type, habitat and evolution requirement are below.沐羽鸟 目前只记录到一个形态。它的数值、属性、栖息地和进化条件都在下面。

Overview简介

Erlath is where the bubbles turn into an energy engine: Bubbles builds a field that spawns one bubble for every water skill the team casts inside it, up to six, and a second press detonates them all for 4 energy each — 10 more per bubble if a puddle has been absorbed. It is filed as a regenerator whose actual output is energy, which makes it a support that wants teammates spending water skills rather than one that wants a target.沐羽鸟就是泡泡变成能量机的地方：泡泡会生成一个领域，队友在领域内每放一次水元素技能就冒出一个泡泡，最多六个；再按一次则把泡泡全部引爆，每个 4 点能量 —— 吸过水滩的话每个再多 10 点。它的定位写着回复，实际产出的是能量 —— 所以它是一只需要队友不停消耗水系技能的辅助，而不是一个需要目标的辅助。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Floaty Bubble浮力泡泡

Scrubs itself down until a large bubble forms around it. The card describes the bubble and stops; nothing here says it protects, lifts or carries, so read it as a way to be inside a bubble rather than as armour.把自己搓了一遍，搓出一个裹住全身的大泡泡。卡上只描述了泡泡本身 —— 没说能防、能浮、能载 —— 所以它是「让你待在一个泡泡里」的办法，不是护甲。

Trait特性

Bubble泡沫

Every 12 seconds it enters Bathing, and the next skill it casts leaves a puddle on the ground. The card stops at the puddle and says nothing about what standing in one does, so what is recorded here is that the puddle appears — not that it is worth standing in.每 12 秒进入沐浴状态，下一次释放的技能会在原地留下一个水滩。卡写到水滩为止，没说站在里面会发生什么 —— 所以这里记录的只是「水滩会出现」，不是「站进去有用」。

    A field where every water skill a teammate casts inside spawns a bubble, up to 6, lasting 15 seconds. Press again to detonate all of them, each worth 4 energy — and 10 more each if a puddle has been absorbed. So it is an engine that runs on the team's water spam, and the second press is a real decision: cash the bubbles in now, or keep the field open for more.生成一个泡泡领域，队友在领域内每放一次水元素技能就生成 1 个泡泡，最多 6 个，持续 15 秒。再次按键引爆全部泡泡，每个回 4 点能量 —— 而如果吸收了水滩，每个再多回 10 点。所以它是一台靠全队刷水系技能运转的能量机，而第二次按键是一个真正的决策：现在就兑现，还是让领域继续开着攒更多。

    A bubble that damages and pushes targets back, leaving a small bubble behind for 9 seconds — and up to 3 charges can be stored, so it can be fired three times in quick succession. The stored charges are the part to plan around: three bubbles back to back shove something a long way, which is a different move from three bubbles spread over a fight.射出一个泡泡，造成伤害并击退目标，同时在原地留下一个持续 9 秒的小泡泡 —— 而且最多可以储存 3 次释放次数，能连着放三发。真正要计划的是那个储存：三发连着放能把目标推出去很远，而这和三发分散在整场战斗里用，完全是两回事。
