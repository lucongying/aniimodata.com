No. 017

Nimbi云朵羊

Wind风 Support辅助 B

Nimbi has 4 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.云朵羊 共有 4 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Nimbi is a four-card support: a wind orb, a lightning bolt, a Cloud Shield and the basic attack, with two of the four costing only 10 energy — so it is cheap to run and never short of something to press. Its name is also the word this family uses for its summons: the Nimbi Attack and Nimbi Burst on its own relatives both call up a thing called a Nimbi.云朵羊是一个四张卡的辅助：一个风弹、一道闪电、一面云盾，加上基础攻击；其中两张只要 10 点能量 —— 所以它便宜、永远有东西可按。它这个名字也是这一族给召唤物的称呼：它自己亲戚身上的 Nimbi Attack 和 Nimbi Burst，召唤出来的那东西就叫 Nimbi。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Puff Up膨胀

Inflates, which buys a higher jump and one mid-air dash. The dash is the useful half: it is a second decision you get to make after you have already left the ground.膨胀起来，换来更高的跳跃和一次空中冲刺。有用的是冲刺那一半 —— 那是你离地之后还能再做的一次决定。

Trait特性

Shrouded in Mist云雾缭绕

Progress builds to 4, and then it enters Shrouded in Mist; the next skill you cast spends the state to make that skill stronger. ⚠️ The two official cards disagree about how progress arrives — the English counts 4 basic attacks, the Chinese also counts 5 seconds spent off the field. The payoff is the same either way, and either way you lose the state the moment you cast.进度叠到 4 层后进入【云雾缭绕】，接着释放的那个技能会消耗掉这个状态、并得到强化。⚠️ 两张官方卡在「进度怎么来」上说法不同：英文只算 4 次普攻，中文还额外算上未登场满 5 秒。两种情况收益一样，而且都是一放技能就没了。

Skills技能

Split the way the official page splits them: Combat holds the skills you cast, Innate the basic attack. The list is the species', not the form's — a form does not change it.分法和官方页面一致：战斗技能是你会主动放的那几个，固有技能里是基础攻击。这份表属于物种，不属于形态 —— 换形态不会换掉它。
