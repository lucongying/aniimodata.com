Homeland家园

The base on the RV you unlock at level 20: what it produces, who to station in it, and how it levels up.20 级解锁的那辆房车基地：它产什么、该派谁去、怎么升级。

What the Homeland is家园是什么

The Homeland is the base that comes with the RV, and it opens at level 20. It is a production system rather than a decorating one: everything the game crafts is made here, Aniipods included, and it keeps producing while you are logged off.家园是随房车一起给你的基地，20 级开放。它是一套生产系统而不是装修玩法：游戏里所有要造的东西都在这里造，Aniipod 也是，而且你下线之后它照样在产。

A home code is the eight-character string attached to a shared build; open the Build Menu's Shared Designs screen and paste it into the combo-code box. A code only works on the server it was shared on, and the player who shared it can withdraw it at any time. In the code columns an em dash means no code has been collected for that level yet — not that no layout exists.家园码是分享出来的布置所带的八位字符码 —— 在家园里打开「家园设计」，进「共享设计」点「导入组合」，把码粘进输入框。它只在分享它的那个服务器有效；分享者撤掉之后，码也就失效了。码栏里的破折号表示这一级还没收到码，不是这一级没有布置。

Power, from RV 12电力：从房车 12 级开始

The Power Module is the first thing in the base that costs something to keep running. What it charges follows the level of the building, not how many buildings there are — a Lv.3 Mine draws three times what a Lv.1 does — so a grid worked out by counting facilities runs short the moment you upgrade one.电力模块是基地里第一样要花钱维持运转的东西。它按建筑等级收费，不按建筑数量 —— 一座 3 级矿场是用电量的三倍，1 级只是一倍 —— 所以只数设施个数搭出来的电网，升一级就不够用了。

Facility设施 Draw用电
Mine, Well, and the two machines that make Aniipods and energy矿山、水井、谜立方制造机、能量机 30 W per level每级 30 W
Every other processing facility其他所有加工设施 15 W per level每级 15 W
  • A generator is 800 W, and the curve breaks at 500. Everything supplied while the load is at or under 500 W runs at 120%; past that the rate slides with the shortfall, so the last building you connect is the slowest thing in the base. Pick the buildings worth powering first and leave the rest on Aniimo.一台发电机 800 W，效率在 500 W 处拐弯。 负载在 500 W 及以下时，接上电网的东西全部按 120% 跑；超过之后效率跟着缺口往下掉，所以最后接上的那一座，就是全基地最慢的。先挑你最在意的几座接电，剩下的继续靠 Aniimo。
  • A selected formula draws power even when it cannot run. An empty bench with a recipe still queued is a load producing nothing. Clear the idle formulas before you log off.只要选了配方就耗电，哪怕它转不动。 空着材料还挂着配方的加工台，是在耗电却不产出。下线前把闲置配方清掉。
  • A powered building is also a connection point. The grid chains through whatever is already live, so a run of benches needs one post rather than a wire each.通电的建筑本身就是接线点。 电网会顺着已经通电的建筑往下接，所以一排加工台只要一个接点，不必每台拉一根线。
  • The ability has to keep pace with the generator. Power opens at Lv.1 with the module at RV 12. RV 14 brings the Lv.2 generator and asks for a Lv.2 Power ability to run it; RV 16 does the same at Lv.3. A generator one level ahead of the ability is not a bigger grid, it is a grid that does not come on.电力能力要跟着发电机一起升。 房车 12 级开电力，能力 1 级；14 级出 2 级发电机，电力能力也得是 2 级才带得动；16 级同理，要 3 级。发电机比能力高一级，不是电网更大，是电网根本开不起来。
  • What it is really buying is space. Folding the processing line into the grid frees five or six cells, and it keeps the dark-ability Aniimo out of the crafting queue: that worker both harvests and cures, so a bench it has to run is a harvest it is not doing. A powered line that runs a little slower is a fair trade for that.它真正买的是格子。 把加工线并进电网，能省出五六格；而且暗属性伊莫不会再卡在制作上 —— 采收和腌制本来就是它，被叫去开加工台，田里就少一个人。加工慢一点，换这个很划算。

There is a reason to keep the grid small beyond the efficiency figure: every watt spent on a bench is a watt the Mine does not get, and the Mine is what produces the materials the next RV level asks for.把电网做小还有一个理由：花在加工台上的每一瓦，都是矿场拿不到的一瓦，而下一级房车要的材料，正是矿场产出来的。

Stationing Aniimo派驻 Aniimo

Aniimo do the work. Each one has Homeland abilities, and the ones you station cannot come with you — an Aniimo working a facility cannot be in your battle team or in the open world at the same time. Want a species in both places and you need a second copy of it.干活的是 Aniimo。它们各有家园能力，而派驻的不能带出门 —— 在设施上上班的 Aniimo 不能同时进你的战斗队，也不能在大世界里跟着你。想同一只两边都用，你得有第二只。

Where the extra output comes from多出来的产量从哪来

  • Element level. If a worker's element level is one step above what the facility asks for, its work efficiency goes up 20%.元素等级。 工人的元素等级比设施要求的高一级，工作效率 +20%。
  • Personality. Every Aniimo hatches with four MBTI-style traits, and each trait matches certain facilities. A worker in a matching facility gets another 20% — so one Aniimo can collect up to four of these bonuses at once.人格。 每只 Aniimo 孵出来时带四条 MBTI 式人格，每条对应一类设施。工人待在匹配的设施里再拿 20% —— 所以一只身上可以叠到四条这种加成。
  • Fixed facilities. A few facilities only take one specific Aniimo; the Dewy House and the Tidewhisper Sandcastle are the two named. The evolved stage works faster than the base one — a Fragrancier in the Dewy House produces Aromathyst faster than a Dewy does.指定设施。 少数设施只收特定 Aniimo，来源里点名的是露屋（Dewy House）和低语沙堡（Tidewhisper Sandcastle）。进化后的形态比基础形态快 —— Fragrancier 在露屋里产芳晶就比 Dewy 快。

The order the base hands work out基地派活的顺序

Work is not claimed by the most capable Aniimo first. The base sends the lowest matching ability, and a higher one takes the job off it partway there: a Lv.1 sower sets off, a Lv.3 arrives and claims the plot, the Lv.1 turns around. The job gets done — the two walks are what it cost.派活不是谁能力高谁先上。基地会先派等级最低的那只，走到一半又被高等级的抢走：1 级播种出发，3 级到了把地块接过去，1 级再原路走回来。活是干完了 —— 代价是白走的两趟。

  • Keep the short jobs on one level. Sowing, watering, tilling and harvesting finish in seconds, so a level gap there buys less than the walking it causes. All Lv.2 or all Lv.3 both work; a mix is the arrangement that costs you.快工种保持同一等级。 播种、浇水、开垦、收割都是几秒钟的事，在那里留一个等级差，赚的还没有多走的那些路多。全 2 级或全 3 级都行，混着最亏。
  • Place the fixed posts yourself. The Mine, the Well, the temperature pair and the leisure buildings are the ones the automatic distribution gets wrong often enough that moving those Aniimo onto them by hand is the habit worth keeping. Leave the fields to sort themselves out.固定岗位自己摆。 矿场、水井、温度组（加热炉和制冷机）以及游乐建筑，是自动分配经常摆错的那几座；把这几个位置的伊莫手动挪过去，是值得养成的习惯。田那边交给它自己排就行。

Take both into account when you assign: the element the facility asks for, and whether the worker's traits match it. The base's ability distribution screen — the exclamation mark beside it — lists the elements it currently needs, which is faster than working it out facility by facility.派人时两件事一起看：设施要的属性，以及工人的人格对不对得上。基地里那个能力分布面板（旁边有个感叹号的那个）会直接列出它当前缺哪些元素，比一个个设施去对要快。

Which Aniimo to station, and the 400,000-a-night layout该派哪几只打工，以及一夜四十万的布局

Keeping them working让它们一直干活

Workers eat, get sick, and stop.工人会饿、会生病、会撂挑子。

  • Total Food Energy is the base-wide food pool. Keep it topped up: below 100% efficiency everything takes longer, crops included.Food Energy 总量是整个基地的食物池。保持满格：掉到 100% 以下效率就降，所有工序都变慢，种地也算。
  • Wheatmeal is the cheap answer. Grinding Wheat makes it, Wheat is free to grow, and one Wheatmeal is worth 9,370 food energy against the 1,000 a purchasable Energy Bite gives.小麦糊（Wheatmeal）是最省的做法。 磨小麦就有，小麦是免费种的，一份小麦糊顶 9,370 食物能量，而商店买的 Energy Bite 只有 1,000。
  • A sick Aniimo stops working and has to be given medicine in person, which means going back to the base to do it.生病的 Aniimo 直接停工，而且得本人回去喂药 —— 也就是说你必须回一趟基地。
  • Visit once a day. Workers can slack off, get sick or get injured if you are away too long, and the Warehouse needs restocking.每天回来一次。 离开太久工人会偷懒、生病或者受伤，仓库也需要补货。
  • Buy seeds before you log off — the night is not free. The base keeps running while you are away, but it falls into a slower idle state; it only runs at its normal rate with someone in the base. A full input buffer is still what makes the night worth anything, but it gets less done than the same hours would with you there.下线前把种子囤够 —— 这一夜不是白赚的。 基地在你离线时照样运转，不过会掉进一种更慢的怠惰状态：基地里得有人，它才按正常速度跑。把输入材料填满仍然是这一夜唯一有价值的地方，只是同样几小时，产出的比你在场时少。

The last line is the one worth acting on. A base that ran out of seeds overnight produced nothing, and no amount of levelling fixes a night of that.最后一条是真正要动手的。一晚上没种子的基地等于什么都没产，而且这不是升级能补回来的 —— 那一夜就是没了。

What to feed them喂什么

The bowl takes cooked dishes, not the raw ingredients they come from. Each batch fills the pool by a fixed amount, which is what the table compares — a batch, not a rate. These are the five that matter, highest first, and every one of them but the soybean is a Quick formula — so the ceiling is set by your Ecological Module as much as by the kitchen.池子里填的是做好的菜，不是它们的原料。做一份就往池子里填固定的一笔能量 —— 这张表比的是一份，不是每分钟。下面五样是排得上的五样，从高到低；除烤大豆之外全是高速配方，所以天花板一半由生态模块决定，一半由厨房决定。

Orders and Home Coins订单与家园币

Orders are the base's income.订单是基地的收入来源。

  • Daily Orders are the main source of Home Coins, the currency the base itself runs on. Home Coins buy plots and facility upgrades; selling Home materials at the Co-op Store is the other way to get them.日常订单是家园币的主要来源，而家园币就是基地自己运转用的货币。家园币用来买地块和设施升级；把家园材料卖到 Co-op Store 是另一条拿法。
  • They refresh twice a day, at 4:00 AM and 4:00 PM server time.每天刷新两次，服务器时间 4:00 和 16:00。
  • How many you get depends on your RV level, and later levels raise the cap.能拿几个取决于房车等级，后面几级会提高上限。
  • Reroll what you cannot make. An order for something you are not producing is dead weight; refreshing for something you can produce is the better move.做不了的就刷掉。 一条你现在根本不产的订单就是废纸；换成能产的才是正解。
  • Refreshing unlocks at RV level 4 (see the table above).刷新在房车 4 级解锁（见上表）。
  • Keep slots free. Orders stack rather than expire, and an RV upgrade adds more of them, so a full board is a board with no room on it. Empty it, or leave at least five slots open before you upgrade.留着空位。 订单会累积、不会过期，而每升一级房车还会再加几条 —— 板子满了就是没地方接新单。要么清空，要么升级前至少留五格。
  • Reroll toward wood. If you are going to spend a refresh, spend it on the reward the ladder actually eats: an order paying wood is worth more than one paying a currency you are not short of. And a normal order that is going to survive the reset is better left alone than thrown away.刷新就洗木头。 真要花一次刷新，就花在房车升级真正吃的那个奖励上：一条付木头的订单，比一条付你根本不缺的货币的订单值钱。而刷新后本来就留得住的普通单，留着比洗掉好。

Keep the base staffed and producing even when you are not chasing a level. The orders are the reason to fill the element roles rather than treat the base as decoration.就算你这一阵不冲等级，也让基地一直有人干活、一直产。订单正是「把属性岗位填满」的理由，别把基地当成装修。

The daily five minutes每天的五分钟

A few things on the base pay out once a day and none of them is on a screen you would open on your way past.基地上有几件事一天只结一次，而且没有一件摆在你顺路会点开的界面上。

  • Pet the ones that ask. An Aniimo will occasionally want attention, and a pet pays a Wish Star. Five a day, and it costs nothing but the walk.该摸的就摸。 伊莫偶尔会想被摸一下，摸一次给一颗许愿星 —— 一天五次，成本就是走过去那两步。
  • Loosen soil for other players. Five plots at any one base, thirty a day in total. At fifteen you come home to a crate of speed-up items waiting at your own base, which is the part worth doing the other fifteen for.去帮别人松土。 任何一处基地一次 5 块，一天合计 30 次。松满 15 次，回自己基地会看到一个装着加速道具的箱子 —— 后面那 15 次就是为它松的。
  • Answer the strike. Walk into the base and an Aniimo may have stopped working over something. Clicking the notice moves you to it and F soothes it; leave it standing there and the facility stays down until you do.处理罢工。 走进基地时，可能有伊莫因为什么事停手了。点那条提示会直接把你带过去，按 F 安抚；不管它，那座设施就会一直停到你管为止。

Getting to the next RV level怎么推到下一级房车

What actually blocks an upgrade.卡住升级的到底是什么。

  • Woodland and Mine byproducts, not Home Coins. The materials an RV upgrade wants are a byproduct of harvesting trees and mining, and early on they are the limiting resource.卡的是林地与矿场的副产物，不是家园币。 房车升级要的材料是伐树和挖矿的副产物，前期它就是那个瓶颈资源。
  • Run the highest-level trees and ore you have. Higher-level plots return more of the byproduct the upgrade is asking for.跑你手上最高级的树和矿。 等级越高的地块，副产物出得越多。
  • Unlock plots. A new plot comes with debris you and your Aniimo clear for Wood Blocks and Mineral Sand — the two upgrade materials — plus the space itself.开新地块。 每块新地带着碎石，你和 Aniimo 清掉它换来 Wood Block 和 Mineral Sand —— 正好是那两样升级材料 —— 外加这块地本身。
  • Plant Growth Tonic and Energizing Work Meal finish a job instantly. Useful both for an order deadline and for running Woodlands and Mines purely for byproducts.Plant Growth Tonic 和 Energizing Work Meal 能立刻完工。 既救订单的截止时间，也用来纯刷副产物。
  • Modification Toolkits unlock recipes. Premium recipes make an item worth more than the plain version; Quick recipes make Farmlands, Woodlands, Mines and the Well yield more.Modification Toolkit 解锁配方。 Premium 配方做出的东西比普通版值钱；Quick 配方让农田、林地、矿场和水井产得更多。
  • Index Score is where the late toolkits come from. Spending Bud Tickets on furniture raises it, and it pays out more Dream Sparks and Modification Toolkits. Furnishing is not only cosmetic.后期工具包靠 Index Score。 用 Bud Ticket 买家具就能抬它，而它给更多 Dream Spark 和 Modification Toolkit。摆家具不只是好看。
  • Check the yield against the carry cap. A Hauling Lv.3 Aniimo brings back 100 at a time. The highest Woodland returns more than 128 and Maple 105, so both take two trips however good the worker is — the plot is not the problem, the trip is. A slightly smaller tree that arrives in one carry can beat the biggest one that does not.看一眼产量和搬运上限对不对得上。 搬运 3 级的伊莫一趟只带 100。最高一级林地一次收 超过 128、枫树 105，所以这两样不管工人多好都得跑两趟 —— 问题不在树，在那一趟。小一点、一趟能搬完的树，能赢过最大但那趟搬不完的。

Toolkits get rarer the higher your RV goes, so the Index stops being optional — later on it is the supply.房车等级越高，工具包越稀，所以 Index 这块迟早绕不开 —— 到后面它就是你的进货渠道。

Layout: three temperatures from two buildings布局：两栋建筑，三种温度

Once you have the Heat Furnace and the Cooling Unit, crops and trees start asking for a temperature: Scorching, Warm, Cool or Freezing.有了加热炉（Heat Furnace）和制冷机（Cooling Unit）之后，作物和树就开始挑温度了：极热、温暖、凉爽、严寒。

  • Overlap two zones and you get a third. A plot inside a Scorching area and a Cool area at the same time reads as Warm, so two buildings cover three growing conditions.两个区重叠就出第三种。 一块地同时落在极热区和凉爽区里，读作 温暖，于是两栋建筑管住三种生长条件。
  • The two extremes overlap into neutral. Freezing plus Scorching gives a temperature-neutral zone, not a hotter or a colder one.两个极端叠出来的是中性。 严寒加极热得到的是温度中性区，不会更热也不会更冷。
  • Bands add up a step at a time. The Cooling Unit at Lv.2 leaves a plot Freezing; lay the Heat Furnace's Lv.1 band over the same plot and it reads Cool — one step back from the cold, not back to nothing. That is how a plot gets pulled to the temperature you want without moving either building.温度带是一档一档往上加的。 冰机二级盖到的地方是严寒；把火机一级的带子叠在上面，同一块地就读作凉爽 —— 是往回退一档，不是退到没有。想要哪一档，与其挪机器，不如让两条带子叠一下。
  • At RV 7 it is how three temperatures coexist. The plots start asking for Freezing, Cool and Warm at once, and two machines serve all three: keep the Cool crops inside the strip where the two bands overlap, the Warm ones inside the Heat Furnace band and outside the Cooling Unit, the Freezing ones in the cold alone.七级就是这么同时养三种作物的。 到那一级，地块会同时要严寒、凉爽和温暖，两栋机器够用：要凉爽的作物放进两条带子重叠的那一条，要温暖的留在火机带子里、躲开冰机，要严寒的只吃冰机。
  • A plot only has to be touched. Any part of a plot inside a temperature zone carries that temperature for the whole plot, so the placement does not have to be precise.碰到一点点就算。 地块只要有任意一角落在温度区里，整块地就按这个温度算，所以位置不用摆得很精准。

Getting this wrong does not kill the crop. It grows slower, which is the same thing as losing the time.摆错了不会把作物弄死。它只是长得慢 —— 而长得慢和浪费掉那些时间是同一件事。

Build mode's four quiet controls建造模式里四个不显眼的开关

Every layout is placed in Build Mode, and the controls the game never points at are the ones that decide how closely you can place what you have in mind.布置全在建造模式里做，而真正决定「摆得准不准」的那几个开关，游戏一个都不会指给你。

  • F3 turns grid snapping off. With it off, furniture and facilities can sit half a cell out of line, which is how a tight production block takes one more bench without pushing the next row out of a temperature band. Turn it back on for the next block, or the rows stop lining up.F3 关掉网格吸附。 关掉之后，家具和设施可以错开半格对齐 —— 产线挤的时候，半格就是多塞一张工作台的位置，前提是它还在温度带里。铺完这一段记得打开，否则下一段的行距就对不上了。
  • F1 is multi-select. Drag a box over the objects you want and save the selection from the panel on the right of the screen. That saved selection is what a combo is.F1 是多选。 拉个框把要的东西圈进去，再从屏幕右边的面板把这一选存下来 —— 存下来的那一选，就是「组合」。
  • There are fifteen saved combos, and deleting one destroys it. The slot comes back; the layout in it does not. Keep the one you are happy with and build the next experiment somewhere else rather than overwriting it.最多存 15 组，删掉就没了。 格子会空出来，里面那套布置不会。所以满意的那套留着，下一个实验换个地方摆，别覆盖它。
  • Sharing is one menu deep. Build Mode's rightmost tab is the combos tab; click the combo's picture, and under its description there is a magnifier — the eight-character code sits under the name, ready to copy.分享藏在菜单里一层。 建造模式最右边那个分类就是组合；点开组合的图，说明下面有一个放大镜 —— 八位组合码就在名字下面，可以直接复制。

This is the other half of the home code: the RV table lists codes to bring in, and this is where the code you hand out comes from. Both directions use the same eight characters.这是上面家园码的另一半：房车表列的是「导入别人的」，这一段说的是你自己的码从哪来。两个方向用的是同一串八位字符。
