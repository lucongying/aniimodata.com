Sparki幽幽焰

Fire火 Regen回能

Sparki has 4 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.幽幽焰 共有 4 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Sparki is a three-card fire species: a 42-power fire ring, a 50-power orb, and the basic attack. The ring is the one worth noticing — it expands and then contracts, and hits harder the further away the target is, which makes this a fire species that wants distance rather than contact.幽幽焰是一个三张卡的火系物种：一发 42 威力的火圈、一颗 50 威力的火弹，加上基础攻击。值得留意的是那个火圈 —— 它先扩张再收缩，而且目标离得越远伤害越高，所以这是一只想要距离、不想要贴身接触的火系。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Blazing Flames灼热火焰

It permanently carries 20 stacks of Fire Debuff on itself, while its two named skills put stacks on the target instead: Trick Fire Ring 4, Fire Orb 3. The 20 is the odd half — it sits on this Aniimo rather than on the enemy, and the official card does not say what it does there, so it is recorded here and left at that.它自身常驻 20 层【火元素异常】，而两个具名技能加的是目标：【诡计火圈】4 层、【火焰弹】3 层。奇怪的是那 20 层 —— 它挂在自己身上而不是敌人身上，而官方卡没说这 20 层在它自己身上起什么作用，所以我们照记，不作解释。
