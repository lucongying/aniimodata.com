Wisptis幽爪镰

Dark暗 DPS输出

Wisptis has 3 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.幽爪镰 共有 3 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Wisptis is a dark damage species with four cards: a 60-power Scythe Dance, a 60-power Forest Whirl in grass, a Dark Dash that grants +40% basic attack damage for 10 seconds, and the basic attack. The Dash is the one that gives the species a plan — everything else is a hit, and that card is a window.幽爪镰是一个暗系输出，四张卡：一发 60 威力的镰舞、一发 60 威力的草系林间回旋、一次给予 10 秒 +40% 普攻伤害的暗影突进，加上基础攻击。真正给这个物种一个思路的是那次突进 —— 别的都只是一下打击，只有它是打开一个窗口。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Cloak隐身

Turns invisible. The official card says nothing beyond that — no duration, no cost, and no word on whether attacking drops it — so treat what you can do inside the state as untested rather than unlimited.进入隐身状态。官方卡上没有别的信息 —— 没写持续时间、没写消耗、也没写攻击会不会解除 —— 所以状态里能做什么，按「没验证过」看，别按「随便做」看。

Trait特性

Battle Fervor狂热战意

Killing something grants a stack of Battlelust: +25% damage for 20 seconds, up to 2 stacks. It only switches on when a target actually dies, so in a long fight where nothing is dropping it never starts at all.击杀目标获得 1 层【战意】：伤害 +25%，持续 20 秒，最多 2 层。它只在有目标真的倒下时才亮 —— 一场迟迟打不死人的拉锯里，它从头到尾都不会启动。

Skills技能

Split the way the official page splits them: Combat holds the skills you cast, Innate the basic attack. The list is the species', not the form's — a form does not change it.分法和官方页面一致：战斗技能是你会主动放的那几个，固有技能里是基础攻击。这份表属于物种，不属于形态 —— 换形态不会换掉它。
