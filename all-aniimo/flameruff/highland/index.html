Overview简介

The game's own description of this form: Flameruffs living in the mountains have perfect camouflage and lava-coated claws for running in extremely hot areas.游戏对这个形态自己的描述：在山地生活的狼群，毛色更利于在环境中伪装，经常在高热区域奔跑的四肢也裹上了一层熔岩壳。

Rock is this form's own — the base form is Fire alone. Its range is entirely its own: Zephyrus Landbridge and Russet Highlands, sharing none of the four areas the base form lists. Its page draws three nodes — Emberpup → Flameruff → Scorchhowl — with Flameruff, the middle one, being this form, and the species tree forks Inferlupa off that same node.土是这个形态自己加上去的 —— 基础形态只有火。它的活动范围完全是自己的：海角丘谷和丹岩高地，基础形态列的四个区域一个都不重叠。它这一页画三个节点 —— 小炭犬 → 焚火狼 → 狼斗士 —— 中间那个焚火狼就是本形态；物种树在同一个节点上还分出狱魂狼一支。
