The six numbers belong to Emberpup and are shared by every form. The rest of what this page says about this form is read off that form's own page on the official wiki.六个数字属于 小炭犬，所有形态共用。这一页关于本形态的其余内容，全部读自本形态在官方维基上的那一页。

Overview简介

The game's own description of this form: Emberpup living in sunny mountain forests have warm golden-yellow fur tips that seem to leave traces of sunshine as they run.游戏对这个形态自己的描述：在阳光充足的山林间生活的小炭犬，毛发尖端是温暖的金黄色，跑动时如同阳光留下了足迹。

The element is the basic form's — Fire alone — and so is the chip count, two. What changes is where it lives and what its page draws: the one area it lists, Beast Fang Ridge, is one of the five the basic form lists, and its line runs three nodes deep — Emberpup → Flameruff → Scorchhowl — where the species tree forks at Flameruff.属性是基础形态的 —— 只有火 —— 家园图标数也一样，两个。变的是它住在哪、页面画了什么：它只列了一个区域狼牙岭，是基础形态五个区域里的一个；它这一页的线画到三个节点 —— 小炭犬 → 焚火狼 → 狼斗士 —— 而物种树的这一步在 Flameruff 处是分叉的。

Homeland ability家园能力

The official page carries 2 Homeland Ability icons for this form, against 2 for the base form, and names none of them in either language. The count is what the wiki prints; the names are not there to read.官方页面给这个形态画了 2 个家园能力图标，基础形态是 2 个，而两种语言都没有给出其中任何一个的名字。维基印出来的是数量，名字不在上面。
