Waleetle摩托郎

Rock土 DPS输出 A

Waleetle has 3 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.摩托郎 共有 3 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Waleetle is the damage reading of the Baleetle fork: it keeps both ball skills and adds Dark Wheel Slash, a Sandstorm Spin that speeds up to double its damage frequency without costing any more energy, and a 195-power Rock Ball Blast that is free. The spin is the card that rewards patience — the longer it runs, the better the rate.摩托郎是滚滚郎分叉里偏输出的读法：它保留两个滚球技能，又加上暗轮斩、一条在不增加消耗的前提下把伤害频率提到两倍的沙暴回旋，以及一发免费的 195 威力岩球爆破。回旋是那张奖励耐心的卡 —— 转得越久，效率越高。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Hustle疾行

Holds a faster run for as long as it is on, and drains stamina the whole time. It is a state, not a dash — letting go is what stops it, and the cost is per second rather than per use. Staying in it to cross a long stretch is the expensive way to travel.开着的时候一直保持更快的移动速度，全程持续消耗体力。它是一个状态，不是一次冲刺 —— 松开才停，代价按秒算，不按次数算。想靠它跑长距离，是最费体力的走法。

Trait特性

Overloaded Engine过载引擎

Moving and casting both fill an Overload meter. Once it is full, the next skill deals 40% extra damage and snaps Sandstorm Spin straight to top speed. The charging condition is the interesting part: you build it by playing normally, not by doing anything special.移动和释放技能都会积攒【过载能量】。充满后，下一次技能额外造成 40% 伤害，并且让【砂暴大回环】立刻进入最高转速。值得留意的是充能条件 —— 正常打就在攒，不需要刻意做什么。
