Overview简介

The game's own description of this form: Emberpups living in the mountains have rock-like fur colors and a shaggy coat created by the mountain winds.游戏对这个形态自己的描述：在山地生活的小炭犬，毛发变成了岩石的颜色，也因为山地上的强风吹拂而变得有些凌乱。

Rock is this form's own addition — the basic Emberpup is Fire alone — and it carries three Homeland chips against the basic form's two. Its range is entirely its own: Zephyrus Landbridge and Russet Highlands, sharing none of the five areas the basic form lists. Its page draws the line three nodes deep — Emberpup → Flameruff → Scorchhowl — where the species tree forks at Flameruff.土是这个形态自己加上去的 —— 基础形态的小炭犬只有火 —— 家园图标也从基础形态的两个变成三个。它的活动范围完全是自己的：海角丘谷和丹岩高地，基础形态列出的五个区域一个都不重叠。它这一页把这条线画到了三个节点 —— 小炭犬 → 焚火狼 → 狼斗士 —— 而物种树的这一步在 Flameruff 处是分叉的。
