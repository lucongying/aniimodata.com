Witchin巫帽草

Dark暗Grass草 Regen回能

Witchin has 3 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.巫帽草 共有 3 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Witchin is a dark-and-grass regeneration species with a harder edge than the role label suggests: Malice Burst and Oozing Malice are 68-power hits, Black Hole Domain pulls a target in for 54, and the kit ends on a 164-power Annihilation Bomb that costs nothing. It keeps Euphoric Sonic Blast from the form it evolved out of, so the line's scream stays with it all the way up.巫帽草是一个暗+草的回复型物种，但比「回复」这个定位更凶：恶念爆发和渗出恶意是 68 威力的打击，黑洞领域以 54 威力把目标吸住，而技能组的终点是一发不耗能量的 164 威力湮灭弹。它保留了进化前形态的狂喜音爆，所以这条线的那一嗓子一路留到了它身上。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Luminous Seed照明种子

Throws a seed that lights the ground where it lands. The light stays where it was put instead of following you, so this is the one to use when you want to mark a spot and walk away from it.扔出一颗种子，落地后照亮那一片地面。光是留在原地的，不跟着你走 —— 想标记一个位置然后走开的时候用它。

Trait特性

Prank恶作剧

Every 18 seconds it arms itself, and the next skill to connect puts Curse on everything it hits for 10 seconds. Dark basic attacks against a Cursed target refund 0.4 energy each. The two halves are one loop: land the curse, then farm the refund with basic attacks while it holds.每 18 秒蓄一次，下一次命中的技能会给打到的目标挂上【诅咒】，持续 10 秒。对带【诅咒】的目标打暗元素普攻，每次回复 0.4 能量。两半是一套循环：先挂上诅咒，再用普攻在持续时间里把能量收回来。

Skills技能

Split the way the official page splits them: Combat holds the skills you cast, Innate the basic attack. The list is the species', not the form's — a form does not change it.分法和官方页面一致：战斗技能是你会主动放的那几个，固有技能里是基础攻击。这份表属于物种，不属于形态 —— 换形态不会换掉它。
