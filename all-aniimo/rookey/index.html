Rookey盔卫士

Dark暗 Break击破

Rookey has 3 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.盔卫士 共有 3 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Rookey keeps the guard-breaking role and adds the card the species is built on: Bouncy Sling, which raises the BREAK damage a target takes by 15% for 15 seconds and then bounces on to other targets — allies included — plus a 196-power Heavy Slam at no cost. Everything else it can do gets better once that debuff is on, which is why the Sling is where the turns should go first.盔卫士保留了击破定位，并加上这个物种赖以成立的那张卡：弹力弹弓 —— 它让目标承受的击破伤害提高 15%、持续 15 秒，随后继续弹向其他目标（队友也算）—— 外加一发不耗能量的 196 威力重砸。那个减益一旦挂上，它别的招都会变强 —— 所以弹弓才是回合该先花的地方。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Detection探查视野

Swaps your view for a detective's one that picks out metal objects and cloaked Aniimo nearby. It finds things rather than moving you, and it is the only exploration skill here that answers a question instead of solving a distance.把视野换成探查视野，能看见周围的金属物品和隐身的伊莫。它的作用不是位移，是找东西 —— 这里是唯一一个用来回答问题的探索技能，不是用来解决距离的。

Trait特性

Guardbreak Resonance防破共鸣

The official card reads BREAK +0. That is the figure as the game prints it, reproduced here rather than guessed at — a value would have to be invented to say anything else, and inventing figures is the one thing this site does not do.官方卡上写的是 击破 +0。这是游戏印出来的数字，原样照抄，不替它补 —— 要说成别的数，就得凭空编一个，而编数字正是这个站不做的事。

Skills技能

Split the way the official page splits them: Combat holds the skills you cast, Innate the basic attack. The list is the species', not the form's — a form does not change it.分法和官方页面一致：战斗技能是你会主动放的那几个，固有技能里是基础攻击。这份表属于物种，不属于形态 —— 换形态不会换掉它。
