Leafy莲冠龙

Grass草Water水 Regen回能

Leafy has a single form recorded so far. Its stats, type, habitat and evolution requirement are below.莲冠龙 目前只记录到一个形态。它的数值、属性、栖息地和进化条件都在下面。

Overview简介

Leafy is the other end of the Pranky fork, and it trades Glacy's healing cards for ground control: Vine Entanglement only stuns what is standing on grass when it lands, and Lotus Bloom answers that with an instant team heal plus 15% grass enhancement. So the species is built around making the ground right first and then punishing whoever is standing on it.莲冠龙是顽皮鱼分叉的另一端，它把莹冰龙的治疗卡换成了场地控制：藤蔓缠绕只会眩晕命中时站在草地上的目标，而莲花绽放正好补上这一点 —— 瞬间团队治疗外加 15% 草元素强化。所以这个物种的思路是先把地面铺对，再惩罚站在上面的人。

Trait特性

Power of Nature自然之力

It only works while it is off the field: every 3 skills a teammate casts, a patch of grass appears under them. A trait that pays out from the bench is asking for a slot rather than for uptime — you get this by leaving it out.它只在未登场时生效：队友每释放 3 次技能，脚下就会生成一片草地。这类从替补席上生效的特性，要的是一个队伍位置而不是在场时间 —— 想拿到它，就得把它换下去。

    A field underfoot that gives Grass teammates 2.4 energy per second for 12 seconds and, if there is grass inside it, upgrades that grass into Vitality Blooms which add 1% HP per second on top. So the card has a floor and a bonus: the energy half works on any ground, and the healing half exists only if you put it down in the right place.在脚下放出一片法阵，每秒给草系队友 2.4 点能量，持续 12 秒；如果法阵里有草地，还会把草地升级成活力花丛，额外每秒回复 1% 生命值。所以这张卡有一个保底和一个加成：回能量那一半在任何地面上都生效，回血那一半只有放对地方才有。
