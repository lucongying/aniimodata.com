No. 008

Tromber长号鸥

Wind风 Support辅助 B

Tromber has 3 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.长号鸥 共有 3 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Tromber is the support arm of the Chirpi fork and the one that keeps the music: Waltz adds a movement-series buff worth +27% crit damage, and Zephyr's Breath gives it a 92-power finisher at no energy cost. Two of its other cards are still tornadoes borrowed from other families, so the kit reads as half inherited and half its own.长号鸥是口哨鸥分叉里偏辅助的那一支，也是保留音乐的那一只：【圆舞曲】提供一个 +27% 暴击伤害的乐章增益，而 Zephyr's Breath 给了它一发不耗能量的 92 威力收尾。它另外两张卡仍是从别的家族借来的龙卷，所以整套技能读起来是一半继承、一半自己的。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Take Off起飞

Takes flight, which makes attacks from the ground miss. The price is printed on the same card: it takes more damage while airborne. That makes this a way to slip one specific attack, not a way to be safer in general.进入飞行状态，地面来的攻击打不中你。代价就写在同一张卡上：滞空时受到的伤害更高。所以它是躲掉某一下的手段，不是让自己更安全的手段。

Trait特性

Band Member乐团成员

Switching out hands 20 energy to the next Chirpi family member you bring in, at most once every 20 seconds. Only a family member collects it, so this is a trait about how the team is built rather than about this species' own turn.战斗中换人时，接下来登场的口哨鸥家族成员获得 20 能量，最多每 20 秒触发一次。只有家族成员能拿到 —— 所以它管的是队伍怎么组，不是它自己这一手。

Skills技能

Split the way the official page splits them: Combat holds the skills you cast, Innate the basic attack. The list is the species', not the form's — a form does not change it.分法和官方页面一致：战斗技能是你会主动放的那几个，固有技能里是基础攻击。这份表属于物种，不属于形态 —— 换形态不会换掉它。
