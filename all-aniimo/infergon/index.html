Infergon炎铠龙

Fire火 DPS输出 A

Infergon has 2 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.炎铠龙 共有 2 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Infergon is the fire ending of the same fork and the species whose kit is built on a swap: a Fiery Charge and a Gale Charge, two 60-power dashes in different elements, with Flaming Claws and its five-second second strike alongside, and a 168-power Hot Breath for free. Two charge cards in two elements is the shape to notice — it can always pick the one the target is not ready for.炎铠龙是同一个分叉里偏火的终点，它的技能组建在一个「换」字上：炽焰冲锋和烈风冲锋是两发 60 威力、不同属性的突进，旁边是烈焰爪和它五秒内的第二下，最后一发免费的 168 威力灼热吐息。值得注意的形状是「两个属性各一发冲锋」—— 它总能挑到目标没准备好的那一个。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

At a glance一眼速览

Mobility探索技能

Bilateral双侧视野

A second view of the same place, described as a different perspective rather than a wider one. Its use is not stated, so treat it as a way of looking at things rather than a way of finding them — Detection is the skill that finds things.看同一个地方的另一种视角 —— 官方说的是「不一样的景色」，不是「更大的范围」。用途没写，所以它更像换一种看法，而不是找东西的办法；找东西的是探查。

Trait特性

Power of Fire火焰之力

Against a target carrying more than 5 stacks of Fire Debuff it deals an extra 30% damage. Read it next to Blazing Flames, whose entire job is laying those stacks down — the two halves are built to be used together and neither does much on its own.对【火元素异常】超过 5 层的目标额外造成 30% 伤害。它要和【炽焰】连着看 —— 后者整件事就是把层数叠上去。两半本来就是配套的，单看哪一半作用都不大。

Skills技能

    A charge that becomes a dive ending the flight state if used airborne, and grants a shield worth 5% max HP for 10 seconds on hit. So it pays on contact rather than on cast: a miss gives you neither the shield nor the repositioning, which makes it a skill to use when the target is not going anywhere.一次冲锋；在飞行状态使用会变成俯冲并结束飞行，命中后获得相当于最大生命值 5% 的护盾，持续 10 秒。所以它的收益在命中时结算，不在释放时结算：打空就既没有护盾、也没有位移 —— 这就要求你挑目标跑不掉的时候用。

That is 4 stacks spent across the pair, and the second strike is the one worth throwing: the window is five seconds, and the bonus depends on the target still carrying stacks when it lands.一次火焰爪击，5 秒内可以再放一次更强的爪击 —— 而两次都可以消耗目标 2 层【火元素异常】，额外造成 30% 威力的伤害。这一对爪子一共花掉 4 层，而真正值得打出去的是第二下：窗口只有 5 秒，加成还取决于那一下落下去时目标身上是否还有层数。

    A charge that deals damage, with two conditional clauses. Used while flying it becomes a dive and ends the flight; and for the duration of the charge it reflects incoming projectiles. So it closes the gap and counters anything shooting at you in the same motion — but only during that short window, which means it is a reaction, not a shield.一次造成伤害的冲锋，带两个条件分支：在飞行状态使用会变成俯冲并结束飞行；冲锋过程中会反弹受到的飞行弹道攻击。所以它一次动作里既拉近了距离、又反制了朝你射来的东西 —— 但只在那很短的一段里有效，也就是说它是反应，不是护盾。
