Lavazar炽岩龙

Fire火Rock土 Break击破

Lavazar has a single form recorded so far. Its stats, type, habitat and evolution requirement are below.炽岩龙 目前只记录到一个形态。它的数值、属性、栖息地和进化条件都在下面。

Overview简介

Lavazar is the fire branch of the Pebbling line and the shortest kit in its own family: a 10-energy Fiery Punch at 67 power, a free Lava Breath, and the basic attack. It is also where the line's typing changes — earth and fire together, against the pure-earth reading its sibling takes.炽岩龙是碎岩仔这条线上的火系分支，也是它自己家族里最短的技能组：10 能量换 67 威力的炽炎拳、一发免费的熔岩吐息，加上基础攻击。它也是这条线换属性的地方 —— 土与火并列，而它的兄弟走的是纯土那条路。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Pyro Charge活性化

Once it has spent 50 energy in total it gains Pyro Charge, and from then on applies 1 stack of Fire Debuff to everything nearby every 2 seconds, each lasting 10 seconds. The 50 is a threshold rather than an extra cost — it is energy you were spending on skills anyway. ⚠️ The official English card is broken here: it reads "a total of EP" and "Every 502s". The figures above are the Chinese card's, which is the coherent one.累计消耗 50 点能量后获得【活性化】，此后每 2 秒给周围的目标叠 1 层【火元素异常】，每层持续 10 秒。这 50 点是一个门槛，不是额外开销 —— 花的是你本来就要花的能量。⚠️ 官方英文卡这一条是坏的：写成「a total of EP」和「Every 502s」。上面的数字取自中文卡，中文卡是通顺的那一边。

    A dashing punch that pierces through targets and then clears every stack of Fire Debuff off them, paying +5% Power per stack. It is the mirror of the card that lays 8 stacks down at once: this one converts the whole pile into damage, so the two are a sequence rather than two equal options.冲刺出拳，贯穿目标并造成伤害，随后清空目标身上所有【火元素异常】，每层额外造成 5% 威力伤害。它和那张「一次铺 8 层」的卡正好是一对：这一张把整堆层数换成伤害，所以两者是一前一后的关系，不是两个等价的选项。
