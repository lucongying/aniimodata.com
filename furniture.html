Furniture家具

Furniture in Aniimo is not a decoration system. Every piece carries Habitability, Habitability is a hard requirement on every RV upgrade from level 10 up, and the store that sells it is where a large part of the mid-game progression actually happens. This page is what it does, what each level asks for, what it costs and what it pays.伊莫里的家具不是装修系统。每一件都带宜居度，而宜居度是房车 10 级之后每一级升级的硬门槛 —— 卖家具的那家店，是中期进度真正发生的地方。这一页讲的是它干什么、每一级要多少、要多少钱，以及它另外还付你什么。

What furniture is for家具用来干什么

Most of what this game files under furniture is not decoration. Every piece carries one number the game will actually charge you for — Habitability — and the RV upgrade screen wants a running total of it before it will spend your Home Coin. The furniture store is therefore a progression gate rather than a decorating shop, and the habit of ignoring it is survivable right up to the level where it stops being optional.游戏归在家具底下的东西，大部分不是装饰。每一件都带一个游戏真会跟你收的数值 —— 宜居度 —— 而房车升级界面在你交出家园币之前，要的是一份宜居度总和。所以家具店是一道进度关卡，不是一家装修铺；而「先不管它」这个习惯正好能一路撑到它不再可选的等级。

  • Habitability is the stat that gates. Of everything the game prints on a furniture piece — capacity, rank, set membership, which zone accepts it — Habitability is the only one that appears in an upgrade requirement.真正卡人的只有宜居度。 游戏印在家具上的所有数字里 —— 容量、品级、所属套装、能被哪个区域接受 —— 只有宜居度会出现在升级要求里。
  • The gate starts at RV 10. Levels 2 through 9 ask for materials and Home Coin and nothing else, which is exactly why players arrive at level 10 owning none of it.门槛从房车 10 级开始。 2 到 9 级只要材料和家园币，别的什么都不要，这正好解释了为什么大多数人到 10 级时一件都没有。
  • What you can reach depends on how many of a piece the game lets you place, not only on which piece is best. The strongest piece the store sells for Bud Tickets pays 1,249 Habitability, against a first gate of 13,000, and the largest single allowance is 3,000 copies of one piece. Cheap pieces you can put down in bulk are what actually close the gap, which is the part of the arithmetic the store does not show you.能堆到多少，取决于一件东西让你摆几个，不只取决于哪一件最好。 商店拿Bud Ticket 卖的那批货里，单件最高的是 1,249 宜居度 —— 而第一道门槛就是13,000；单个上限最高的是同一种东西摆 3,000 个。真正把缺口填上的是那些便宜、能大批量摆下去的东西 —— 这部分账商店不给你看。
  • The same purchases pay a second time. Furniture is the largest of the five Home Index categories, and the Index has a reward ladder of its own. The last section on this page covers it.同一笔钱会付你两次。 家具是 Home Index 五个分类里最大的一个，而 Index自己还有一条奖励阶梯。这一页最后一节讲它。

Every figure on this page is read off the game's own furniture store and Home Index screens rather than estimated. Where a value only exists for some of the levels it applies to, the table says so instead of filling the gap.这一页每个数字都是从游戏自己的家具商店和 Home Index 界面上读的，不是估的。只对部分等级存在的数字，表里就写成缺，不拿别的填。

What each RV level asks for每一级房车要多少

From RV 10 the upgrade screen asks for a Habitability total on top of the materials and Home Coin it already wanted. The requirement climbs 500 a level from 13,000 at RV 10 to 16,000 at RV 16, then switches to 1,000 a level and finishes at 20,000. The three plot columns are the other half of the same gate, and they are conditions rather than rewards: a level does not ask you to have that many plots lying around, it asks you to have placed them. Farmland wants two more every level and woodland one more every level, while mines only want a new one on the even levels, so the odd levels carry a dash rather than repeating the figure above them.从房车 10 级起，升级界面在原本就要的材料和家园币之外，另要一份宜居度总和。这条线从 10 级的 13,000 起每级涨 500，到 16 级的 16,000 之后改成每级涨 1,000，最后停在 20,000。右边三列地块是同一道关卡的另一半，而且它们是条件不是奖励：那一级要的不是你手上有这么多地块，是要你已经把它们摆下去。农田每级多要两块、林地每级多要一块，矿场只在偶数等级多要一个，所以奇数等级那一格印的是破折号，而不是把上一级的数字再抄一遍。

What Habitability costs宜居度要多少钱

The furniture store prices its stock in Bud Tickets, and across that stock the exchange rate between the two columns is flat. On 791 of the 795 Bud Ticket pieces the Habitability figure and the price are the same number, one for one. There is no bargain to hunt for and no trap to avoid, because there is no spread to exploit: 13,000 Habitability at RV 10 costs 13,000 Bud Tickets, and the four exceptions below move that by about one percent. They are the only pieces in the game where the two columns disagree, and they disagree in both directions.家具店的货标的是 Bud Ticket，而这一批货两列之间的汇率是平的。795 件Bud Ticket 家具里有 791 件，宜居度和标价是同一个数字，一比一。没有便宜可捡，也没有坑要躲，因为中间根本没有价差：10 级要的 13,000 宜居度就是13,000 张 Bud Ticket，下面那四个例外也只动得了其中百分之一。真正让两列对不上的只有这四件，而且两个方向都有。

The useful reading is the other direction — the two pieces that cost more than they pay are the only purchases in the store that lose you Habitability, and both are the sort of thing bought for how they look. The stock that is not priced in Bud Tickets at all is the facilities — the farmland, woodland and mine plots, the wells and beds and the windmill — which Home Coin buys instead, and which is why the plot columns above are a Home Coin bill rather than a furniture one.四件都是 Legendary —— 商店把它的门面货放在这一档 —— 而它们对你的实际价值上限很小：两件赚的加在一起，相对 13,000 张的门槛只省下 138 张。有用的是反过来读：这两件「花的比给的多」的，是全店里唯一会让你亏宜居度的消费，而两件都是买外形的那种东西。完全不标 Bud Ticket 的那批货是设施 —— 农田、林地、矿场地块，水井、床和风车 —— 那些收的是家园币，所以上面那三列地块其实是家园币的账，不是家具的账。

How many of each piece you can place一种家具让你摆几个

Every piece carries a placement limit — the number of copies of that one piece the game will let you put down. It is the second half of the arithmetic, and it matters more than the price does: the most Habitability any single copy pays is 1,249, against a first gate of 13,000, so no one purchase is decisive and what compounds is the limit. A 44-Habitability fence you can place 300 times is worth more to your total than a 639-Habitability bed you can place 10 times. The table below takes each of the fourteen limits that exist and shows the strongest piece in the Bud Ticket stock under it and what that limit is worth at the piece's rate. Where the same figure is reached by more than one piece the cell says how many, because which of them you buy does not change the arithmetic.每一件都带一个摆放上限 —— 同一种东西游戏让你放下几个。这是这笔账的另一半，而且比价格更重要：单件最高只给 1,249 宜居度，第一道门槛就是13,000，所以没有哪一笔购买是决定性的，真正会滚起来的是上限。一件 44 宜居度、能摆 300 次的栅栏，对你总值的贡献大于一件 639 宜居度、只能摆 10 次的床。下面这张表把游戏里存在的十四个上限逐条列出，附上 Bud Ticket 那批货里该上限下最强的一件，以及把这个上限摆满值多少。同一个数字有几件并列时，格子里写的是件数 —— 因为买其中哪一件都不改变这笔账。

Read the last column as a ceiling rather than a plan. Reaching 102,000 Habitability from one piece with a 2,000 limit costs 102,000 Bud Tickets, which is the same as reaching 102,000 any other way, and the flat rate from the table above is why. What the column actually shows is how much of a total has to come from somewhere else. Six of these fourteen limits could carry the first gate on their own if you placed one piece to its maximum, and only four could carry the last one — so through most of the ladder the total is reached by stacking several limits rather than by finding a piece.最后一列当天花板读，不要当计划读。靠一件上限 2,000 的东西堆到 102,000宜居度，就要花 102,000 张 Bud Ticket —— 和用任何别的方式堆到 102,000 一样贵，上面那张表的平价汇率就是原因。这一列真正说明的是：一笔总数里有多少必须来自别处。十四个上限里有六个，单靠一种摆满就能扛住第一道门槛；而只有四个能扛住最后一道 —— 所以阶梯中段那几级，靠的是几个上限叠出来，不是找到某一件。

What the Home Index paysIndex 给你什么

Habitability is not the only thing furniture is for. The Home Index scores everything you have ever obtained, once per entry — a second copy of a piece you already own adds nothing — and furniture is its largest single category at 9,420 points, over a third of the whole thing. The five categories come to 25,332 points, and the ladder of grades they feed pays out in Bud Tickets, Glimmer and the Modification Toolkit.家具不是只为宜居度。Home Index 给你拿到过的每一样东西记一次分 —— 已有的东西再拿一件不加分 —— 而家具是其中最大的单个分类，9,420 分，占全部三分之一强。五个分类合计 25,332 分，而它们喂出来的那条等级阶梯，结算的是 Bud Ticket、Glimmer 和 Modification Toolkit。

  • Furniture — 9,420 points, over a third of the total and the only one of the five you can move by spending Bud Tickets.家具 —— 9,420 分，占三分之一强，也是五个分类里唯一一个能靠花 BudTicket 推动的。
  • Mutant Plant — 7,050 points, from what the farmland and woodland turn out rather than from anything you buy.变异植物 —— 7,050 分，来自农田和林地种出来的东西，不来自你买的任何东西。
  • Resource Product — 5,140 points, the output of the production facilities.资源产物 —— 5,140 分，生产设施的产出。
  • Building Materials — 2,530 points, the walls, roofs and floors.建材 —— 2,530 分，墙、屋顶和地板。
  • Season Index — 1,192 points, the smallest, and the one that depends on what the current season happens to be.Season Index —— 1,192 分，最小的一块，也是唯一一块取决于当前是哪个季节的。

The bottom of the ladder is a formality — the first grade starts at 500 and furniture alone carries you past it. The top is not. Master of Collections closes at 20,000 and there is nothing above it, and since the five categories add up to 25,332 while the ladder tops out at 24,500, the last few hundred points have nowhere to go. Buying furniture for the Index pays for a while and then stops paying; buying it for Habitability is the purchase that never stops mattering.阶梯底下那一截是走形式 —— 第一级从 500 分起，光家具就够把你送过去。顶上那截不是。Master of Collections 在 20,000 分收尾，上面再没有东西，而五个分类加起来是 25,332 分、阶梯却停在 24,500 —— 最后那几百分没有地方可去。为 Index 买家具能付你一阵子，然后就不付了；为宜居度买家具，是那种一直都在算数的购买。
