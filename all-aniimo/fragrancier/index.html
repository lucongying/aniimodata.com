Fragrancier香氛鸟

Dark暗 Support辅助

Fragrancier has a single form recorded so far. Its stats, type, habitat and evolution requirement are below.香氛鸟 目前只记录到一个形态。它的数值、属性、栖息地和进化条件都在下面。

Overview简介

Fragrancier adds two cards to the line: Fragrance Marking for damage at no energy cost, and Fragrance Bomb, a fourth support skill. It is also the only species in this family that carries a Mobility card at all, and its Withering Bloom prints 22 power against Dewy's 10 — the same card, a bigger number on it.香氛鸟在这条线上加了两张卡：不耗能量、纯打伤害的香气标记，以及第四张辅助技能香氛炸弹。它也是这一家子里唯一带移动卡的物种；而它的枯萎绽放写着 22 威力，Dewy 的那张是 10 —— 同一张卡，数字更大。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Take Off起飞

Takes flight, which makes attacks from the ground miss. The price is printed on the same card: it takes more damage while airborne. That makes this a way to slip one specific attack, not a way to be safer in general.进入飞行状态，地面来的攻击打不中你。代价就写在同一张卡上：滞空时受到的伤害更高。所以它是躲掉某一下的手段，不是让自己更安全的手段。

Trait特性

Dangerous Fragrance危险香气

Any skill that damages a target also cuts its Dark Resistance by 12% for 20 seconds. It rides on every damaging skill rather than on one of them, so the reduction is effectively always up — which makes this a trait working for the rest of the team's Dark damage as much as for its own.任何造成伤害的技能都会同时降低目标 12% 暗元素抗性，持续 20 秒。它挂在每一个伤害技能上，而不是某一个 —— 所以这个减抗基本一直在，等于它同时也在替队伍里其他人的暗元素伤害干活。

    Area damage plus a heal of 5% of its own max HP to the whole team — doubled if there is only one target in range. That inverts the usual rule: the healing is at its largest in the situation a damage skill is normally at its worst, which is exactly what makes it worth casting at a single enemy.范围伤害，同时基于自身最大生命值的 5% 回复全队生命值 —— 如果范围内只有一个目标，回复量翻倍。这是把常见规则倒过来写：治疗量最大的场景，恰好是伤害技能通常最吃亏的场景 —— 也正是这一点让它在单挑一个敌人时值得放。

    Jumps up to dodge an attack, lets a scent gather around it, then dive-pecks for damage — and if the dodge actually succeeded it gains a shield worth 10% max HP for 20 seconds. Same shape as the pebble-kick dodge elsewhere in the index, except that one pays in damage and Lockdown and this one pays in a shield: it is the defensive reading of the same idea.快速跃起回避攻击，让香气环绕身体后俯冲啄击 —— 如果这次回避真的成功了，就获得相当于最大生命值 10% 的护盾，持续 20 秒。和图鉴里那个碎石踢的回避是同一种形状，只是那个兑现的是伤害和【封锁】，而这个兑现的是护盾 —— 同一个思路的防御版。

The bonus is attached to one specific thing — dark basic attacks — rather than to damage in general, so it pays a team built around that and does nothing for one that is not.给前方目标挂上持续 20 秒的【暗香】标记，标记期间暗元素普攻对它们 +50% 伤害。这个加成绑定的是一件很具体的事 —— 暗元素普攻 —— 而不是泛泛的伤害，所以它只对围绕这个搭建的队伍有意义，别的队伍什么也拿不到。
