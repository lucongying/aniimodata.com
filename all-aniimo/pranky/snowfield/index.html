The six numbers belong to Pranky and are shared by every form. The rest of what this page says about this form is read off that form's own page on the official wiki.六个数字属于 顽皮鱼，所有形态共用。这一页关于本形态的其余内容，全部读自本形态在官方维基上的那一页。

Overview简介

The game's own description of this form: Prankies living in cold regions have frost on their gills and tail fins, and their membranes are padded with snow, appearing thicker.游戏对这个形态自己的描述：生活在寒冷地区的顽皮鱼，鳃叶和尾鳍上都结起了冰霜，皮膜也被积雪垫起，显得厚实了些。

Ice is this form's own — the base form is Water alone. Its range is entirely its own: Driftwise Meadow, sharing none of the three areas the base form lists. Its page draws three nodes — Skippy → Pranky → Glacy — with Pranky, the middle one, being this form; the species tree forks Leafy off that same node, a branch this page does not draw.冰是这个形态自己加上去的 —— 基础形态只有水。活动范围完全是它自己的：雪麓草甸，基础形态列的三个区域一个都不重叠。它这一页画三个节点 —— 莲顶鱼 → 顽皮鱼 → 莹冰龙 —— 中间那个顽皮鱼就是本形态；物种树在同一个节点上还分出莲冠龙一支，这一页没有画。
