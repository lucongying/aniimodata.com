The ten lists we read do not argue about which codes exist — they argue about what three of them pay, and about whether an eleventh is already dead. We have not redeemed one ourselves, so this page prints where the lists agree, names where they do not, and says which parts nobody can settle from here.我们读的十份清单不争「有哪些码」—— 争的是其中三个给什么，以及第十一个到底死没死。我们自己一个都没兑过，所以这一页印各家一致的部分，把不一致的摆出来，并说清哪些部分在这里谁也定不下来。

How this list was put together这份清单是怎么来的

Ten lists, four languages, one afternoon of reading. Nothing here is our own testing, and the differences between the lists are printed rather than smoothed over.十份清单、四种语言、一个下午的对照。这里没有一条是我们自己测的，清单之间的差异是印出来的，不是抹平的。

  • No account of ours has redeemed these. We have not typed one in. Two of the lists we read say theirs were tested personally; we cannot check that either, and we are not repeating the claim as though it were ours.我们自己一个码都没兑过。 没有一个码被我们输进游戏。读到的十份清单里，有两份说自己亲自测过 —— 那我们也核实不了，更不会把这句话当成自己的来说。
  • Ten lists, four languages. English, French, German and Russian lists were compared side by side. Five carry all ten codes; the other five carry between one and seven of the same ten, and add nothing the rest do not already have. Agreement across languages is the closest thing to confirmation available without an account.十份清单，四种语言。 英文、法文、德文、俄文的清单是并排比的。其中五份十个码全有，另外五份只有其中一至七个，而且没有任何一个是其余清单没有的。在手上没有账号的前提下，跨语言的一致就是最接近确认的东西。
  • A code can die two ways. One list says aniimoparty did not expire so much as run out — it reached its redemption limit. That is how the same code can be live on one list and dead on another without either of them being wrong.码有两种死法。 有一份清单说 aniimoparty 不是过期，是「额度用完了」——达到了兑换上限。所以同一个码可以在一份清单上是活的、在另一份上是死的，而两边都没错。
  • The most reliable free thing on this page is not a code. The Twitch campaign near the bottom pays more for two hours of watching than any code here, and it is the only item on the page with a published end date.这一页上最稳的免费东西不是码。 页面下方那个 Twitch 活动，看两小时给的量比这页任何一个码都多，也是这一页唯一一个有公开截止日期的。

The one that is already dead已经死了的那个

aniimoparty is the eleventh code, and the one the lists split on hardest. Two mark it dead on September 19 — one of them saying it reached its redemption limit rather than expiring — a third says it no longer seems to work, and a fourth was still listing it as new two days later. The two that give it a reward do not even agree on what it was: 50 Glimmer on one, 10,000 Credits and 5 Aniipod on the other. It costs ten seconds to try. Just do not read the failure as your account being broken.aniimoparty 是第十一个码，也是清单分歧最大的一个。两份在 9 月 19 日把它标成死的 —— 其中一份说它是达到了兑换上限而不是过期 —— 第三份说它似乎已经不能用，第四份两天后还把它列为新的。给它奖励的那两份连奖励是什么都不一致：一份写 50 Glimmer，另一份写 10,000 Credits 加 5 个 Aniipod。试一下只花十秒钟，只是别把失败读成你的账号出了问题。

Redeeming one怎么兑一个

The path is the same on PC, console and phone — only the button that opens the menu changes. On PC that button is Escape.PC、主机、手机上的路径一样，只有打开菜单的那个键不同。PC 上是 Escape。

  1. 01

    Open the menu打开菜单

    Escape on PC, or the ball icon in the top-right corner on a phone. The settings cog is on the right-hand bar.PC 上按 Escape，手机上是右上角的球图标。设置齿轮在右侧那条栏上。

  2. 02

    Settings, then AccountSettings，然后 Account

    Anything tied to your login lives under Account.跟登录账号绑的东西都在 Account 里。

  3. 03

    Gift Code Redemption

    This is the button; the field is labelled Enter redeem code. Paste rather than type — these codes are case-sensitive, and a wrong capital is the most common way to fail at this.就是这个按钮，输入框写的是 Enter redeem code。粘贴，别手打 —— 这些码区分大小写，一个大写字母打错是最常见的失败原因。

  4. 04

    Redeem, then open your Mail兑完去开邮箱

    The reward does not land in your inventory. It arrives as a letter in the in-game Mail, and you have to open it and claim the attachment.奖励不会直接进背包。 它会变成游戏内邮箱里的一封信，你要去把附件领出来。

  5. 05

    Do not expect it in the first hour别指望第一小时就能用

    This is the part the lists disagree on. Two say the code box works from the moment you have the main menu and only the Mail is locked; two others say the box itself is locked until the main story has sent you to Bloomville. Every version puts the wait at about the first hour of play, and the Mail is the gate in all of them — so a rejection on a new account usually means “come back later” rather than “dead code”.这是清单之间吵的那一点。两份说拿到主菜单就能用兑换框、只有邮箱是锁的；另外两份说兑换框本身要等主线把你送到 Bloomville 才开。所有版本都把等待放在开局第一小时左右，而邮箱在每一版里都是那道门 —— 所以全新账号被拒，通常是「晚点再来」，不是「码死了」。

When a code says no码说不行的时候

Only three things cause this, and just one of them is the code's fault.只有三种原因，其中只有一种是码自己的问题。

  • The capital letters. These codes are case-sensitive — ANIIMOGIFT and aniimogift are two different strings, and only one of them is a code. Copy and paste it.大小写。 这些码区分大小写 —— ANIIMOGIFT 和 aniimogift 是两个不同的字符串，只有一个是码。复制粘贴。
  • The Mail is not open yet. A new account can paste a live code into the box and get nothing it can use, because the reward is a letter and the letter has nowhere to go. This is the one that looks like a dead code and is not.邮箱还没开。 新账号可以把一个活着的码贴进兑换框，却拿不到任何能用的东西，因为奖励是一封信，而信没有地方可去。这一种最像死码，其实不是。
  • It is actually gone. Either the studio retired it on a date, or enough people redeemed it that it hit its cap. The codes announced on a livestream or inside a single event are the fastest to go.它真的没了。 要么官方到期下架，要么兑的人够多、撞上了上限。直播里发的、单个活动里发的，死得最快。

Where the new ones turn up新码会从哪儿冒出来

Codes arrive with announcements rather than on a schedule. Three places are worth watching, in this order:码是跟着公告走的，不按时间表。三个地方值得盯，按这个顺序：

  • In-game news. The news centre inside the game carries the announcement first, and it is the one channel nobody can fake.游戏内新闻。 公告会先进游戏里的新闻中心，而这是唯一一个没人能造假的渠道。
  • The official X account and Discord server. That is where the launch codes came from. Check that a Discord invite is current before you join — invite links expire.官方 X 账号和 Discord 服务器。 开服那批码就是从这儿来的。加 Discord 之前先确认邀请链接还有效 —— 邀请链接会过期。
  • Livestreams. Historically the most reliable moment for a code to appear, and the reason one sometimes shows up and dies within days.直播。 历史上码最常出现的时刻，也是一个码有时候冒出来几天就死掉的原因。

The other code that is not a code另一个不是码的码

Aniimo Together is a web event, and the code it hands out is not a gift code — it is an invitation. Pasting it into Gift Code Redemption will get you nowhere; it exists so other players can join your squad on the event page, and it only works during the launch window.Aniimo Together 是个网页活动，它给的那个码不是礼品码，是邀请。贴进 Gift Code Redemption 不会有任何结果；它只用来让别人在活动页面上加入你的小队，而且只在开服窗口期内有效。

  • Six players, one Aniipod Ultra each. A squad is six people. Fill all six slots and every member is owed one Aniipod Ultra — but nothing is delivered while the event is running, and only one list spells that payout out, so treat the Ultra as the shape of the reward rather than a figure two sources agree on.六个人，每人一个 Aniipod Ultra。 一个小队是六人。六个位置满员，每个成员就有一个 Aniipod Ultra —— 但活动期间什么都不会发；而且只有一份清单写明了这个奖励，所以把它当成奖励的样子，而不是两份来源都对得上的数字。
  • The event ends on September 27, 2026, and the rewards go out after it closes, within about a day.活动 2026 年 9 月 27 日结束，奖励在结束后发放，大约一天之内。
  • The two pay differently. A gift code pays you immediately, in the game. A squad code pays a whole group, later, on a website — which is the difference worth remembering before you conclude a code is broken.两种码给的路径不同。 礼品码当场给你，在游戏里；小队码给一整队人，晚一点，在网页上 —— 在断定一个码坏了之前，这个区别值得记住。
