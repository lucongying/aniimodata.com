Flamerion幻焰灵

Fire火 Regen回能

Flamerion has 4 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.幻焰灵 共有 4 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Flamerion adds two cards to Sparki's three: Phantom Barrage, which turns the Fire Debuff already sitting on a target into energy at 3.2 per stack, and a 100-power Phantom Flame Cannon that costs nothing. The Barrage only pays out on stacks that are there in the first place, so this is a fire species carrying one card that works only after its other cards have.幻焰灵在幽幽焰的三张之上加了两张：幻影弹幕，把目标身上已经存在的【火元素异常】按 每层 3.2 点换成能量；以及一发不耗能量的 100 威力 Phantom Flame Cannon。弹幕只在层数已经存在时才兑现 —— 所以这是一只身上有一张「必须等别的卡先打过才生效」的火系。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Blazing Flames灼热火焰

It permanently carries 20 stacks of Fire Debuff on itself, while its two named skills put stacks on the target instead: Trick Fire Ring 4, Fire Orb 3. The 20 is the odd half — it sits on this Aniimo rather than on the enemy, and the official card does not say what it does there, so it is recorded here and left at that.它自身常驻 20 层【火元素异常】，而两个具名技能加的是目标：【诡计火圈】4 层、【火焰弹】3 层。奇怪的是那 20 层 —— 它挂在自己身上而不是敌人身上，而官方卡没说这 20 层在它自己身上起什么作用，所以我们照记，不作解释。
