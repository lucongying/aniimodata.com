Items物品

Aniimo's bag holds 3,599 items and most of them are furniture. This page is the other end of it — the ones that change what you can do. Every Aniipod and what each one actually multiplies, the twelve elements and the two items each one has, the materials that move an Aniimo's numbers after you have caught it, and what every currency in the game is for.伊莫的背包里有三千五百九十九件道具，其中大部分是家具。这一页管的是另一头 ——那些能改变你能做什么的东西：每一个谜立方各自到底加几倍、十二个元素和它们各自对应的两件道具、捕捉之后还能推动伊莫数值的材料，以及每一种货币究竟用来买什么。

Where the 3,599 items actually are三千五百九十九件到底在哪

The game files its items into 53 categories and gives each one of them a rarity colour. Four categories are most of the count, and not one of those four does anything: furniture, home materials, ancient relics and quest props are 1,864 of the 3,599 between them. This page covers the categories that have a mechanic behind them, and it says which those are rather than leaving you to work it out from what is missing.游戏把道具分成 53 个分类，每一件再给一个稀有度颜色。其中四个分类就占了大半个总数，而这四个里没有一个是有用的：家具、家园材料、古遗物、任务道具，加起来 3599 件里的 1864 件。这一页只收背后有机制的分类，而且明说是哪几个，不让你从「少了什么」里自己猜。

Rarity is the other axis and it runs white, green, blue, purple, gold and prismatic — Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Prismatic. It is the colour of the slot the item sits in and not a statement about power: all twelve element stones are Epic, and so are most of the Aniipods, including the one that cannot fail.另一个维度是稀有度，从白、绿、蓝、紫、金到琉璃 —— 白色、绿色、蓝色、紫色、金色、琉璃。它标的是道具格子的颜色，不是强弱的承诺：十二颗元素石全是紫色，谜立方也大多是紫色，包括那个不可能失手的。

Every number in this table is the one printed on the Aniipod itself, in the game's own text. None of it is a community estimate and none of it is derived from our own testing — we have not tested it, and no site that has is telling you how. Five of the thirteen multiply your catch chance. The four at the bottom do not multiply anything, because they cannot fail, and that is a different kind of item.这张表里每个数字都是印在谜立方自己身上的，是游戏的原文。没有一个是社区估计，也没有一个来自我们自己的实测 —— 我们没测过，测过的站也没告诉你它是怎么测的。十三件里有五件会乘算捕捉概率。底下四件不乘任何东西，因为它们不会失败，那是另一种道具。

Two things the tables elsewhere get wrong, and one that matters. Game8's item list prints the Tumbler at 1.5×, and 3× for its nurturing case. The item's own text says 2× and 6× — in the English client and the Chinese one — and 1.5× is the Aniipod Pro, one tier below it. If you wrote the Tumbler off on the strength of that table, it is better than the table says. The second is smaller: the lock-on Aniipod is spelled two ways across the official sources, and either name finds the same item. What actually matters is what Glimmer is contested for — Aniipod Ultra, Capafruit and Psyche Fruit all cost the same currency, and they are the three things a player most wants more of. A stack of Ultra is a stack of training you did not buy.别处的表错了两个地方，其中一个有点要紧。 Game8 的道具表把大白球印成 1.5 倍、滋养时 3 倍。道具自己的文案写的是 2 倍 和 6 倍 —— 英文端中文端都是 ——而 1.5 倍是它下面一档超级立方的数字。你要是照着那张表把大白球划掉了，它比表上说的强。另一个小一些：带锁定那件谜立方的拼法在官方两处来源里不一致，两个名字指向同一件道具。真正要紧的是辉石在争什么 —— 冠军立方、潜能果实、性格果实花的是同一种货币，而这恰好是玩家最想要更多的那三样。囤一摞冠军立方，就是囤了一摞没买的养成材料。

The twelve elements, and the two items each one has十二个元素，和每个元素各自的两件道具

The game keeps these in two separate categories and never puts them side by side, but they are the same twelve elements in the same order. An element stone is what evolves an Aniimo of that element. An element shard is what you nurture with at a Bloom to draw that element in. Every stone and every shard is Epic, so rarity tells you nothing here — only the element does. The first nine rows are the elements the rest of this site documents. The last three are in the game's item data and its Bloom filters and are printed rather than quietly dropped, because a reader who finds a Forge Stone in their bag deserves to find it here too.游戏把它们放在两个分开的分类里，从不并排展示，但它们是同样十二个元素、同样的顺序。元素石用来让该元素的伊莫进化；元素碎晶是你在滋养地脉花时投进去、把该元素引过来的东西。每一颗元素石和每一颗碎晶都是紫色，所以稀有度在这里什么也说明不了 ——只有元素说了算。前九行是本站其余部分覆盖的那九个元素。后三个确实存在于游戏的道具数据和地脉花的筛选条件里，这里照样印出来，不悄悄删掉：在自己的背包里翻出一颗锻钢石的人，有资格在这一页也找到它。

Where they come from. The stones for the nine documented elements drop from Alphas, which is what makes evolution material a by-product of boss hunting rather than of farming. Light Stone is the exception — a level reward instead. The shards are dug out of clay pots, tree stumps and dirt mounds while you explore, or bought outright from the Credit Shop, so they are by far the easier half of the pair to keep in stock. Flux Crystal substitutes for any element stone and Flux Shard for any shard: those two are worth hoarding precisely because they are never the wrong element.获取途径。 九个正式元素的进化石都掉自头目，所以进化材料的本质是打boss的副产品，不是刷出来的。耀光石是例外 —— 它来自等级奖励。碎晶则是探索时开陶罐、树桩、土堆挖到的，或者直接在信用币商店买，所以这一对里它明显是更容易囤的那一半。无定型晶体可替代任意元素石，无定型碎晶可替代任意碎晶：这两样值得囤，正因为它们永远不会是错的那个元素。

Training materials养成材料

These are the items that move an Aniimo's numbers after you have caught it. Two vocabularies are worth keeping apart, because the game uses both and never explains the difference: Innate Potential is the roll the Aniimo was born with and is fixed the moment you catch it, while Acquired Potential is what training adds on top. Star Dust, Star Sand and Starcryst Essence all raise the acquired side. Almost nothing raises the innate side, and the two things that do are at the top of this table.这些是捕捉之后还能推动伊莫数值的道具。有两套说法值得分清楚，因为游戏两套都在用、却从不解释区别：先天资质是伊莫出生时就定下的，捕捉那一刻就固定了；后天资质是养成分加上去的。星之尘、星之砂、星之晶粹提升的都是后天那一侧。能动先天那一侧的几乎没有，能动的那两件在这一页最上面。

Currency, and which one you are short of货币，以及你到底缺哪一种

Thirty-three currencies is a lot for one game, and the useful thing to know about them is that they are not interchangeable — most are locked to a single mode and buy nothing outside it. So this table says what each one buys rather than what it is described as, because that is the question actually being asked when you are standing in front of a vendor.一款游戏有三十三种货币是有点多，而真正值得知道的是它们不能互换 ——大多数锁死在单一玩法里，出了那个玩法什么也买不到。所以这张表写的是每一种能买什么，而不是它的描述文字，因为你站在商人面前时问的就是前一个问题。

The three that are actually scarce. Most of the list above is mode-locked and solves itself — you earn it where you spend it. Three are not: Credits and Glimmer are spent by systems that have nothing to do with each other, and Primegy is a rate rather than a balance, so it is the only one where not spending it is a loss. If you are deciding what to protect, those are the three.真正稀缺的只有三种。 上面大半张表都锁在单一玩法里，自己会解决 —— 在哪儿赚的就在哪儿花。有三种不是：信用币和辉石被彼此毫不相干的系统同时消耗，而源能是速率不是余额，是唯一一个「不花就是亏」的。要决定先保哪个的话，就是这三个。
