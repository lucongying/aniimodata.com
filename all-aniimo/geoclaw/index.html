Geoclaw晶壳蟹

Ice冰 Break击破 A

Geoclaw has a single form recorded so far. Its stats, type, habitat and evolution requirement are below.晶壳蟹 目前只记录到一个形态。它的数值、属性、栖息地和进化条件都在下面。

Overview简介

Geoclaw is the ice ending of the Budclaw fork: four ice cards plus a 173-power Freezing Claws finisher at no energy cost. Every card in the kit is ice, with no second element anywhere — which makes it the most committed single-element species in its family, and the one most easily walled out by a resistance.晶壳蟹是花芽蟹分叉里偏冰的那一端：四张冰系卡，加上一发不耗能量的 173 威力冰霜爪收尾。技能组里每一张卡都是冰，没有第二个属性 —— 这让它是这个家族里对单一属性最执着的一只，也是最容易被抗性挡住的一只。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Tunnel钻地

Goes under the ground, which makes it immune to all ranged attacks, at a stamina cost per second. The hole in that is written on the card: a pound-type skill digs it straight back out and throws it into the air. Against a melee enemy this is close to free; against anything with a ground slam it is a way to be lifted off your feet.钻到地下，免疫所有远程攻击，代价是按秒扣体力。破绽就写在卡上：震地类技能会把它从地里掀出来、打到空中。对近战敌人几乎等于白拿；对会砸地的对手，这是主动把破绽递过去。

Trait特性

Frostbite Sunder霜蚀破甲

Its basic attacks lay down Ice Debuff, and against a target past 5 stacks it deals 35% extra BREAK. Two clauses that only mean something together: the first is the supply and the second is what the supply is for. This species stacks its own resource instead of waiting on a teammate to apply it.它的普攻会挂【冰元素异常】，而对超过 5 层的目标额外造成 35% 击破。两句只有合起来才有意义 —— 前一句是供给，后一句说明供给用来干什么。这个物种自己就能把层数叠上去，不用等队友来挂。

Skills技能
