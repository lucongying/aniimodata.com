Beginner新手入门

5 steps, in order5 步，按顺序

The opening hours: what actually happens, the one choice you cannot take back, and what not to spend yet.开局这几个小时：实际会发生什么、哪个选择收不回来，以及现在还不该花什么。

  1. 01

    You are given an Emberpup — you do not choose it第一只直接发到你手里

    In An Idyllic Trip you make your character and name it, then Nicole points you at an Emberpup to catch. It is the Aniimo you are handed, not one of a set you pick from; the quest then has you use it to burn the branches across the path. Your character's gender is decided here and cannot be changed later.「An Idyllic Trip」里你先捏脸起名。之后 Nicole 会指着一只 Emberpup 让你抓，没有别的选项。接下来你要用它烧掉挡路的树枝。角色的性别在这里就定死了，之后改不了。

  2. 02

    Three catches, marked for you三次有标记的捕捉

    Aniimo Catching Journey asks for three and marks them on your map: Celestis, Hummin and Skippy.「Aniimo Catching Journey」要你抓三只，地图上会给标记：Celestis、Hummin 和 Skippy。

  3. 03

    The sun-and-moon mural日月壁画

    The Mysterious Cavern takes you to a mural of a sun and a moon Aniimo, and asks you to pick a guardian: Helion or Lunara. It is the only real decision in the opening, and the next section is about it, because you cannot undo it.「The Mysterious Cavern」会把你带到一幅日月 Aniimo 壁画前，让你选一位守护者：Helion 还是 Lunara。开局只有这一个选择收不回来，下一节专门讲它。

  4. 04

    You join the Institute either way不管怎么选都得进研究所

    Institution Initiation puts you in the Polaris Institute whatever you answer — the story does it for you. The Prismana Pact sign-up screen appears here too, but this is not where you claim it.「Institution Initiation」里你无论怎么回答都会加入 Polaris Institute，剧情替你决定了。Prismana 契约的登记界面也在这里出现，但领取不在这儿。

  5. 05

    Where the tutorial actually ends新手期真正结束的地方

    At the end of Chasing the Clouds you fight Alpha Turbo, then report to Dr. Lewis. That one conversation unlocks Elite Training and promotes you to Student I — after this the game stops holding your hand.「Chasing the Clouds」最后你会打 Alpha Turbo，然后回去找 Dr. Lewis 复命。这次对话解锁 精英训练，并把你晋升为 Student I。从这里开始，游戏不再手把手带你。

Party队伍

Team Building队伍搭配

5 roles into 4 slots5 个定位，4 个位置

The game sorts every creature into a role, and only some of them fit in a party at once. This is that arithmetic, and what each role brings.游戏会把每只生物归进一个定位，而一队装不下所有定位。这一页讲这套算术，以及每个定位实际带来什么。

DPS输出

The only role that leads on ATK, and the widest bucket in the index at 29 species. It is also the lowest for BREAK and the lowest for both defences: the ATK ceiling of 130 is the highest of any role and the HP ceiling of 104 is the lowest. That is what the label buys and what it costs, not a verdict on how long a DPS lasts in a fight.唯一在 ATK 上领跑的定位，也是全图鉴最大的一档，29 个物种。同时它是 BREAK 最低的一档，也是两个防御都最低的一档：ATK 上限 130 是全定位最高，生命上限 104 是全定位最低。这就是这个标签买到的东西和付出的代价，而不是「输出在场上能活多久」的判决。

Break击破

Where the BREAK stat lives. The mean is 94.1 against 48.7 for DPS, and only DPS is a bigger bucket. It is also the role with the lowest ATK ceiling — 100, the only one that does not reach 110 — and the widest P.DEF spread in the index, 52 to 113.BREAK 这项数值住的地方。 均值 94.1，输出是 48.7；只有输出这一档比它更大。它也是 ATK 上限最低的定位 —— 100，唯一一个够不到 110 的 —— 并且物防极差是全图鉴最宽的，52 到 113。

Support辅助

Not a low-damage bucket, which is the thing the label hides. Its ATK averages 103.9 and its ceiling of 120 is the highest in the index after DPS's; what separates the two is the floor, 68 against 88. Support is also the widest bucket by HP, 70 to 113, which is what a label covering both buffers and off-healers should look like.不是一档低伤害的定位，而这正是标签藏起来的那件事。它的 ATK 均值 103.9，上限 120 仅次于输出；把两者分开的是下限，68 对 88。辅助也是生命极差最宽的一档，70 到 113 —— 一个既装增益手又装半个奶妈的标签，本来就该长这样。

Regen回能

REGEN, by name and by number. A mean of 107.9 is the highest of the five, and the bucket's floor of 93 sits above every other bucket's mean. It is the second-smallest bucket at seven species.REGEN，名字和数字对得上。 均值 107.9 是五档里最高的，而且这一档的下限 93 高于其余任何一档的均值。它是第二小的一档，七个物种。

Heal治疗

Five creatures, and the toughest of them on paper. Heal has the highest HP mean (102.6) and the highest M.DEF mean (87.4), and it is the only role whose ATK ceiling stops below 100. Five of 86 is the scarcest label by a wide margin — the next smallest is Regen at seven — and that scarcity is the practical form of the four-and-five problem: the role you are most likely to be missing is the one the index carries fewest of.五只生物，而且是纸面上最硬的一档。 治疗的生命均值最高（102.6）、魔防均值最高（87.4），也是唯一一个 ATK 上限低于 100 的定位。86 分之 5 是最稀缺的标签，差得很远 —— 下一档是回能的 7 只 —— 而这种稀缺就是「五定位四位置」的实际形态：你最容易缺的那种定位，恰好就是图鉴收得最少的那种。

01Is Aniimo free to play?Aniimo 免费吗？

Yes. It is free to download and free to play on every platform it ships on, and there is no subscription. What it sells is cosmetics, convenience and a handbook, and the two specific things money does gate are named in the “What it costs” section.免费。每一个上线的平台都能免费下载、免费游玩，没有订阅制。它卖的是外观、便利道具和一本手册 —— 钱能卡住的那两件事，在「它花多少钱」一节里点名。

05Is Aniimo on Mac?Aniimo 有 Mac 版吗？

Not yet. The Steam build is Windows-only, and Pawprint has said a Mac version is planned for later with no date attached. Nothing has been said about Linux at all.还没有。Steam 版本只支持 Windows，官方说过 Mac 版计划「稍后推出」，但没给日期。Linux 则一个字都没提过。

06Do I have to be online to play?玩 Aniimo 必须联网吗？

Yes. There is no offline mode — the PlayStation listing states online play is required, and Steam lists a broadband connection under both the minimum and the recommended specifications.必须。没有离线模式 —— PlayStation 商店页面写明需要联网游玩，Steam 在最低配置和推荐配置两栏里都列了宽带连接。

08Does Aniimo have crossplay and cross-save?Aniimo 支持跨平台联机和跨平台存档吗？

Crossplay, yes. Steam lists cross-platform multiplayer, and the Xbox listing advertises online co-op for two to four players with cross-platform play. Cross-save, not confirmed — we found no statement from Pawprint that progress carries between platforms, so do not assume it does before you buy a second copy.跨平台联机：支持。 Steam 列了 cross-platform multiplayer，Xbox 页面写明 2 到 4 人在线合作并支持跨平台。跨平台存档：没有官方确认 —— 我们没找到官方说过进度能在平台之间带过去，所以在买第二份之前别当成已经支持。

09What can you buy in Aniimo?Aniimo 里能买什么？

Four things: Lumin Crystals, the paid currency, sold from $0.99 to $99.99; the Monthly Pass at $4.99; the Advanced Companion Handbook at $9.99; and cosmetics. The crystals also buy catch-rate items and the rotating shop stock, which is where most of the spending ends up.四样：Lumin Crystals（付费货币，0.99 到 99.99 美元）、Monthly Pass（4.99 美元）、Advanced Companion Handbook（9.99 美元），还有外观。结晶还能买捕捉率道具和轮换商店的货，大部分钱最后花在这里。

10Does Aniimo have gacha or loot boxes?Aniimo 有抽卡或开箱吗？

No creature gacha. The executive producer, Jovi Zhang, has said the game was designed without one, so you catch what you want rather than rolling for it. Steam does link a Probability Details page — it looks like gacha odds and is not: it is the catch-rate and Sparkling maths, and Pawprint publishes the whole thing at aniimo.com/formula-multipliers.没有精灵抽卡。执行制作人 Jovi Zhang 说过这游戏在设计上就不做抽卡 —— 想要哪只自己去抓，而不是靠抽。Steam 上确实挂了一个 Probability Details 页面，看着像抽卡概率，其实不是：那是捕捉率和 Sparkling 的算式，官方把整份数据都公开在 aniimo.com/formula-multipliers。

Systems系统

The four mechanics that trip up new players.最容易把新玩家绊住的四个机制。

Prismana

Recoloured, sometimes re-typed, and able to carry an exclusive skill. Stats stay the same — what changes is the move pool. Two ways to get one:

  • Prismana Flows — regional weather events. The map forecasts them per region, so camp the forecast.
  • Nurture — raise an area's Prismatic Energy. It climbs through six states: Empty, Very Low, Slight, Abundant, Very High, Full. A Prismana is guaranteed to spawn at Full.

Nurture needs that area's Bronze Badge first.

The Prismana Pact is signed early but claimed at Wayfarer I, which wants level 40, and that claim is the free one. Rule of thumb: do not spend it on something you can already catch in an early area.

换色、有时还会换属性，并且能带一个专属技能。数值不变，变的只是技能池。两种获取途径：

  • Prismana Flows 是地区天气事件。地图会按地区预报，盯着预报蹲点。
  • Nurture 是把地区的 Prismatic Energy 推上去。它会经过六个状态：Empty、Very Low、Slight、Abundant、Very High、Full。到 Full 时 Prismana 是必出的。

Nurture 需要先拿到该地区的 青铜徽章。

Prismana 契约很早就登记，但要到 Wayfarer I、也就是 40 级才能领，而这一领是免费的。经验法则：别花在一个你早期地区就能抓到的身上。

Aniipods

Eight types. Standard 1x, Pro 1.5x; Mega, Hyper and Track all 2x. Ultra never fails and its catch is at least Elite Potential; Sparkling Cube never fails, always rolls Perfect Potential, and is always Sparkling.

Pro Catcher unlocks at Student I, Master Catcher at Student III.

八种球。Standard 1x、Pro 1.5x；Mega、Hyper 和 Track 都是 2x。Ultra 不失败，且捕获的至少是 Elite Potential；Sparkling Cube 不失败，必出 Perfect Potential，且必定 Sparkling。

Pro Catcher 在 Student I 解锁，Master Catcher 在 Student III。

Primegy

The stamina currency. One point every six minutes, cap 300. Spent on evolution materials, Resonance Star-Ups, Held Items and Capability Awakening materials.

Spend it every day. It has no other use, and regeneration stops once you hit the cap.

体力货币。每六分钟回一点，上限 300。花在进化材料、Resonance 升星、携带道具和 Capability Awakening 材料上。

每天都花掉。它没有别的用途，而且一旦到上限就不再回复了。

Homeland家园

Unlock route: push the main story to Sea of Flowers → finish Wally's quest and report back to Dr. Lewis → reach Student Level II → track Own Your Homeland in the Elite Trainer tab → find Fannie.

Element roles: Fire (cooking, smelting), Grass (farming), Water (brewing, watering), Rock (tilling, mining), Electric (power), Ice (cooling), Wind (processing), Dark (harvesting, chopping, curing), Holy (lighting).

要先到 Sea of Flowers，做完 Wally 的任务回去找 Dr. Lewis 复命，等级到 Student Level II，再从 Elite Trainer 页签里追踪 Own Your Homeland，最后找到 Fannie。

各属性在家园里干什么：火负责烹饪和冶炼，草负责种植，水负责酿造和浇水，土负责耕地和采矿，电负责供电，冰负责冷藏，风负责加工，暗负责收获、砍伐和腌制，光负责照明。
