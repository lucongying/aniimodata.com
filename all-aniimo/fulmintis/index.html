Fulmintis电刺螳螂

Electric电 DPS输出 S

Fulmintis has 2 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.电刺螳螂 共有 2 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Fulmintis is the lightning ending of the Wisptis line and the half that turns the family's crit plan on: Lightning Blade Impact enters Aim Mode to fire two shock waves and paralyzes for 6 seconds on a critical hit, while a Lightning Surge support carries the flat +35% skill crit rate that makes that condition reliable. It is a dark species' descendant that changed element completely, and the whole kit is built around one condition.电刺螳螂是幽爪镰那条线的雷系终点，也是把这一家子的暴击思路打开的那一半：闪电刃冲击进入瞄准模式发射两道冲击波，并且暴击时麻痹 6 秒；而雷电涌动这张辅助卡提供固定的 +35% 技能暴击率，让那个条件变得可靠。它是一个暗系物种的后代、彻底换了属性，而整套技能都围绕着一个条件搭建。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Cloak隐身

Turns invisible. The official card says nothing beyond that — no duration, no cost, and no word on whether attacking drops it — so treat what you can do inside the state as untested rather than unlimited.进入隐身状态。官方卡上没有别的信息 —— 没写持续时间、没写消耗、也没写攻击会不会解除 —— 所以状态里能做什么，按「没验证过」看，别按「随便做」看。

Trait特性

Electro Stash电力积蓄

A skill that lands a critical hit gives back 4 energy. It is a small figure, but it is the only thing on this species' sheet turning critical rate into energy, so a high-crit build gets to collect it often enough for it to count.技能打出暴击时回复 4 点能量。数字不大，但这是这个物种整张表上唯一把暴击率换成能量的东西 —— 暴击堆得够高的配装里，这 4 点来得足够频繁，就有意义了。

Skills技能

Split the way the official page splits them: Combat holds the skills you cast, Innate the basic attack. The list is the species', not the form's — a form does not change it.分法和官方页面一致：战斗技能是你会主动放的那几个，固有技能里是基础攻击。这份表属于物种，不属于形态 —— 换形态不会换掉它。

    Dash in, then keep slashing for as long as energy holds, ending on a Spinning Slash worth 26 Power when the energy runs out or the button is pressed again. Every 3 slashes accumulated in the run doubles that finisher. So the energy bar is the length of this skill — and a full bar does not merely buy more slashes, it buys the multiplier stacking up behind the finisher.冲刺接近目标后，只要还有能量就持续连斩；能量耗尽或再次点击时，以一发 26 威力的回旋斩收尾。每累计 3 段连斩，这发收尾伤害翻倍。所以能量条就是这个技能的长度 —— 而一条满能量买到的不只是「多砍几刀」，而是收尾背后越叠越高的那个倍率。
