Bolty闪电貂

Electric电 Break击破

Bolty has 2 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.闪电貂 共有 2 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Bolty is a three-card lightning BREAK species: a 48-power dash, a 20-power tail, and the basic attack. Its only stage-3 ending is Blazen, which keeps both the element and the role — so this is one of the lines in the index where what you are building toward is visible from the very first card.闪电貂是一个三张卡的雷系击破物种：一次 48 威力的突进、一记 20 威力的尾击，加上基础攻击。它唯一的三阶终点是雷光貂，元素和定位都保留 —— 所以这是图鉴里「从第一张卡就能看出你在往哪练」的几条线之一。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

High Jump高跳

A taller jump than the normal one and nothing else — no state to hold, no stamina spent. It is the plainest exploration skill on the site: one press, one higher arc.就是比平常跳得更高，没有别的附加：不耗体力，也不用维持状态。这是全站最朴素的探索技能 —— 按一下，多跳一截。

Trait特性

Power Sustain电力续航

After its skills land 6 critical hits, it goes Overcharged for 6 seconds: an immediate shield worth 5% HP, and every skill costs 12 less energy. The catch is on the same card — no Ultimate charge can be gained for the duration. Six seconds of cheaper skills bought with six seconds of not building toward the Ultimate, which is a trade worth making on purpose or not at all.技能累计打出 6 次暴击后进入【激电状态】，持续 6 秒：立刻获得相当于 5% 生命值的护盾，所有技能少花 12 点能量。代价就写在同一张卡上 —— 这段时间内无法获得终结值。用 6 秒不攒终结技换 6 秒更便宜的技能，这笔账要么有意去算，要么就别算。

Skills技能
