Stellarys星魔师

Dark暗 DPS输出 A

Stellarys has 3 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.星魔师 共有 3 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Stellarys is Celestis with a full kit bolted on: three separate Glides in dark, ice and water, a Starfall that stacks its own damage bonus the more its stars connect, and a 166-power Comet Aureus that costs nothing. It shares the Grand Sorcerer trait with its own stage-1 form, and the glides are the point — the same 74-power attack wearing three elements, so there is always a version a given target is not ready for.星魔师就是在星乐蒂身上装了一整套技能：暗、冰、水三种各一的滑翔冲击，一发星星命中越多、自己增伤越高的星坠，以及一发不耗能量的 166 威力 Aureus 彗星。它和自己的第一阶共用魔法大师这个特性，而重点在那三个滑翔 —— 同一发 74 威力、换了三种属性，所以总有一个版本是目标没准备好的。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Grand Sorcerer魔法大师

For 5 seconds after it casts anything, its basic attacks deal an extra 6 Power on hit. The window is short and the figure is flat — it does not scale with ATK — so what this rewards is staying in the fight after a skill rather than firing one off and backing out.释放技能后的 5 秒内，普攻命中会额外造成一次 6 威力的伤害。窗口短，数字也是定值 —— 不随攻击成长 —— 所以它奖励的是技能之后继续贴着打，而不是放完就撤。

Skills技能

Split the way the official page splits them: Combat holds the skills you cast, Innate the basic attack. The list is the species', not the form's — a form does not change it.分法和官方页面一致：战斗技能是你会主动放的那几个，固有技能里是基础攻击。这份表属于物种，不属于形态 —— 换形态不会换掉它。

That is two escape routes on one card — the retreat and the swap — and the swap only works while you have not run far, since the clone is destroyed past 20m.立刻解控、向后拉开距离，同时在原地留下一个持续 20 秒的分身。分身无法被攻击，会模仿本体的普攻和技能，造成本体 20% 的伤害；再次释放技能，本体会和分身交换位置。一张卡上给了两条退路 —— 后撤和换位；而换位只有在你没跑远的时候才用得上，因为距离超过 20 米分身就销毁了。
