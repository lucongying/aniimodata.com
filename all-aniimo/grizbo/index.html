Grizbo暴睡熊

Rock土 DPS输出 S

Grizbo has 2 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.暴睡熊 共有 2 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Grizbo is the line's last stage and the species with the largest single number in the index: Rock Smash at 260 power for no energy at all. Around it sit a dark claw for coverage, a Critical Hit that converts its own Claw Mark stacks at 5% Power each, and a 30-energy 90-power attack. The free 260 is the reason to field the species; everything else is there to make it land harder.暴睡熊是这条线的最后一阶，也是图鉴里单发数字最大的物种：碎岩重击，260 威力，完全不耗能量。围着它的是一张补属性的暗影爪、一张把自己身上的【爪印】按每层 5% 威力换成伤害的会心一击，以及一发 30 能量换 90 威力的攻击。那发免费的 260 才是值得上场的理由；别的都只是让它打得更狠。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Head Hammer头撞

Drives its head down and throws out a Shock Wave from the impact. Where the other exploration skills move you, this one puts something on the ground in front of you.把头砸向地面，在落点激起一道冲击波。别的探索技能挪的是你自己，这个是在你面前的地上留下一道波。

Trait特性

Raging Rampage怒气狂暴

Rage builds from three sources: each basic attack 2, every 10 energy spent on skills 10, and each hit taken 2. At 100 it enters Enraged for 20 seconds — +30% attack speed and skill damage, +25% energy recovery — and stops gaining Rage while it lasts. Note which source is richest: 10 energy spent is worth as much as five basic attacks, which is what makes this a trait about casting rather than about swinging.【怒气值】有三个来源：每次普攻 2 点，技能每消耗 10 点能量 10 点，每次挨打 2 点。达到 100 点进入【狂暴状态】，持续 20 秒：攻速与技能伤害 +30%、回能速度 +25%，期间不再累积怒气。注意哪个来源最肥：花 10 点能量抵得上 5 次普攻 —— 所以这是一个偏向「放技能」而不是「平A」的特性。

Skills技能
