Iris飘飘花

Grass草 DPS输出 A

Iris has 7 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.飘飘花 共有 7 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Iris is a grass attacker with an unusual shape: Floral Storm is a channel you hold and stop by hand, ramping from one petal to three as it runs, and Vortex of Petals is a 60-power hit for 20 energy. Two skills and a basic attack is the entire species, so the choice it presents is simply whether to keep the storm going or spend on the vortex.飘飘花是一个形状特别的草系输出：花舞风暴是一条按住、也可以手动中断的引导技能，花瓣会随着时间从一朵涨到三朵；而花瓣漩涡是一发 20 能量换 60 威力的攻击。两个技能加一个基础攻击就是它的全部，所以它给出的选择无非是：让风暴继续转，还是把能量花在漩涡上。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

At a glance一眼速览

Mobility探索技能

Blooming开花

Leaves flowers on the ground behind it as it dances. The official card ties them to no speed, cost or effect, so as far as the game states anything, this is the exploration skill that is only itself.跳舞时脚下会留下花朵。官方卡没有把这件事和速度、消耗或任何效果挂钩 —— 就游戏给出的说法而言，这是唯一一个只是它自己的探索技能。

Trait特性

Disco Aniimo舞力四射

Two separate things on one card. Standing on grass gives +30% Damage Amp. In battle, every skill that lands a hit adds 1 Dance Power — hitting several targets still counts once — and at 24 Dance Power the next skill is free. Twenty-four hits is a long climb for a single free cast, so treat the grass bonus as the part you can rely on and the free skill as the extra.一张卡上是两件互不相干的事。站在草地上，伤害加深 +30%。进入战斗后，技能每命中一次得 1 点【舞力】—— 命中多个目标也只算一次 —— 累积 24 点后，下一次技能不消耗能量。24 次命中换一次免消耗，攒得相当久：草地那 30% 是你能指望的部分，免消耗算额外的。

Skills技能

Split the way the official page splits them: Combat holds the skills you cast, Innate the basic attack. The list is the species', not the form's — a form does not change it.分法和官方页面一致：战斗技能是你会主动放的那几个，固有技能里是基础攻击。这份表属于物种，不属于形态 —— 换形态不会换掉它。

    Petals that track targets while the caster keeps moving, with one more petal added over time up to 3 at once, and a second press to stop early. The ramp and the stop switch belong together: the skill is worth holding when a fight is going to last, and the manual stop exists so you are not left spinning when it is not.一边移动一边释放追踪目标的魔法花叶，花叶数量会随时间增加，最多一次 3 朵，而再按一次可以提前中断。渐增和中断是一对：这仗要打一会儿的时候，它值得一直开着；而手动中断的存在，是为了让你在不需要的时候不用干转。
