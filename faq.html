Platform, price and getting in平台、价格与入门

What to settle before you download anything.下载之前先把这几件事定下来。

01Is Aniimo free to play?Aniimo 免费吗？

Yes. It is free to download and free to play on every platform it ships on, and there is no subscription. What it sells is cosmetics, convenience and a handbook, and the two specific things money does gate are named in the “What it costs” section.免费。每一个上线的平台都能免费下载、免费游玩，没有订阅制。它卖的是外观、便利道具和一本手册 —— 钱能卡住的那两件事，在「它花多少钱」一节里点名。

02What platforms is Aniimo on?Aniimo 有哪些平台？

PC, through Steam, the Epic Games Store and Pawprint's own launcher. PlayStation 5. Xbox Series X|S, including Xbox Play Anywhere and Xbox Cloud Gaming. And iOS and Android.PC（Steam、Epic 游戏商城，以及 Pawprint 自家的启动器）、PlayStation 5、Xbox Series X|S（含 Xbox Play Anywhere 与 Xbox 云游戏），以及 iOS 和 Android。

03When did Aniimo come out?Aniimo 什么时候上线的？

PC and consoles on 16 September 2026, mobile on 23 September 2026. Steam shows 15 September in some regions — that is the same moment in a different timezone, not a second release.PC 和主机是 2026 年 9 月 16 日，手机端是 9 月 23 日。Steam 在部分地区显示 9 月 15 日 —— 那是同一时刻落在不同时区，不是第二个发售日。

04Is Aniimo coming to Nintendo Switch?Aniimo 会上 Switch 吗？

Not as far as anyone has said. No Switch version has been announced and Pawprint has never mentioned one — Switch is not among the platforms the company has confirmed.没有这个说法。官方从未宣布过 Switch 版本，也从未提过 —— 它不在官方确认过的平台名单里。

05Is Aniimo on Mac?Aniimo 有 Mac 版吗？

Not yet. The Steam build is Windows-only, and Pawprint has said a Mac version is planned for later with no date attached. Nothing has been said about Linux at all.还没有。Steam 版本只支持 Windows，官方说过 Mac 版计划「稍后推出」，但没给日期。Linux 则一个字都没提过。

06Do I have to be online to play?玩 Aniimo 必须联网吗？

Yes. There is no offline mode — the PlayStation listing states online play is required, and Steam lists a broadband connection under both the minimum and the recommended specifications.必须。没有离线模式 —— PlayStation 商店页面写明需要联网游玩，Steam 在最低配置和推荐配置两栏里都列了宽带连接。

07Can I play Aniimo on Steam Deck?Aniimo 在 Steam Deck 上能玩吗？

It runs, though Valve has not rated it. Steam's own badge reads Unknown; ProtonDB, where players report their own results, has it at Platinum across 113 reports, and it wants Proton 10 or newer. Pawprint has said official support is planned for November.能跑，但 Valve 没给它评级。Steam 自己的标识是 Unknown；ProtonDB 上玩家自报的结果是 Platinum（113 份报告），需要 Proton 10 或更新版本。官方说过完整支持计划在 11 月。

08Does Aniimo have crossplay and cross-save?Aniimo 支持跨平台联机和跨平台存档吗？

Both, and cross-save is now confirmed. Steam lists cross-platform multiplayer, and the Xbox listing advertises online co-op for two to four players with cross-platform play. On top of that, Pawprint's own mobile launch notes describe full cross-progression: log in with the same account on the same server and your progress, resources and party carry across PC, console and mobile. The data is per-server, so the same account on a different server is a different save.两个都支持，而且跨平台存档现在已确认。 Steam 列了 cross-platform multiplayer，Xbox 页面写明 2 到 4 人在线合作并支持跨平台。除此之外，官方自己的手机端上线说明写明支持完整跨平台进度：在同一个服务器上用同一个账号登录，进度、资源和队伍就能在 PC、主机、手机之间带过去。数据是按服务器分的，同一账号登到别的服务器就是另一份存档。

09How big is the download, and what do you need to run it?下载多大，要什么配置才能跑？

About 40 GB on PC, though some pages quote 45, and an SSD is recommended. PlayStation 5 and Xbox are lighter, around 30–35 GB, and the mobile build is about 3.5 GB. The floor on PC is Windows 10 (64-bit), an i7-4770 or Ryzen 7 2700, 8 GB of RAM and a GTX 970 or RX 5600; the comfortable build is an i7-10700 or Ryzen 5 5600 with 16 GB and an RTX 3060 or RX 6700.PC 上大约 40 GB，也有页面写 45，并且建议装在 SSD 上。PS5 和 Xbox 轻一些，约 30–35 GB，手机端约 3.5 GB。PC 的最低线是 Windows 10（64 位）、i7-4770 或 Ryzen 7 2700、8 GB 内存、GTX 970 或 RX 5600；舒服的配置是 i7-10700 或 Ryzen 5 5600、16 GB 内存、RTX 3060 或 RX 6700。

What it costs它花多少钱

The store, the pass, and the one gate we could actually find.商店、通行证，以及我们唯一能找到的那道门槛。

10What can you buy in Aniimo?Aniimo 里能买什么？

Four things: Lumin Crystals, the paid currency, sold from $0.99 to $99.99; the Monthly Pass at $4.99; the Advanced Companion Handbook at $9.99; and cosmetics. The crystals also buy catch-rate items and the rotating shop stock, which is where most of the spending ends up.四样：Lumin Crystals（付费货币，0.99 到 99.99 美元）、Monthly Pass（4.99 美元）、Advanced Companion Handbook（9.99 美元），还有外观。结晶还能买捕捉率道具和轮换商店的货，大部分钱最后花在这里。

11Does Aniimo have gacha or loot boxes?Aniimo 有抽卡或开箱吗？

No creature gacha. The executive producer, Jovi Zhang, has said the game was designed without one, so you catch what you want rather than rolling for it. Steam does link a Probability Details page — it looks like gacha odds and is not: it is the catch-rate and Sparkling maths, and Pawprint publishes the whole thing at aniimo.com/formula-multipliers.没有精灵抽卡。执行制作人 Jovi Zhang 说过这游戏在设计上就不做抽卡 —— 想要哪只自己去抓，而不是靠抽。Steam 上确实挂了一个 Probability Details 页面，看着像抽卡概率，其实不是：那是捕捉率和 Sparkling 的算式，官方把整份数据都公开在 aniimo.com/formula-multipliers。

12Is Aniimo pay to win?Aniimo 是氪金变强吗？

Broadly no, with one exception worth knowing. We found nothing on sale that simply makes a fight easier, and the one Aniimo people call paywalled — Fulmintis — now has a free route: the free track of the Season 1 handbook hands out an egg that hatches one. What is still paid-only is the evolution material behind it, so the free egg gets you a Fulmintis but not a way to evolve a second Wisptis into one. Power is not sold; one evolution line is gated.大体上不是，只有一个例外值得知道。我们没找到任何直接让战斗变简单的付费品，而被大家说成「付费墙」的那只 —— Fulmintis —— 现在有免费途径：第一赛季手册的免费线会发一颗孵化出它的蛋。真正仍然只付费的是它背后的进化材料：免费蛋能给你一只 Fulmintis，但不给你把第二只 Wisptis 进化成它的办法。强度不卖；有一条进化线是收费门槛。

13Is there a monthly pass or a battle pass?有月卡或战斗通行证吗？

Both, and they are nearly the same thing under two names. The Monthly Pass is $4.99 and works the way passes usually do — daily and weekly tasks for currency and items across a season. The Season 1 Companion Handbook has a free track everyone gets and an Advanced track, which is the $9.99 purchase.两个都有，基本是同一个东西的两个名字。Monthly Pass 4.99 美元，形态和常见的通行证一样 —— 一个赛季内用每日和每周任务换货币和道具。第一赛季的 Companion Handbook 有一条所有人都有免费线，以及需要 9.99 美元购买的 Advanced 线。

14Is any Aniimo locked behind a paywall?有 Aniimo 被锁在付费墙后面吗？

The creature is not — the material is. The free track of the Season 1 handbook gives an egg that hatches a Fulmintis, so you can own one without paying. Evolving Wisptis into a second one needs the Soul of Electric Shock, which only comes from the paid Advanced track, and the Advanced track also carries a branching-evolution material. So every creature is reachable by playing; a couple of specific evolutions are not.被卡住的是材料，不是精灵本身。第一赛季手册的免费线会给一颗孵化出 Fulmintis 的蛋，所以不花钱也能拥有一只。想自己把 Wisptis 进化出第二只，需要Soul of Electric Shock，而它只从付费的 Advanced 线出；Advanced 线里还带一份分支进化材料。所以每一只精灵都能靠玩拿到；有几种具体的进化路线不能。

15How do you get more Aniimo Storage?Aniimo Storage 容量怎么扩？

Buy the pass. The Advanced Companion Handbook adds 5 Aniimo Storage slots, and the higher Keepsakes tier adds 10. That is the only route we could find — there is no free or in-game way to raise the cap yet, which is worth knowing before you start hoarding.买通行证。Advanced Companion Handbook 加 5 个 Aniimo 储物位，更高一档的 Keepsakes 加 10 个。这是我们能找到的唯一途径 —— 目前没有任何免费或游戏内的扩容方式，开始囤东西之前值得先知道这一点。

Catching捕捉

The pods, the odds, and why the throw failed.球、概率，以及那一下为什么会失败。

16How do you catch an Aniimo?怎么抓 Aniimo？

Weaken it and throw an Aniipod, or skip the fight entirely by approaching from behind — that works outside combat and is the fastest way to fill a collection. The throw is rolled against a formula the studio publishes: the area's base chance, multiplied by your pod, the level gap and whatever status the target is under.先打弱，再扔 Aniipod；或者干脆绕到背后偷袭，跳过整场战斗 —— 这招在战斗外有效，也是集图鉴最快的路子。命中与否按官方公开的算式结算：地区基础概率，乘上你用的球、等级差，以及目标身上的状态。

17Which Aniipod should I use?该用哪种 Aniipod？

Match the pod to the job. Aniipod is the baseline at ×1, Pro is ×1.5, Mega is ×2, and Track and Hyper are ×2 with extras — homing, or a faster throw. Tumbler is ×2 normally but ×6 on Wild Surge targets, and it catches more than one at a time, which is what it exists for. Aniipod Ultra and the Sparkling Cube are guaranteed catches, so save them for something you cannot afford to miss.按用途选。Aniipod 是基准 ×1，Pro ×1.5，Mega ×2，Track 和 Hyper 是 ×2 附带额外功能 —— 追踪，或者投掷更快。Tumbler 平时 ×2，但在 Wild Surge 目标上是 ×6，而且能一次抓多只，它存在的意义就是这个。Aniipod Ultra 和 Sparkling Cube 是必中，留给输不起的那只。

18Does a back attack improve the catch rate?背袭能提高捕捉率吗？

Yes — ×1.5, only outside combat, and only with a standard Aniipod. Tumblers do not get it. Guides that describe this as a 150% bonus are reading the same number wrong: it is half again as likely, not two and a half times.能，×1.5，只在战斗外有效，而且只对普通 Aniipod 生效 —— Tumbler 吃不到这个加成。有些攻略把这写成「提升 150%」，是把同一个数字读错了：是变成一点五倍，不是变成两点五倍。

19Why does catching keep failing?为什么老是抓不住？

Four things decide it. Level: up to ten levels above you costs nothing, then it falls off a cliff — fifteen or more above you is ×0.1, and being overlevelled earns no bonus at all. HP, in combat: full health is ×0.8 and the curve only really bites below 10%, so chipping a bar down is worth less than it looks. Status: sleep, stun, lure and eating are ×2, deep sleep is ×100, and Break is ×1.5. Stacking: statuses do not stack — if any active one is below ×1 the game uses the lowest, so a sleeping Aniimo that has noticed you is worth ×0.9, not ×2.四件事决定结果。等级：高出你 10 级以内没有惩罚，之后就断崖式下跌 —— 高出 15 级以上是 ×0.1，而且等级压过对方没有任何加成。血量（战斗中）：满血是 ×0.8，曲线要到 10% 以下才真正起作用，所以磨掉一点血比看上去更不值。状态：睡眠、眩晕、诱导、进食是 ×2，深度睡眠是 ×100，Break 是 ×1.5。叠加：状态不叠乘 —— 只要身上有一个倍率低于 ×1，取的就是最低那个，所以一只已经注意到你的睡眠目标算 ×0.9，不是 ×2。

20Can you catch Alpha and Omega Aniimo?Alpha 和 Omega 能抓吗？

Not while they are standing. Alpha Aniimo have a 0% catch rate until they are defeated, and 6% afterwards. Omega Aniimo cannot be caught at any point — you fight them for the materials, not for the creature.站着的时候不能。Alpha 在被打倒前捕捉率是 0%，打倒之后 6%。Omega 任何阶段都抓不了 —— 打它是为了材料，不是为了那只精灵。

21Do Aniipods get used up when a catch fails?抓失败了球会没吗？

A throw that misses comes back to you — the pod only leaves your bag if it lands. Whether a pod that lands but fails to catch is consumed is something we could not confirm anywhere, so plan as though it might be.扔空了的球会飞回你手里 —— 只有命中了才从背包里扣。命中但没抓住算不算消耗，我们哪儿都没查到确证，所以按会消耗来准备。

22What is a Wild Surge?Wild Surge 是什么？

The timed swarm that starts when you Nurture at a Bloom — around twenty native Aniimo at once. It is what the Tumbler is built for, at ×6. It is also the only route to a Prismana, so read that section before spending your three daily Nurtures on anything else.在 Bloom 培育之后启动的那波限时精灵潮 —— 一次涌出二十只上下的本地 Aniimo。Tumbler 就是为它准备的，在那里是 ×6。它也是通向 Prismana 的唯一路径，所以每天三次免费培育怎么花，先看完 Prismana 那一节再说。

Evolution and forms进化与形态

Three stages, and the things that do not come back.三个阶段，以及那些回不来的东西。

23How do you evolve an Aniimo?怎么进化 Aniimo？

By hand, from a menu. Pause, open Aniimo Management, pick the Aniimo, and use the circular evolve icon beside Level Up and Training. It lists the criteria, charges the material and does it. Golden double arrows mean it is ready; grey means the criteria are only partly met; no icon means that is the final stage.手动，而且藏在菜单里：暂停 → Aniimo Management → 选中那只 → 点 Level Up and Training 旁边的圆形进化图标。它会列出条件、扣掉材料，然后执行。金色双向箭头代表条件已满足，灰色代表只满足了一部分，没有图标说明已经是最终形态。

24What are the evolution stages called?进化阶段叫什么？

Lumin, then Gamma, then Nova — and not every line uses all three. Lunara and Helion each have a single stage, and they are not weaker for it.Lumin、Gamma、Nova。不是每条线都会走完三段 —— 比如 Lunara 和 Helion 各自只有一段，而且并不因此更弱。

25Where do evolution materials come from?进化材料从哪来？

Mostly from Alpha world bosses: each claim costs 30 Primegy, and how much you get scales with your Character Rank and Badge level. The game will tell you which boss drops what — pause, then Institute Guide, Aniimo Challenge, Alpha Challenge Evolution Material.主要来自 Alpha 世界 BOSS：每领一次要花 30 Primegy，拿到的数量随你的角色等级和徽章等级提高。游戏自己会告诉你哪只 BOSS 掉什么 —— 暂停 → Institute Guide → Aniimo Challenge → Alpha Challenge Evolution Material。

26How do branching evolutions work?分支进化怎么运作？

Some Aniimo have two valid forms at the same stage, with a different list of criteria behind each, so you choose by meeting one list or the other. Budsquire is the clearest example: one branch wants you to defeat a Thornblade using Budsquire alone, the other wants a finished side quest and a particular personality trait. The two lists have nothing in common.有些 Aniimo 在同一阶段有两个合法形态，各自对应一份完全不同的条件表，满足哪一份就走哪一条。Budsquire 是最清楚的例子：一条分支要求你只用 Budsquire 打倒一只 Thornblade，另一条要求完成一段支线，并且性格里带某个特定词条。两份条件毫无交集。

27Can you undo an evolution, or re-pick a branch?进化能撤销吗？分支能重选吗？

No. Evolution stage is on the game's short list of permanent things, alongside a caught Aniimo's gender, its Innate Potential and its Resonance Star Rank. We found no item or service that reverses a branch, so treat the choice as final.不能。进化阶段在游戏的「不可逆」清单上，和已捕捉 Aniimo 的性别、Innate Potential、Resonance Star Rank 并列。我们没找到任何能撤销分支的道具或服务，所以当作最终选择来对待。

28Can you change an Aniimo's form after catching it?抓到之后还能改形态吗？

Only regional forms, and only with a Morph Orb — 300 Glimmer each, ten a week from the Glimmer Shop, and only into a form you have already caught. Prismana is deliberately excluded from that, so a Prismana still has to be earned at a Bloom.只能改地区形态，而且只能用 Morph Orb —— 300 Glimmer 一个，Glimmer 商店每周限购十个，而且只能变成你已经抓到过的形态。Prismana 被明确排除在外，想要还是得去 Bloom 挣。

29Do different forms of the same Aniimo have different stats?同一只 Aniimo 的不同形态，数值会不一样吗？

No, and this catches people out. Compare the official pages and the stat block is identical across forms — what changes is the element, the habitat, and sometimes one skill. Highland Emberpup is Fire and Rock where the base form is Fire alone, with exactly the same numbers. So you collect forms for typing and coverage, not for a stronger copy of a creature you already have.不会，这一点最容易让人误会。把官方页面并排看，各形态的六项数值是完全一致的 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，有时还有某一个技能。高地 Emberpup 是火加岩，基础形态只有火，数字一模一样。所以收形态是为了打击面和属性覆盖，不是为了拿到一只更强的同款。

30What is Transmog, and how do you unlock it?Transmog 是什么，怎么解锁？

A Legendary-only cosmetic re-skin. It unlocks after you befriend your first Legendary Aniimo, and it changes how the creature looks without touching its stats. It sits in the systems table on the progression page alongside the other unlocks, so this is a pointer rather than a second copy.只给 Legendary 用的外观替换。在你交到第一只 Legendary Aniimo 之后解锁，只改外观，不动数值。它和别的解锁系统一起列在进度页的系统表里，所以这里只做指引，不抄第二份。

Prismana and rare variantsPrismana 与稀有变体

The alternate forms, how they are triggered, and the odds the studio publishes itself.特殊形态怎么触发，以及官方自己公开的概率。

31What is a Prismana Aniimo?什么是 Prismana？

A rare alternate form that around twenty-five species have, plus two that exist as nothing else. You will not find one wandering the map — a Prismana has to be triggered. They arrive with a holographic skin, a second element and one extra skill.约二十五种 Aniimo 各自拥有的一种稀有特殊形态，另有两只是「只有 Prismana、没有别的形态」。它不会在地图上闲逛 —— Prismana 必须被触发。出现时带全息外观、第二属性，以及额外一个技能。

32How do you get a Prismana?怎么拿到 Prismana？

The long way round. Earn the region's Bronze Badge, then Nurture at a regional Bloom to start a Wild Surge, then catch what appears — catching, not defeating, is what fills the region's Prismatic Energy. Drive that to Full and the Prismana spawn is guaranteed. You get three free Nurtures a day, and the spawn is one-time: miss it and you start again.最笨也最稳的办法：先拿到该地区的铜徽章，然后在地区 Bloom 培育触发 Wild Surge，再把出现的东西抓掉 —— 是抓，不是打倒，抓才涨该地区的 Prismatic Energy。把它推到 Full，Prismana 的出现就是必定。每天三次免费培育，而且那次刷新只出一次：错过就重来。

33How many Prismana are there?Prismana 一共多少只？

Twenty-five, counted off the publisher's own index: twenty-five of its 86 entries list a Prismana Form, and every one of those also has a regular form. You will see twenty-seven quoted, and the gap is two names — Somniwing and Irisalis. Those two are real, but they are the game's Legendary Aniimo rather than Prismana, which is where the wider count goes wrong. The Prismana figure stays twenty-five.二十五只，按发行方自己的索引数的：索引里的 86 条中有 25 条列了 Prismana 形态，而这 25 只每一只都有常规形态。你会看到有人引用二十七这个数，差的这两只就是 Somniwing 和 Irisalis。这两只是真实存在的，但它们属于游戏的 Legendary 传说级，而不是 Prismana —— 多出来的那个数就是这么来的。所以 Prismana 的数字仍然是二十五。

34How do you get a Legendary Aniimo?怎么拿到 Legendary（传说）Aniimo？

There are two, and neither is a catch in the wild. Somniwing (No. 30) arrives once you bank 180 points in the Path of Twining Elite Program. Irisalis (No. S1-F), the Legendary evolution of Iris, needs 99 Irisalis Petals gathered across the Walking with Irisalis event — you spend them to craft the Legendary Aniipod: Irisalis, then beat and catch her at the Rift before the event window closes.有两只，都不是野外随手能抓的。Somniwing（第 30 号）在 Path of Twining 精英计划里攒够 180 点后到手。Irisalis（第 S1-F 号），Iris 的传说进化型，需要收集 99 枚 Irisalis Petals（在 Walking with Irisalis 活动里集），用它造出 Legendary Aniipod: Irisalis，然后在活动窗口关闭前到 Rift 打倒并抓住她。

35What are the odds of triggering a Prismana?触发 Prismana 的概率是多少？

Pawprint publishes them, which is rarer than it should be. From a Vein Essence Nurture, the chance of triggering a Prismana Flow outright is 3%; each Nurture also adds 480 Prismatic Energy, and 10,500 is the pity threshold that forces a Flow. Against those numbers the guaranteed routes — the Prismana Pact, or a Prismana Crystal at a Branch — are usually the better deal.官方公开了，这比它本该有的待遇要少见。用 Vein Essence 培育时，直接触发 Prismana Flow 的概率是 3%；每次培育另加 480 点 Prismatic Energy，10,500 是强制触发一次的保底线。对着这几个数看，保底路线 —— Prismana Pact，或者 Branch 上的 Prismana Crystal —— 通常比赌那 3% 划算。

36Are Prismana stronger than the normal form?Prismana 比普通形态强吗？

Not on paper. We compared four species' official pages and the stat blocks are identical; what changes is the element and the movepool. Guides that call them stronger are describing the second typing and the extra skill, which genuinely can be better — but it is a different creature rather than a bigger one.数值上不强。我们并排比了四只的官方页面，数值块完全一致，变的是属性和技能池。说它「更强」的攻略，描述的其实是第二属性和那个额外技能 —— 那确实可能更好用，但那是另一只精灵，不是同一只的加强版。

37What other rare variants are there?还有哪些稀有变体？

Four more. Sparkling is cosmetic particle effects in three styles — Common, Dazzling and Shadow — and the published odds on a Sparkling Cube are 99%, 1% and 0%, with the 1% moving from Dazzling to Shadow on eggs from Operation: Egg Heist. Umbral always rolls with Perfect Potential, but its dark tint is stripped when you evolve it. Alpha Aniimo are simply bigger. Blessed ones have swallowed a Lumin Amber and look no different at all once caught.另外四种。Sparkling 只是粒子外观，分 Common、Dazzling、Shadow 三款，Sparkling Cube 的官方概率是 99%、1%、0%，其中那 1% 在 Operation: Egg Heist 的蛋上会从 Dazzling 挪到 Shadow。Umbral 必定带 Perfect Potential，但进化时暗色外观会被洗掉。Alpha 单纯是体型更大。Blessed 是吞过 Lumin Amber 的，抓到之后外观和普通的一模一样。

38Can you change a Sparkling Aniimo's colour?Sparkling 的颜色能改吗？

Today, only by reroll. A Sparkling Pigment — five Sparkling Sigils at the Outpost Vendor — rerolls the colour at random, which is why people complain it keeps landing on one they already have. Version 1.1 changes that: it lets you switch between hues the creature has already unlocked, including back to its original. The hues are per-species, so unlocking a colour on one Aniimo does not unlock it on another.现在只能靠重掷。一个 Sparkling Pigment（在 Outpost Vendor 用 5 个 Sparkling Sigil 换）会随机重掷颜色，这也是为什么大家抱怨老是掷回已经有的那个。1.1 版本会改这件事：它让你在已经解锁的颜色之间来回切，包括切回原始色。颜色是按物种各算各的，所以在一只身上解锁了某个色，不会在另一只身上也解锁。

Combat, elements and teams战斗、属性与队伍

What beats what, and who to build first.什么克什么，以及先养谁。

39What are the nine elements, and what beats what?九个属性是什么，谁克谁？

The nine are Fire, Water, Grass, Electric, Ice, Wind, Rock, Dark and Holy — some guides say Light for Holy and Earth for Rock. In short: Ice beats Water and Electric; Water beats Fire and Rock; Electric beats Water and Wind; Fire beats Ice and Grass; Grass beats Water and Rock; Rock beats Ice and Fire; Wind beats Grass and Dark; Dark beats Electric, Grass and Holy; Holy beats Wind and Dark. In game, a golden thumb on a squad portrait means you have the advantage and a white one means you do not. The damage multipliers are missing on purpose: 1.6× and 0.625× are quoted everywhere, we could not verify either against a page we trust, and one source says 0.375×.九属性是火、水、草、电、冰、风、岩、暗、圣 —— 也有攻略把圣叫光、把岩叫土。简版：冰克水、电；水克火、岩；电克水、风；火克冰、草；草克水、岩；岩克冰、火；风克草、暗；暗克电、草、圣；圣克风、暗。游戏里队伍头像上的金色拇指代表你占优，白色代表你吃亏。伤害倍率这里故意没写：1.6 倍和 0.625 倍到处被引用，但我们没法在我们信得过的页面上核实，还有一份来源写的是 0.375 倍。

40What are spatial counters?空间克制是什么？

A second rock-paper-scissors that runs alongside the elements, and the game barely explains it. Four attack types: Ranged, Flying, Tunnel and Pound. Ranged counters Flying but struggles against Tunnel; Pound counters Tunnel but struggles against Flying. Two pairs rather than one triangle. It matters most in Twine mode, where dodging a ranged attack by digging is the actual mechanic.和属性并行的一套石头剪刀布，游戏几乎没解释过。四种攻击类型：远程、飞行、钻地、重击。远程克飞行，但打不中钻地；重击克钻地，但打不中飞行。是两组对克，不是一个三角。它在 Twine 模式下最要紧 —— 那里用钻地躲远程就是核心操作。

41What is a good team composition?队伍怎么配比较好？

One of each role rather than five damage dealers: DPS, Break, Support and Regen, with a dedicated Heal if your Support cannot keep the party up. Stacking DPS is the common mistake — it starves you of Energy and costs you more damage than it added.每个定位各一个，而不是五个输出：DPS、Break、Support、Regen，如果你的 Support 撑不住全队，再加一个专职 Heal。堆输出是最常见的错 —— 能量会被抽干，亏掉的伤害比堆出来的多。

42Which starter should I pick, Helion or Lunara?开局选 Helion 还是 Lunara？

It genuinely does not matter much. They have identical base stats and are both Light DPS; the difference is entirely in the kit, with Helion melee and Lunara ranged. If you want a tiebreak, Lunara's range is the more forgiving of the two, and both sit in the top tier of the current rankings.说实话差别不大。两只基础数值完全一样，都是光属性输出；区别全在技能组 —— Helion 近战，Lunara 远程。非要一个理由的话，Lunara 的射程更宽容，而且两只在当前榜单里都在第一梯队。

43Can you get the other starter later?另一只开局精灵后面还能拿到吗？

Nobody has confirmed it either way — the studio has never said, and we could not find a source that settles it. Treat it as a choice you are making, not a formality.没有任何一方确认过 —— 官方从没说过，我们也找不到能定论的来源 —— 所以把它当成一个真正要做的选择，而不是走个形式。

44What is Twine mode?Twine 模式是什么？

Merging with a captured Aniimo and playing as it. It is the entire traversal system — flying, digging, climbing, swimming, sprinting and tunnelling only work while Twined, so chests and puzzles are gated behind owning the right creature. It also turns combat from issuing orders into fighting directly, and it is how you speak to wild Aniimo: Twine with one of the same family and you can hold a conversation with its kin.Twine（联结）：和已捕捉的 Aniimo 联结，自己变成它。整个移动系统都在这上面 —— 飞行、钻地、攀爬、游泳、冲刺、隧道全都只在 Twine 状态下可用，所以宝箱和谜题是拿「有没有那只精灵」来卡的。它还让战斗从下达指令变成直接操作，也是和野生 Aniimo 对话的唯一方式：和同族的一只 Twine，就能和它的同类聊上。

45What is the best Aniimo in the game right now?现在最强的 Aniimo 是哪只？

The studio has never ranked them, and no single list is worth trusting outright. Going by what the kits themselves do, Fulmintis is the DPS pick, Inferlupa the Break pick, Gracewing the Heal pick and Turbo the Support pick, with Somniwing for Regen — that last one is real, it is one of the game's two Legendary Aniimo. Below those, the runners-up are close enough that we have not averaged them into a list that would look like a consensus and would not be one.官方从来没有排过名，也没有哪一份榜单值得单独相信。按技能组本身看，输出是 Fulmintis，Break 是 Inferlupa，治疗是 Gracewing，辅助是 Turbo，Regen 是 Somniwing —— 最后这只现在是真实存在的，是游戏两只传说 Aniimo 之一。这几个名字往下，其余的差距都很小，我们没有把它们平均成一份看着像共识、其实不是共识的排名。

Playing with other people和其他人一起玩

What you can do together, and what stays solo.哪些能一起做，哪些始终是单人。

46Can you play the story in co-op?主线剧情能联机一起推吗？

No, and this is the part that catches people out. The main story is solo — co-op is visiting another player's world, and quests and cutscenes do not fire for a guest, so the campaign cannot be advanced together. Everything around it — catching, exploring, world bosses, the co-op Sanctums — is fine to do together.不能，这正是最容易让人误会的地方。主线只能单人推 —— 联机是进别人的世界，任务和过场对客人不触发，所以剧情没法一起推进。主线之外的部分 —— 捕捉、探索、世界 BOSS、需要两人的 Sanctum —— 都可以一起做。

47How do you play with a friend?怎么和朋友一起玩？

Add them by their Friend ID / UID (or meet in a hub), then invite them into Companion Mode so you share one world. The friends and party menus do not appear until you have beaten Alpha Turbo, so co-op is locked for roughly the first stretch of the game.用 Friend ID / UID 加好友（或者在聚集地碰到），然后邀请对方进 Companion Mode，就能共享同一个世界。好友和队伍菜单要打倒 Alpha Turbo 之后才出现，所以游戏开头一段是没法联机的。

48Can you trade Aniimo with other players?能和其他玩家交换 Aniimo 吗？

Yes, but only with friends — there is no auction house. Trading needs the Good Friend affinity rank (100 points; affinity accrues at most 100 a day), and what you can hand over widens as the rank rises. Prismana, Sparkling and Alpha Aniimo cannot be traded at all. It is capped at five trades a day, each traded creature is locked for 30 days, and a trade can randomly bump the creature's Individual Value — so it is not always a clean swap.能，但只能和好友换 —— 没有拍卖行。交换需要 Good Friend 好感等级（100 点；好感每天最多涨 100），能送出去的东西随等级升高而变多。Prismana、Sparkling 和 Alpha 一概不能交易。每天最多五笔，每只交易出去的精灵会被锁 30 天，而且交易可能随机给精灵抬一档 Individual Value —— 所以它未必是一次干净的交换。

49Is there PvP?有 PvP 吗？

Yes, but it is PvEvP, not duels. Operation: Egg Heist drops three-player teams into the Lost Isles to race rival teams and wild Aniimo for eggs; a Diamond variant runs six teams, and Chaos Mode is the hardest tier. There is no straight 1v1 arena, and Holo-Battle — despite the name — is co-op PvE, not player versus player.有，但是 PvEvP，不是单挑。Operation: Egg Heist 把三人小队丢进 Lost Isles，和敌对小队、野生 Aniimo 抢蛋；有个 Diamond 变体是六队对打，Chaos Mode 是最难的一档。没有正面对决的 1v1 竞技场；而 Holo-Battle 这个名字听着像 PvP，其实是合作的 PvE。

50Which Sanctums need two players?哪些 Sanctum 需要两个人？

Three of them. The BOSS page lists the seventeen Sanctums and marks which three are built for two players — the other fourteen are solo — so that is where to check before you go looking for a partner.三个。BOSS 页面列了十七个 Sanctum，并标出哪三个是为两人设计的 —— 其余十四个是单人 —— 所以去找人之前先去那里看一眼。

Progression, the world and its limits进度、世界与限制

Energy, resets, badges, and bending the clock.能量、重置、徽章，以及拨动时钟。

51What is Primegy, and what does it block?Primegy 是什么？卡住了什么？

It is the energy system, and it is narrower than people fear. It regenerates one every six minutes to a cap of 300, and it is spent claiming Alpha and Omega rewards and opening Holo-Battle Sim chests, at 30 a time. It does not gate the story, catching, exploration or Operation: Egg Heist — so running dry never stops you playing, it only stops you cashing boss rewards.就是那个能量系统，而且比大家担心的窄得多。每 6 分钟回 1 点，上限 300，花在领取 Alpha 和 Omega 奖励以及开 Holo-Battle Sim 宝箱上，每次 30 点。它不卡主线、不卡捕捉、不卡探索、不卡 Operation: Egg Heist —— 所以没能量从来不会让你玩不下去，只是领不了 BOSS 奖励。

52When is the daily reset?每日重置是几点？

4:00 AM server time. Servers are grouped as America (UTC−5), Europe (UTC+1) and APAC (UTC+8), and dailies reset against your server's clock rather than your own.服务器时间凌晨 4:00。 服务器按 America（UTC−5）、Europe（UTC+1）、APAC（UTC+8）分组，日常按你所在服务器的钟走，不是按你的。

53What are badges for?徽章有什么用？

They are the region's gate, and two of them matter. The Bronze Badge is what unlocks Nurturing at all — no Bronze, no Prismana. The Silver Badge, which wants 70% collection in that region plus that region's Alpha defeated, is what lets you infuse Lumin Amber at a Branch to change the weather or force a Prismana Flow.它是地区的门槛，其中两个很关键。铜徽章决定你能不能培育 —— 没有铜，就没有 Prismana。银徽章要求该地区图鉴 70% 加上打倒该地区 Alpha，拿到之后才能在 Branch 注入 Lumin Amber，用来改天气或强制触发 Prismana Flow。

54Can you change the time of day?时间能改吗？

Yes, freely and as often as you like, with no cooldown. Open the menu, use the sun-and-moon icon on the panel at the right, and pick Noon, Nighttime, Early Morning or Dusk. Then check the map: selecting the nearest Bloom lists what spawns there and when, which is how the time-locked forms are meant to be found.能，随时改，随便改，没有冷却。打开菜单，用右侧面板上的日月图标，选正午、夜晚、清晨或黄昏。然后去看地图：选中最近的 Bloom，它会列出那里会刷什么、什么时候刷 —— 限时形态本来就是这么找的。

55Can you change the weather?天气能改吗？

Not by hand, in the current version. The Change Weather option at the Breezy Plains Branch exists and is greyed out — Snowy, Rainy and Thunderstorm cannot be selected, and the only thing the Branch will do is induce a Prismana Flow. Two routes do work: wait for the real-time cycle, which the map forecasts in local time with the weather-gated Aniimo listed underneath, or visit another player's world in co-op, where the weather is whatever theirs happens to be. Photo Mode weather is cosmetic and changes nothing.当前版本不能手动改。Breezy Plains Branch 上的 Change Weather 功能确实存在，但是灰的 —— 雪天、雨天、雷暴都选不了，Branch 唯一能做的只是引导 Prismana Flow。真正可行的只有两条路：等实时天气循环（地图会按本地时间预报，并把只在该天气出现的 Aniimo 列在下面），或者联机进别人的世界，那边天气是什么就是什么。拍照模式的天气只是外观，不影响任何东西。

56What is the level cap?等级上限是多少？

Higher than the promotion ladder suggests. The last Pathfinder title tops out at level 60. Pawprint has never stated a character cap, so treat 71 as the number players work from rather than a promise. The cap is a chain: an Aniimo cannot out-level your Pathfinder level, which cannot out-level your Student level, and you raise the top by clearing Elite Training and passing the promotion assessment. Extra character EXP is not wasted while you are capped — it is held and applied once the cap lifts.比晋升阶梯看上去更高。最后一个 Pathfinder 头衔止步于 60 级。官方从未公布过角色上限，所以把 71 当成玩家在用的数字，而不是一个承诺。上限是一条链：Aniimo 等级不能超过你的 Pathfinder 等级，后者又不能超过你的 Student 等级；抬高天花板要靠通关 Elite Training 并通过晋升考核。封顶时多出来的角色经验不会浪费 —— 会被存着，上限一抬就补上。

57How long is the game?这游戏多长？

The main story is roughly 16–17 hours. Main plus side quests lands around 40–60 hours, and 100% — every side quest and every Aniimo — is a 50-hour-plus job by the lowest estimate and 100 hours by the only completionist report. Most of these come from the closed beta, so treat them as a shape rather than a stopwatch.主线大约 16–17 小时。主线加支线大约 40–60 小时；100% —— 每条支线加每一只 Aniimo —— 最低的估计是 50 小时以上，唯一一份全收集报告是 100 小时。这些数字多半来自封测，所以当个大概形状看，别当秒表。

58What is there to do after the story?主线打完之后有什么可做的？

The endgame switches from the campaign to a handful of repeatable loops: Holo-Battle Simulator, which throws enhanced Alpha bosses at you inside a three-minute timer for a score; Operation: Egg Heist for the PvEvP side; finishing region badges and the collection; hunting Prismana; and the Elite Training promotions that push your level cap upward. There is no separate raid tier beyond that yet.终局从剧情转向几个可反复刷的循环：Holo-Battle Simulator，在三分钟倒计时里刷强化版 Alpha BOSS 比分数；Operation: Egg Heist 玩 PvEvP；补全地区徽章和图鉴；找 Prismana；以及抬高等级上限的 Elite Training 晋升。除此之外，目前没有另外一层团本。

Events and what's live now活动与当前版本

Where the calendar lives, and what comes back.日历在哪里，以及什么会复刻。

59Where do I find the current events?在哪看当前的活动？

In the game's Events menu, opened with F2. The windows rotate on their own schedule — some weekly, some over a few weeks — so we do not mirror the calendar here, where it would be stale on arrival. At launch the recurring patterns were a 30-day check-in, a rotating regional boost, weekend egg events, a photo hunt and an ecological survey, plus the long Legendary Journey. The Events page is the only list that stays current.在游戏的活动菜单里，按 F2 打开。活动窗口按自己的节奏轮换 —— 有的按周，有的几周一轮 —— 所以我们不在这里镜像那份日历，它一上来就会过期。上线时的轮换里有一套 30 天签到、一个按地区轮换的增益、周末蛋活动、一次找线索的拍照狩猎和一次生态调查，外加长线的传说之旅。活动页是唯一能保持最新的清单。

60Do limited-time events come back?限时活动会复刻吗？

The short ones do — the photo hunt, the survey, the weekend egg event and the regional boost recur on a weekly or fortnightly rotation, so a missed window is usually only days from returning. The long seasonal events run against a fixed end date (the Irisalis chapter, for instance, closes in December 2026), and nothing we found confirms a finished seasonal event ever re-runs.短的那些会 —— 拍照狩猎、生态调查、周末蛋活动和地区增益都按周或双周轮换，所以错过一个窗口，通常几天后就会回来。长的赛季活动则对着一个固定结束日期跑（比如 Irisalis 那一章在 2026 年 12 月关闭），而我们没找到任何能证明已结束的赛季活动会完整复刻的证据。

61What is the Companion Handbook?Companion Handbook 是什么？

The season pass. Every player gets a free track; the paid Advanced track (about $9.99) adds a Prismana egg with Perfect Potential, an exclusive evolution material, +5 storage and cosmetics. The free track is not empty either — its level-10 reward is an egg that hatches a Fulmintis, which is how the one creature people call paywalled is actually reachable without paying.赛季通行证。人人都有免费线；付费的 Advanced 线（约 9.99 美元）额外给出带 Perfect Potential 的 Prismana 蛋、一份专属进化材料、+5 储物位和外观。免费线也不空 —— 它的 10 级奖励是一颗孵化出 Fulmintis 的蛋，这也是那只被称作「付费墙」的精灵其实不花钱也能拿到的原因。

62What are Twitch Drops?Twitch Drops 是什么？

A campaign that pays in-game rewards for watching partnered streams with your Twitch account linked. The codes page carries it — what it needs and where the drops land — so this is a pointer rather than a second copy.一个把 Twitch 账号绑定后看合作直播就能领游戏内奖励的活动。兑换码页面里写了它 —— 需要什么、奖励发到哪 —— 所以这里只做指引，不抄第二份。

Accounts, errors and getting unstuck账号、报错与脱困

The failures with known causes, and the one that only looks like lost progress.有明确成因的那几个故障，以及那个看着像丢档的。

63Why does it say Account Login Failed?为什么提示 Account Login Failed？

Four causes, in the order worth checking. Mashing Start Adventure queues a second login before the first has finished — press it once and wait. Sitting on the login screen times the session out before you ever get in. A version mismatch between client and server clears itself once both are updated. And the one nobody guesses: an install path containing non-alphabetic characters can break the login on its own, so move the install somewhere plain.四个成因，按值得排查的顺序：猛点 Start Adventure 会在第一次登录没走完之前排队第二次 —— 点一次然后等。停在登录界面不动会在你进去之前就把会话超时掉。客户端与服务器版本不一致，两边都更新完就自己好了。最后一个没人猜得到：安装路径里带非字母字符本身就能让登录失败，把安装位置挪到一个干净的路径下。

64What are the fixes for the other common errors?其他常见报错怎么修？

There is an official pinned thread covering nine, and these are the ones people actually hit. Controller misbehaving: disable Steam Input. Red glow in the eyes is a rendering artefact — turn Global Illumination down or switch to the Performance preset. NPC damage not registering: restart. Black screen: usually drivers, or DirectX 11 against 12, so try the other and turn DLSS off. Network error 107 was patched in 1.0.3551601.0, so update before anything else. On Steam Deck, use Proton 10.0.0-4. 65My character is gone — what happened?角色不见了，怎么回事？

Check which server you are on before you file anything. Characters exist only on the server they were created on, so logging in to a different one shows you an empty account rather than an error. Switch back and it will be there.先看你在哪个服务器，再考虑提工单。角色只存在于创建它的那个服务器上，登到别的服务器只会看到一个空账号，而不是报错。切回去它就在。

66How do you get unstuck?卡住了怎么办？

There is an Unstuck button in Settings, and that is precisely what it is for — it is the first thing to press when you are inside geometry or a quest will not advance. If it does not help, restart the game rather than fighting it; several of the known bugs are fixed by nothing more than a relaunch.设置里有一个 Unstuck 按钮，它就是干这个的 —— 卡进地形里，或者任务推不动的时候，第一个该按的就是它。要是没用，重启游戏，别硬耗；已知的 bug 里有好几个靠重开就好了。

67Are there working Aniimo codes?Aniimo 有能用的兑换码吗？

Yes — fourteen are still live in October 2026, paying out Glimmer, Credits, Growth Flowers and Aniipods. The two codes that worked at launch, aniimowelcome and aniimoparty, have both hit their redemption limit and are kept on the page as spent rather than deleted. The box is at Settings → Account → Gift Code Redemption, and a rejection on a new account usually means the Mail is not open yet rather than a dead code.有 —— 2026 年 10 月还有十四个能用，给 Glimmer、Credits、Growth Flower 和 Aniipod。开服时能用的两个码 aniimowelcome 和 aniimoparty 都撞上了兑换上限，在页面上按「已跑完」列着，而不是删掉。入口在 设置 → 账号 → Gift Code Redemption；新账号被拒，多半是邮箱还没开，不是码死了。

68How do you contact support?怎么联系客服？

Four ways in, and they reach the same place. In game, open the menu and pick Support, or press F10 to file a report on the spot. The web Support Center is at fpcs-web.aniimo.com, and the global support email is support_en@aniimo.com (support_jp@aniimo.com for Japan). Attach the exact error text and your server name — that is what the two answers above about login and missing characters turn on.四条路，通向同一个地方。游戏内打开菜单选 Support，或直接按 F10 现场提报。网页客服中心是 fpcs-web.aniimo.com，全球客服邮箱是 support_en@aniimo.com（日本是 support_jp@aniimo.com）。把报错的原文和你的服务器名一起附上 —— 上面两条登录失败和角色不见的答案，靠的就是这两样。

69Can you change your character's name?能改角色名吗？

Yes, but not for free. A Name Change Card costs 300 Glimmer from the Shop's Item tab, you can buy one a week, and names cap at 14 characters. The card only renames your Pathfinder — you cannot rename an individual Aniimo yet, which the game says is planned for a later update.能，但不是免费。一张 Name Change Card 在商店的 Item 栏花 300 Glimmer，每周能买一张，名字最长 14 个字符。这张卡只能改你的 Pathfinder 名 —— 单只 Aniimo 目前还改不了名，游戏说计划在后续更新里加。

70Can you get a refund?能退款吗？

Rarely, and it is the platform's call as much as Pawprint's. The official line is that digital purchases are generally not refundable once the purchase completes, and anything already used or consumed is normally out. On PC a refund runs through Steam's or Epic's own policy rather than the studio's, so ask the store as well as support.很少能，而且这更多是平台说了算，不全是官方说了算。官方口径是：数字购买一旦完成，一般不退，任何已经使用或消耗过的东西通常也不在退款范围内。在 PC 上退款走的是 Steam 或 Epic 自己的政策，而不是工作室的，所以商店和客服两边都要问。

71Why does the game crash on some PCs?为什么有些电脑上会崩溃？

If you are on a recent Intel 13th- or 14th-generation CPU, you are not imagining it — that is a known stability issue, not your build. Otherwise work the same list as a black screen: update your GPU drivers, try DirectX 11 against 12, and turn DLSS off. A relaunch clears several of the known crashes on its own.如果你是较新的 Intel 第 13 或 14 代 CPU，那不是错觉 —— 那是一个已知的稳定性问题，不是你配置的错。否则就照黑屏那条排查：更新显卡驱动，DirectX 11 和 12 换另一个试，把 DLSS 关掉。重启能自己解决其中好几个已知崩溃。
