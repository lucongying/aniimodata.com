Homeland家园

Homeland Materials家园材料

The Homeland is built out of two materials and two chains. Wood Blocks come off the Woodland and Mineral Sand out of the Mine, and each one refines four steps up — into the Laminated Beams and Refined Ore a late RV upgrade asks for by name. This is the whole ladder, the four upgrade steps whose costs are printed, the two planting facilities that wood actually comes out of, and the twelve materials the wilderness supplies instead.家园是靠两种材料和两条加工链撑起来的。木块出自林地，砂矿出自矿场，各自往上精炼四级 —— 变成高级房车升级点名要的复合梁和精炼矿。这一页是整条阶梯、官方印全了的四级升级成本、这些木材真正出自的两座种植设施，以及野外供应的那十二种材料。

The two everything starts from一切都从这两种开始

Both are farmed rather than found, and both arrive as a by-product of something you were doing anyway — which is why the honest answer to "where do I get wood" is not a place on the map.两种都是种出来的，不是捡来的，而且都是你本来就在做的事的副产物 —— 所以「木头去哪儿弄」这个问题的诚实答案，不是一个地图上的地点。

Material材料 Where it comes from从哪来
Wood Block木块 The Woodland. Plant a tree and the facility logs it for you, and a higher tree level yields more. Clearing a plot's clutter when it unlocks pays a little, and the Homeland shop will trade for more.林地。种下去，设施会替你把它伐掉，树等级越高产量越多。解锁地块时清理杂物也能得一点，家园商店可以再换一些。
Mineral Sand砂矿 The Mine, which works the same way and wants an Aniimo with Mining. Plot clutter and open-world pickups — shells, stumps — top it up.矿场，运作方式一样，需要有采矿能力的伊莫。地块杂物和野外拾取（贝壳、木桩）也能补上一些。

Both are also the two things the Homeland shop trades for daily and the two an order can pay in, so a shortage of either has three fixes rather than one. The move worth avoiding is selling them raw: an upgrade wants the material and the coin both, and a refined good sells for more than the parts that went into it.这两样也正好是家园商店每天能兑换的两种，以及订单会付的两种，所以缺任何一种都有三条路而不是一条。唯一值得避开的做法是直接把它们卖掉：升级同时要材料和家园币，而加工成品卖得比它用的原料贵。

What the two planting facilities hand back两座种植设施到底能给你什么

Every recipe the Farmland and the Woodland print, level by level. The Woodland is the one to read twice: a tree yields Wood Blocks alongside its own product, and the amount follows the facility level rather than the tree — so the wood the chains above run on is a by-product of whatever else you happened to grow.农田和林地印出来的每一条配方，按等级排开。林地这张值得读两遍：每棵树在产出自己那份东西的同时还出木块，而数量跟着设施等级走、不跟着树走 —— 上面两条加工链吃的木头，其实是你顺手种别的时的副产物。

The four rows without a picture are the four with no entry in the item database — the two seasonal crops, the collaboration crop and Premium Wheat. They are real recipes the facility prints, but nothing in the database carries them, so there is no icon to fetch and no Chinese name to print; their English names stand in both languages rather than a translation being invented here. A quick formula is not a second crop. It is the same plant under a second recipe that grows for longer and returns far more, and it is gated by the Ecological Module at a level the note column names rather than by the plot. Read the quick rows as the way to make one crop in bulk and the plain rows as the way to make everything else.没有图标的那四行，正好就是物品库里没有条目的那四行 —— 两个赛季作物、一个联动作物，外加 Premium Wheat。它们是设施真会印出来的配方，但物品库里没有对应条目，所以既没有图标可下，也没有中文名可写；这几行在中英两版里都直接用英文名，而不是在这里替它编一个译名。高速配方不是另一种作物，而是同一种植物换一套配方：种得更久、收得更多，门槛在生态模块而不在地块，具体等级写在备注列 —— 高速那几行该按「把某一种作物量产」来读，其余各行才是「别的作物」。

Seven plants carry a seed in the database and no recipe on either facility — Lotus Root, Sorghum, Taro, Pine, Volcano Bean, Aloeswood and Pumpkin. That is the database's shape rather than a gap here: it prints the seed and neither facility prints a line for it. The Pine's seed is the only one whose source is stated anywhere, and it is the Homeland shop.还有七种植物在物品库里有种子，但两座设施一条配方都没给它们 —— 莲藕、高梁、芋头、松树、火山豆、沉香、南瓜。这是物品库本身的形状，不是这一页漏了：它印了种子，设施却一条都没印。七种里只有松树种子在任意一处写明了来源，是家园商店。

The two chains两条加工链

Each chain is five links and runs one way only. Every step takes the link below it and nothing else, which is why the last column is the one to read: the RV never asks for the same material twice.每条链五环，且只朝一个方向走。每一环只吃它下面那一环，不加别的 —— 这正是最后一列值得看的原因：房车不会两次要同一种材料。

Read that last column down, not across. An upgrade does not want wood, it wants one named wood — and by RV 18 a single step wants 800 Laminated Beams, which sits four links above the tree you planted. So the bench has to keep running through every tier instead of being built once and forgotten. The link above the last one printed here is each chain's ceiling, and nothing in the printed ladder has asked for it yet.最后一列要竖着读，不是横着读。 升级要的不是「木头」，而是某一种具名的木头 —— 到 RV 18，一步就要 800 根复合梁，而它在你种下那棵树往上数四环的位置。所以加工台得一路开着，不能建一次就不管了。上面印的再往上一环是这条链的天花板，目前公布出来的阶梯还没用到它。

What an upgrade costs一次升级要什么

The publisher's own data prints four upgrade steps in full. Each one swaps the material it wants for the next link of the chain above — which is the pattern worth carrying past the four rows.官方数据把四级升级完整印了出来。每一级要的材料都换成加工链上的下一环 ——这个规律比这四行本身更值得带走。

The coin column is the one that catches people: it grows by roughly an order of magnitude every four levels while the material count barely moves. Production that exists only to be sold stops being optional at the top of this ladder, and it is why refined goods rather than raw ones are what the shop should be given. What each level *gives* you — capacity and unlocks — is on the Homeland page, which is a different table over the same ladder and not a second copy of this one. The third thing the screen asks for is the one that is neither of those: it also wants a Habitability total, which no material satisfies. That one is on the furniture page, since it is a number you buy rather than a number you make.真正容易让人措手不及的是家园币那一列：每四级就往上翻大概一个数量级，而材料数量几乎不动。所以到阶梯上半段，纯为了卖钱而存在的那部分生产就不是可选项了—— 这也正是商店该收加工成品、而不是原料的原因。每一级给你什么（容量和解锁）在家园页上，那是同一条阶梯上的另一张表，不是这张的第二份。升级界面要的第三样东西两边都不是：它还要一份宜居度总和，而那是没有材料能顶的。那一项在家具页上 —— 因为它是买来的数字，不是做出来的数字。

The twelve the wilderness supplies野外供应的那十二种

Nothing above is gathered; these are nothing but. Each of the four mapped regions carries three of them, and the Homeland orders ask for them — which is what makes picking them up in passing worthwhile. Several also grow in the neighbouring regions, so a region that will not hand one over is not a bug.上面没有一种是采来的，这十二种全是。已制图的四个地区各带三种，而家园订单会要它们 —— 顺路捡才值得。有几种除了本区也长在邻近地区，所以某个地区不肯给你某一株并不是 bug。
