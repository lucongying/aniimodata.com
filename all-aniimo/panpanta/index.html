Panpanta胖胖獭

Water水 Break击破

Panpanta has 3 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.胖胖獭 共有 3 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Panpanta is the BREAK ending of the same fork and the species with the widest spread of energy costs in its family: a Splash Shield at 20 energy, a Water Cannonade at 20, a Hydro Roll at 30, and a 176-power Home Run that costs nothing. Where its sibling Piopiota spends water to defend the team, this one is built to keep swinging.胖胖獭是同一个分叉里偏击破的那一端，也是这一家子里能量消耗跨度最大的物种：20 能量的水花护盾、20 能量的水炮连发、30 能量的水滚，以及一发不耗能量的 176 威力全垒打。它的兄弟漂漂獭花水去保护队友，而这一只生来就是为了继续挥。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Appeal出风头

On entering battle, if there is an opposite-sex Susuta family member on the team, it turns pink and gains +30% BREAK efficiency for 15 seconds, on a 20-second cooldown. The team requirement is the entire gate — without a family member of the right sex, the trait does nothing at all.自己登场时，如果队伍里有不同性别的羞羞獭家族成员，它会变成粉色并提高 30% 击破效率，持续 15 秒，冷却 20 秒。队伍条件是唯一的门槛 —— 没有符合条件的家族成员，这个特性等于不存在。
