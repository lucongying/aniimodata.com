Beginner Guide新手入门

The opening hours in order: what actually happens, the one choice you cannot take back, and what not to spend yet.开局这几个小时，按顺序讲：实际会发生什么、哪个选择收不回来、以及现在还不该花什么。

The opening, in order开局顺序

Main-story quests come in this order and cannot be done out of sequence. The quest names are the game's own.主线任务就是这个顺序，不能跳着做。任务名是游戏自己的叫法。

  1. 01

    You are given an Emberpup — you do not choose it第一只直接发到你手里

    In An Idyllic Trip you make your character and name it, then Nicole points you at an Emberpup to catch. It is the Aniimo you are handed, not one of a set you pick from; the quest then has you use it to burn the branches across the path. Your character's gender is decided here and cannot be changed later.「An Idyllic Trip」里你先捏脸起名。之后 Nicole 会指着一只 Emberpup 让你抓，没有别的选项。接下来你要用它烧掉挡路的树枝。角色的性别在这里就定死了，之后改不了。

  2. 02

    Three catches, marked for you三次有标记的捕捉

    Aniimo Catching Journey asks for three and marks them on your map: Celestis, Hummin and Skippy.「Aniimo Catching Journey」要你抓三只，地图上会给标记：Celestis、Hummin 和 Skippy。

  3. 03

    The sun-and-moon mural日月壁画

    The Mysterious Cavern takes you to a mural of a sun and a moon Aniimo, and asks you to pick a guardian: Helion or Lunara. It is the only real decision in the opening, and the next section is about it, because you cannot undo it.「The Mysterious Cavern」会把你带到一幅日月 Aniimo 壁画前，让你选一位守护者：Helion 还是 Lunara。开局只有这一个选择收不回来，下一节专门讲它。

  4. 04

    You join the Institute either way不管怎么选都得进研究所

    Institution Initiation puts you in the Polaris Institute whatever you answer — the story does it for you. The Prismana Pact sign-up screen appears here too, but this is not where you claim it.「Institution Initiation」里你无论怎么回答都会加入 Polaris Institute，剧情替你决定了。Prismana 契约的登记界面也在这里出现，但领取不在这儿。

  5. 05

    Where the tutorial actually ends新手期真正结束的地方

    At the end of Chasing the Clouds you fight Alpha Turbo, then report to Dr. Lewis. That one conversation unlocks Elite Training and promotes you to Student I — after this the game stops holding your hand.「Chasing the Clouds」最后你会打 Alpha Turbo，然后回去找 Dr. Lewis 复命。这次对话解锁 精英训练，并把你晋升为 Student I。从这里开始，游戏不再手把手带你。

Helion or LunaraHelion 还是 Lunara

The only opening decision you cannot take back. Both are Holy-type DPS whose six stats match to the point, so the question is range rather than power. Lunara holds her distance, marks the target and cuts its Light resistance, which makes her the easier first pick. Helion fights in melee and drags groups together, which pays off once you can dodge reliably.开局唯一一个选完就收不回来的决定。两只都是光属性输出，六项数值一模一样，所以这是距离问题，不是强度问题。Lunara 拉开距离，给目标上标记、削它的光抗，第一次玩更容易上手；Helion 贴身打，能把怪聚拢，等你能稳定闪避之后更强。

Your Student rank caps everything else学生等级卡住其他一切

Student Level is a hard cap on your character and on every Aniimo you own — you cannot out-level your rank. If your team has stopped growing, this is usually why, and the fix is Elite Training rather than more catching.学生等级是你角色以及你所有 Aniimo 的等级硬上限，你超不过自己的等级。队伍突然练不动了，多半就是这个原因。该去打精英训练，别再抓了。

What you cannot undo做了就收不回来的事

The game does not collect these in one place, and two of them are settled in your first hour.游戏没把这些集中放在一个地方，其中两条在你开局第一个小时里就定了。

  • Your character's gender. It is set at creation and there is no way back to it. The cards you can buy for 300 Glimmer each change everything around it — a Redesign card for face, makeup and build, a Hair Saloon card for hair colour — and switching to a hairstyle you have already unlocked is free.角色的性别。 捏完就定了，没有改回来的办法。能买到的卡各 300 Glimmer，改的都是它周围的部分：Redesign 卡管脸、妆容和体型，Hair Saloon 卡管发色。切到已经解锁过的发型不要钱。
  • Helion or Lunara. You get one, and neither turns up in the wild or in an egg, so the one you did not pick is not waiting for you elsewhere on the map. Dr. Lewis mentions an item called the Ancient Prism that would let you awaken it; nobody has been seen using one.Helion 还是 Lunara。 只能得一只。两只都不在野外刷新、也不在蛋里，所以没选的那只不会在地图别处等你。Dr. Lewis 提过一个叫 Ancient Prism 的道具，说能唤醒它，但没人见过真的用出来。
  • Releasing an Aniimo. The confirmation screen is final, and the training materials you already fed it do not come back. The Farewell Gift you receive is a bundle of growth items, which is not the same thing.放生 Aniimo。 最后那个确认框点了就算数，已经喂进去的训练材料不退。换回来的 Farewell Gift 是一包成长道具，跟投进去的不是一回事。
  • Potential and stat rolls. These are fixed the moment you catch an Aniimo and nothing in the game rerolls them, so the first one of a species you catch is rarely the one worth building. Personality is the exception — that can be rerolled.潜力和个体值。 抓到那一刻就定死，游戏里没有任何东西能重 roll。所以同一个物种你抓的第一只，多半不是值得养的那只。人格是例外，那个可以重 roll。
  • Your server. The regional servers run separately and a character cannot be moved between them. Cross-save carries your progress between devices on the same server, not between servers, so the region you pick at creation is the region you play.你的服务器。 各区服各自独立，角色不能在它们之间转移。Cross-save 搬的是同一服务器上不同设备之间的进度，不是服务器之间。创角时选的区服，就是你以后一直玩的区服。

What not to spend yet现在还不该花的东西

Primegy is a budget rather than a savings account, and two of the things you are handed early look expendable and are not.Primegy 是预算，不是存款。另外，开局给你的东西里有两样看着像一次性消耗品，其实不是。

  • Primegy is a budget, not savings. It ticks back one point every six minutes and stops at 300, and whatever is sitting at the cap when you stop playing is simply gone — a full meter is a meter you are wasting. Claims are what spend it: 30 for an Alpha, and the same pool pays for crafting and for spare Aniipods at your base. Evolution materials come off Alpha kills, so this is also what paces your evolution line.Primegy 是预算，不是存款。 每六分钟回一点，到 300 就停，停在那儿的时候没花掉的部分就是白扔的，满格的能量条等于在浪费。花它的地方是领奖励：Alpha 一次 30，基地合成和额外造 Aniipod 用的是同一个池子。进化材料也是从 Alpha 身上掉的，所以这条线同时还卡着你进化的节奏。
  • A throw that misses costs you nothing. An Aniipod that does not connect comes back to you. Only the one that lands leaves your inventory, so the price of a bad throw is the walk back into range rather than the pod.扔空的不花钱。 没砸中的 Aniipod 会自动回到身上，只有砸中的那只才会从背包里消失。所以一次失手的代价是走回去重新找角度，不是那只球。
  • Aniipod Ultra and Sparkling Cube never fail. Ultra lands the catch and sets Potential to at least Elite. The Sparkling Cube lands the catch, sets Potential to Perfect, and forces the Sparkling appearance. Both sit in the catch-rate table as guaranteed, which means none of the multiplier maths — back attacks, Break, the level gap — applies to them. Throw them at something you intend to keep.Aniipod Ultra 和 Sparkling Cube 从不失手。 Ultra 保底捕获，潜力至少 Elite；Sparkling Cube 保底捕获，潜力固定 Perfect，而且必定是 Sparkling 外观。这两个在捕捉率表里都是保底项，背面偷袭、Break、等级差那些倍率对它们没有意义。要扔就扔给你打算长期养的。
  • Do not close the Alpha capture screen. Once an Alpha is down, leaving the capture interface voids the material rewards and does not refund the Primegy the attempt cost.打完 Alpha 别关捕捉界面。 Alpha 倒下之后再退出捕捉界面，材料奖励全部作废，那次尝试花的 Primegy 也不退。
  • Battle stats are not the whole animal. Mobility is listed per species rather than shared across the roster, so a plain-looking Aniimo can still be the one that gets you over a cliff or across water.别只看战斗数值决定留不留一只。 移动能力是按物种单独给的，不是全图鉴共用，一只看着平平的 Aniimo，可能正是能带你翻山崖、过水面的那只。
