Aniimo Egg Heist PvP: Hypnyto, and One Mantis for Each Opponent《伊莫》抢蛋大作战 PVP：迷瞳翼带两螳螂

A four-slot PvP party for Operation: Egg Heist: Hypnyto carries the damage, Fulmintis answers Hypnyto, Ignitis answers Fulmintis, and Glacy keeps the main one standing — plus the habits that get the egg home.一套抢蛋大作战的 PVP 队伍：迷瞳翼打伤害，电刺螳螂专收迷瞳翼、淬刃螳螂专收电刺螳螂，莹冰龙保住主 C —— 再加上把蛋平安搬回去的那几个习惯。

The side that wins an Egg Heist run is rarely the one that fought the hardest — it is the side still standing at the end with the egg in hand. So there are only two things worth preparing: a party that takes a fight against anyone, and a handful of habits that get the egg carried home. Four slots, and the rest is footwork.抢蛋这个模式里，赢的一方很少是打得最凶的那个，多半是最后还站着、蛋还在手上的那个。所以真正要准备的东西就两样：一套遇到谁都敢接的队伍，几个能把蛋平平安安搬回去的习惯。四个位置，剩下的全是走位。

The main slot is Hypnyto (Wind · DPS), with two mantises rotating behind it — one there to answer Hypnyto, one there to answer Fulmintis — and a healer in the fourth. The two mantises are not filler: you switch to whichever one the other side just brought out.主战是迷瞳翼（Hypnyto，风 · 输出），后面两只螳螂轮换 —— 一只专收迷瞳翼，一只专收电刺螳螂 —— 第四格放治疗。两只螳螂不是凑数的：对面出什么，你就换哪一只。

What is left is all about where you stand. Mark your own base before anything else, use the wind field to travel when the stamina bar is empty, climb a wall when the fight has gone against you, and once the egg is in hand take it somewhere awkward and high.剩下的都是走位上的事。开局先给自家标个点，没体力的时候靠风场赶路，打不动就上墙，拿到蛋就往那些奇奇怪怪的高点上搬。

The party: one main that stays up, two mantises that answer配队：主 C 站住，两只螳螂管克制

The main damage is Hypnyto (Wind · DPS), and the whole party's damage sits on him. The support slots are Ignitis (Dark · DPS) and Fulmintis (Lightning · DPS), with Glacy (Water · Ice · Heal) covering the fourth. Three support slots made of two mantises and one heal is the shape that keeps the main damage dealer alive and always leaves a counter to switch to.主 C 是迷瞳翼，风属性输出，整队的伤害都压在他身上。支援位放淬刃螳螂（Ignitis，暗 · 输出）和电刺螳螂（Fulmintis，电 · 输出），再让莹冰龙（Glacy，水 · 冰 · 治疗）顶一个治疗位。三个支援位里两只螳螂、一个回复，这个结构就是为了让主 C 一直有人保，也一直有克制手可以换。

The reason for two mantises is coverage. PvE is built the same way: Hypnyto in the main slot, plus a Fulmintis or an Ignitis depending on what you are walking into. When Hoptail arrives, this party gets updated once more.带两只螳螂，是为了把克制位补齐。PVE 里基本也是这么配：主 C 迷瞳翼，配一只电刺螳螂或者淬刃螳螂，看对面是什么。等蹦蹦貂出来，这套还会再更新一遍。

The counter slot: whoever they bring, you bring the answer克制位：对面换谁，你就换谁

Two rules, and that is the whole of it. Against Hypnyto, play Fulmintis. Against Fulmintis, play Ignitis. One mantis handles one opponent; whichever one is out, the other waits below it.规矩就两条。遇到迷瞳翼，用电刺螳螂打；遇到电刺螳螂，用淬刃螳螂打。一只螳螂管一种对手，谁上场，另一只就在下面等着。

In practice it is direct. When Fulmintis comes at you there is no need to back off: hold it with Hypnyto first, then switch to Ignitis and finish it head-on. The moment it shows itself, chase it — do not give it the gap to reposition. See Hypnyto and you switch to Fulmintis and chase it the same way.打起来其实很直接。电刺螳螂冲上来不用退，先用迷瞳翼控住，接着切淬刃螳螂正面打死；它一露头，直接追着打，别给它换位的空当。看见迷瞳翼就切电刺螳螂追着打。

First thing in the match: mark your own base开局先给自家插个点

The first thing you do after the doors open is put a marker on your own base. When the route to the extraction point gets messy, or you get scattered apart mid-run, that marker is what gets you home — without it you circle the same ground looking for it. It costs a few seconds and pays out for the rest of the match.进局之后第一件事，给自己家标个点。运蛋的时候路一乱、被人打散，回头找家全靠它，不用在原地绕圈。插个点花不了几秒，整局都在还。

The wind field: a free leg, and it carries the egg风场：不花体力的腿，还能把蛋带走

Hypnyto's exploration skill is a wind field, and its most useful property is that moving with it costs no stamina. When the bar is on empty, that is how you travel, and how you chase an egg down.迷瞳翼的探索技能是风场。它最实用的地方是不消耗体力就能移动 —— 体力条见底的时候，赶路靠它，追蛋也靠它。

It carries the egg with it, too. That is convenient when you are the one chasing and just as convenient when you are the one being chased — push it and the egg takes off into the air with you.风场还能连蛋一起带着走。追人的时候顺手，被追的时候也顺手，急起来甚至能让蛋直接飞起来。

Walls and high ground are both a way out墙上和顶上，都是活路

When the buffs are gone and the fight is not going your way, climb a wall. From up there, anyone who wanted to finish you has to find a new angle, and often enough they simply stop coming. Do not treat a wall as a safe box, though — some ranged attacks still reach you.状态没了、正面打不动的时候，可以往墙上爬。爬上去了，一些想过来收你的人就得重新找角度，有时候干脆就不追了。但别把墙当保险箱，有些远程照样打得到你。

With the egg, go the other way — up. The awkward high points are often out of reach for melee Aniimo entirely; something like a mantis can be nudged off the edge just by moving along it.反过来，拿到蛋以后要往高处走。那些奇奇怪怪的高点，近战伊莫有时候根本够不着。像螳螂这种，贴着边缘稍微动一动，它自己就掉下去了。

Three on one: wait until they are all there一打三：等他们凑齐再动手

Your team goes down first and three of them come after you together. Running will not work at that point. Do not spend your skills early — let them all close in, hold the group with Hypnyto's control, then switch to Ignitis and take all three in one go.队友先倒了，三个人一起追过来，这时候跑是跑不掉的。别急着交技能，等他们都凑到身边，迷瞳翼一个大控先按住，再切淬刃螳螂一口气收掉三个。

Remember the order and that is enough把顺序记住就够了

Set the party up first: Hypnyto in the middle, two mantises switched by what the other side puts out. Mark your base as the match opens. Travel on the wind field when the stamina bar is empty, and chase eggs with it. Climb a wall when you cannot win the exchange, and come back down once you have recovered. Take the egg high rather than trading health on flat ground. And if three of them do close in, hold still and wait until they are all there.先把队伍配好：迷瞳翼站中间，两只螳螂看对面换。开局第一件事是插点。没体力用风场，追蛋也用它。打不过就上墙，缓过来再下来。拿到蛋往高处搬，别在平地上跟人换血。真被三个人围住，稳住，等他们凑齐再动手。
