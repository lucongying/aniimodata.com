Fentuft天使耶

Electric电 DPS输出

Fentuft has a single form recorded so far. Its stats, type, habitat and evolution requirement are below.天使耶 目前只记录到一个形态。它的数值、属性、栖息地和进化条件都在下面。

Overview简介

Fentuft is a lightning species carrying the strangest power figure in the index: Thunder Field prints 1 power, because what it does is not damage — it makes every lightning attack that lands inside it trigger a second one. So the species' real damage card is Lightning Dart at 50, and the Field is a multiplier you place before it.天使耶是一个雷系物种，带着图鉴里最奇怪的一个威力数字：雷电场写着 1 威力 —— 因为它做的不是伤害：它让每一次落在场内的电元素攻击再触发一次。所以这个物种真正的输出卡是 50 威力的闪电飞镖，而电场是你在它之前先布置好的那个倍率。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Scurry疾冲

A short forward dash. Short is the operative word — this is a repositioning tool, not travel. Use it to close a gap or break a line, not to cross a map.向前快速冲一小段。重点在「一小段」：它是位移手段，不是赶路手段 —— 用来贴身或者拉开距离，不是用来跑图的。

Trait特性

Thunderbond空雷契约

Every attack that connects adds a stack of Thunderbond, and at 30 stacks the next skill or Ultimate it uses puts it into Thunderwing Stance for 10 seconds. Thirty is high enough that this is a trait built up across a fight rather than one that fires early, and which button springs it is your call.每次攻击命中叠 1 层【空雷契约】，叠到 30 层时，接下来释放的技能或终结技会让它进入【雷翼姿态】，持续 10 秒。30 层不算少 —— 这是一个要在战斗里慢慢攒的特性，不是开局就亮的；而由技能还是终结技来触发，是你自己决定的。

    A field that follows the target for 8 seconds, and while it holds, every lightning attack that lands on a target inside it triggers an extra lightning attack plus a stack of Lightning Debuff, at most once every 0.2 seconds. So it multiplies lightning damage rather than dealing damage of its own — and because it travels with the enemy, walking away is not an option.一片持续跟随目标的雷电场，存在 8 秒；期间电场内目标每受到一次电元素攻击，就会额外再挨一次电元素攻击并叠一层【电元素异常】，最多每 0.2 秒触发一次。所以它是对电系伤害的放大器，本身不是伤害来源 —— 而且它跟着敌人走，躲是躲不掉的。
