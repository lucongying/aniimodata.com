Progression进阶流程

Three tracks run at once once the story stops holding your hand — the regions you open, the level cap that holds you, and the area badges the promotions quietly ask for.主线不再手把手教你之后，有三条线同时在走 —— 你能开的地区、卡住你的等级上限，以及晋升任务悄悄问你要的地区徽章。

The order the world opens世界打开的顺序

Not one band below comes from the studio. The official wiki is a creature index and nothing else — 209 pages, every one of them an Aniimo — and the official site carries news, activities and the probability tables. Neither documents a single region. The bands here are the habitat tables on wikily.gg and gameplay.tips, which agree on every row; one smaller fan database, aniimotools.dev, prints different numbers for five of the seven regions it covers. Treat the early rows as the least settled part of this table.下面没有一个区间来自官方。官方 wiki 就是个生物图鉴 —— 209 个页面全是 Aniimo —— 官网只有新闻、活动和概率表，两边都没写过任何一个地区。这里的区间出自 wikily.gg 和 gameplay.tips 的栖息地表，两家的每一行都对得上；另有一个较小的同人数据库 aniimotools.dev，它收录的七个地区里有五个数字不一样。前面几行是这张表里最不牢靠的部分。

Read these as a neighbourhood rather than a corridor. Berylline Causeway and Blitzwood open in the same range; Rosetower Woods finishes five levels higher than Russet Highlands although it comes first in the promotion order; and no source prints a canonical order of area unlocks at all — the order above is put together from the story chapters and the promotion regions, not quoted from anywhere. Nor does anything physically stop you walking into a late region early. What stops you is a cap or a quest that has greyed out, not a wall. The map carries one more area name, 夕阳海原, that no habitat table lists with a band, so it has no row here.把这张表读成一片区域，不是一条走廊。青石台和闪电之森在同一区间开；玫瑰塔林的等级比丹岩高地还高五级，在晋升顺序里却排在前面；而且没有任何来源印过一份权威的地区解锁顺序 —— 上面这个顺序是由剧情章节和晋升地区拼出来的，不是从哪里抄的。也没有任何东西真的拦着你提前走进后期地区：拦你的是等级上限或者变灰的任务，不是一堵墙。地图上还有一个地区名 夕阳海原，没有任何栖息地表给它区间，所以这里没有它的行。

Area badges — the spine that actually gates you地区徽章 —— 真正卡住你的那条线

Every charted area carries its own four-tier badge series, and this is the track that catches people out, because none of it looks compulsory until a promotion asks for it by name. Three tiers are named Bronze, Silver and Gold; the fourth is called Legendary on one database and Prismatic on another, so the tier is described here rather than named.每个有数据的地区都有自己的一套四档徽章，而这条线最容易把人卡住 —— 因为在某个晋升任务点名要它之前，它看上去完全是可选的。三档叫 Bronze、Silver、Gold；第四档在一个数据库里叫 Legendary，在另一个里叫 Prismatic，所以这里只描述它，不给它起名。

Two of these matter more than the rest, and neither is optional. The Wayfarer II promotion — the one that lifts your cap to 50 — is written up with badge collection as its main requirement rather than a fight, and Elite Training Chapter 3 includes badge objectives. So a player who skips badges does not stall on experience points; they stall on a requirement no quest log spells out. Two rows in the source data also break the pattern instead of repeating it: Driftwise Meadow's Silver badge asks for no Alpha kill, and its Gold badge wants 3 Lumin Amber where every other area wants 5. The fourth tier's own listing names unlocking the badge as one of its requirements, which is a transcription slip rather than a step.其中两档比其余重要得多，而且都不是可选项。Wayfarer II 的晋升 —— 就是把你上限提到 50 的那次 —— 写出来的主要要求是收徽章而不是打一架，而精英训练第 3 章的目标里也有徽章。所以跳过徽章的人不是卡在经验上，是卡在一条任务日志里根本不写的要求上。来源数据里还有两行是打破规律而不是重复规律的：雪麓草甸的 Silver 徽章不要求打 Alpha，它的 Gold 徽章要 3 个 Lumin Amber，而其他所有地区都要 5 个。第四档自己那条列表里，把「解锁这个徽章」写成了它的解锁条件之一 —— 那是抄录时串行了，不是一个步骤。

The Branch, and why Amber is region-lockedBranch，以及为什么 Amber 是锁地区的

Lumin Amber is the one currency the game locks to a place: what you pick up in Nimbus Fields feeds the Nimbus Fields Branch and nothing else, and there is no way to move it. Infuse what you have before you leave, because the alternative is walking back. Change Weather needs three things rather than one: 60 Amber infused at the Branch, that area's Silver badge, and a Prismana Crystal for every Flow you call. And in the current build, rain, snow and thunderstorms appear in the menu but are greyed out — the only weather you can actually call is a Prismana Flow, up to three times a day with a one-minute cooldown. What opens, and when什么在什么时候开

Aniimo's systems do not open on a level curve. They open on three separate gates — your title, your character level, and how far you are through Elite Training — and most of the late ones want a combination. This is the earliest each one becomes available, with the caveats left attached rather than filed off.Aniimo 的系统不是按等级曲线开的，是按三道各自独立的门槛开的 —— 你的头衔、你的角色等级、你的精英训练进度 —— 而后期那些大多要三者凑齐。下面是每一个最早能拿到的时点，附带条件一并留着，没有磨平。

Before you leave a region离开一个地区之前

Almost everything you would have to walk back for is on a timer or a one-off, and the game announces none of it. The short list:需要你走回头路的事，几乎都卡在计时器或者一次性机会上，而游戏一件都不会提前告诉你。短清单如下：

  • Infuse your Amber before you go. It is region-locked, and the Branch is the only place it goes.走之前把 Amber 注进去。 它是锁地区的，而 Branch 是它唯一的去处。
  • Push every area to 40% before you move on. That is the Bronze badge, and it is what turns on Nurture and the +10% catch rate there. 70% plus the area's Alpha gets you Silver, which is the tier the Wayfarer II promotion asks for by name.换地区之前先把每个地区推到 40%。 那就是 Bronze 徽章，是它打开了培育和该地区 +10% 的捕捉率。70% 加上该地区的 Alpha 给 Silver，而 Wayfarer II 的晋升点名要的就是这一档。
  • Report your catches at the Outpost. Research points turn into character experience and into accessory slots for that species, and the Research Expert talent raises what the same reporting pays by up to 30%.到 Outpost 去交捕捉报告。 研究点会变成角色经验，也会变成该物种的饰品槽；而 Research Expert 这个天赋能把同一份报告给的钱提高最多 30%。
  • Keep one of every species you catch. Quests and exploration routes later ask for specific Aniimo, and releasing one early means going back for another.每种留一只。 后面的任务和探索路线会点名要特定的 Aniimo，早放生一只就等于再回来抓一只。
  • Take the one-time rewards while you are standing there. Alpha first clears, Sanctum first clears and badge milestones do not come round again.趁人还在，把一次性的奖励拿掉。 Alpha 首杀、Sanctum 首通、徽章里程碑都不会再来第二次。
  • Do not spend the free Prismana Pact blind. It is a single pick, claimed at Wayfarer I. Choose something you will still be using at the end, and if you are holding a Sparkling Cube, spend that on the same creature.别闭着眼睛花掉那个免费的 Prismana Pact。 它只能选一次，在 Wayfarer I 领。挑一只你到后期还会用的；如果你手上还留着 Sparkling Cube，就砸在同一只身上。
  • Leave the irreversible things until you are sure. Releasing an Aniimo is permanent; evolution stage, star rank and innate potential cannot be changed afterwards; and the prologue's choice between Helion and Lunara locks the other one out for good.不可逆的事，想清楚再做。 放生是永久的；进化阶段、星级、Innate Potential 之后都改不了；而序章在 Helion 和 Lunara 之间的选择，会让另一只永远拿不到。

The level ceiling等级上限

Every promotion raises your cap and your Aniimo's cap together, and the caps the promotions move are 25, 35, 40, 45, 50, 55 and 60 — with 60 belonging to Trailblazer I, the last title the official probability tables name.
