Pomegg拉拉蛋

Grass草 Break击破 B

Pomegg has 4 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.拉拉蛋 共有 4 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Pomegg is a grass BREAK species with four cards: a 45-power grass hit, a cheaper 36-power combo, a Frost Strike that leaves what it touches Frozen for 2.5 seconds, and the basic attack. The freeze is what makes the species work — an unconditional area freeze is exactly what a break species wants on the turn before it goes at a guard bar.拉拉蛋是一个草系击破物种，四张卡：一发 45 威力的草系打击、一套更便宜的 36 威力连击、一次让命中的目标 2.5 秒【冰冻】的霜击，加上基础攻击。真正让这个物种成立的是那次冰冻 —— 一个不带条件的范围冰冻，正是击破型物种在动护盾条之前那一回合想要的东西。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Tuning调音

Each cast adds a stack of Volume, to 6; at 6 stacks it gains +20% critical rate for 15 seconds. The stacks are only ever read at the top, so the ramp is the real cost — the first five casts buy nothing on their own.每释放 1 次技能叠 1 层【音量】，最多 6 层；叠满后暴击率 +20%，持续 15 秒。层数只在满层时才被读取，所以「攒」这件事本身就是成本 —— 前五次释放单独看什么都不给。

Skills技能
