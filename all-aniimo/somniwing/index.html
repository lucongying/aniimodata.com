Somniwing美梦蝶

Wind风Grass草 Regen回能

Somniwing has a single form recorded so far. Its stats, type, habitat and evolution requirement are below.美梦蝶 目前只记录到一个形态。它的数值、属性、栖息地和进化条件都在下面。

Overview简介

Somniwing is one of the two Legendary Aniimo, and the support half of that pair: wind/grass on the sheet, grass alone in the kit — all five skill cards are grass, so its first element never comes up in combat. 538 total, in a support's shape: 111 HP and 115 REGEN against 92 ATK, with both defences at 85. There is no basic form to compare against and no habitat to point at, and it does not arrive through a Wild Surge — it comes from 180 points in the Path of Twining Elite Program. What the kit does is three things: hold a target still, hand energy back to itself, and leave the team a lane of EP recovery.美梦蝶是两只传说伊莫之一，也是其中偏辅助的那一只：表面上 风/草，技能组里只有草 —— 五张技能卡全是草元素，所以第一个属性在实战里根本不会出现。总属性 538，是辅助的形状：111 生命、115 回能，对上 92 攻击，两防同为 85。它没有基础形态可以对照、没有栖息地可写，也不走 Wild Surge —— 是在 Path of Twining 精英计划里攒够 180 点来的。技能组干三件事：把目标按住、把能量还给自己、给队伍留一条回能的通道。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

At a glance一眼速览

Mobility探索技能

Fly Lv.2飞行 Lv.2

An action ability whose whole content is the level: Fly Lv. 2. The card states no speed, no duration and no energy cost, and the English wiki heads it Pathfinding rather than naming a movement state — so read it as how this species gets around, not as something you switch on.一项行动能力，而它的全部内容就是这个等级：飞行 Lv.2。卡上没写速度、没写持续时间、也没写消耗，而英文维基给这张卡的标题是 Pathfinding、不是某个移动状态的名字 —— 所以把它当成「这个物种怎么移动」，而不是一个能打开的状态。

Trait特性

Energy Full能量充盈

Max EP +10, flat and unconditional — the trait does nothing else. Read it against the kit's prices rather than on its own: 20 for Hypnotic Spores, 10 for Energy Orb plus up to 40 more while charging. What a higher ceiling buys here is one more cast before the bar is empty.能量上限 +10，无条件、无触发，特性就这一件事。要把它放在技能组的价格里看，而不是单独看：催眠孢子 20、能量球起手 10 再加最多 40 的蓄力。在这里，一个更高的上限买到的是「条空之前还能再放一次」。

Skills技能

Split the way the official page splits them: Combat holds the skills you cast, Innate the basic attack. The list is the species', not the form's — a form does not change it.分法和官方页面一致：战斗技能是你会主动放的那几个，固有技能里是基础攻击。这份表属于物种，不属于形态 —— 换形态不会换掉它。

    Sleep for 5 seconds, +25% damage taken while it holds, and any damage ends it early. Against grass it does nothing at all, so there it is a 20-cost 28-power card and no more. Everywhere else the question is order: the sleep is worth 25% to the *next* hit, which means this wants to go before the expensive card, not after it. Whether its own 28 damage counts as damage taken and wakes the target on the same cast is not stated.睡眠 5 秒、期间受到的伤害 +25%，任何一次伤害都会提前解除。对草元素完全无效 —— 打草队时它就是一张 20 能耗、28 威力的普通卡。除此之外，问题在顺序：睡眠值的是下一发那 25%，所以它要排在贵的那一发前面，而不是后面。至于它自己这 28 点伤害算不算「受到伤害」、会不会在同一发里把目标打醒，卡上没有写。

    30 power for 10 energy, chargeable to 180 — 6× — by holding. Each 0.6 seconds of charge spends 10 more and adds a stage, four on top of the base, so a full one costs 50. The five stages run 1/2/3/4.5/6× of the base, which makes the first two charges worth 30 damage for 10 energy — exactly the rate of an uncharged cast — and the last two worth 45 for the same 10. The top of the bar is the cheap end, not the expensive one.10 能耗打 30 威力，按住最多蓄到 6 倍、180。每 0.6 秒多花 10 点加一段，基础之上最多四段，所以满蓄一共 50 点。五段分别是基础威力的 1/2/3/4.5/6 倍：也就是说前两次蓄力是花 10 点换 30 点伤害，和不蓄力时一模一样；后两次同样的 10 点换的是 45 点。所以这根条越往上越便宜，不是越往上越贵。
