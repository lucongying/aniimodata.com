Privacy Policy隐私政策

What AniimoData does with your data — which, on a site with no accounts, no forms and no comments, is very little.AniimoData 会拿你的数据做什么 —— 在一个没有账号、没有表单、也没有评论的站上，可说的很少。

Last updated: October 6, 2026最后更新：2026 年 10 月 6 日

1. Overview1. 概述

This policy explains what AniimoData (aniimodata.com) does with your data. The short version is that the site has no accounts, no forms and no comments, so there is very little to explain. The longer version is below.这一页说明 AniimoData（aniimodata.com）会拿你的数据做什么。短的说：本站没有账号、没有表单、也没有评论，所以可说的很少。长的在下面。

2. About This Site2. 本站是什么

AniimoData is an unofficial, reader-supported reference site for the game Aniimo, published at aniimodata.com. Every page is static: the whole site is pre-built files, with no server-side application, no database and no login holding your data.AniimoData 是一个非官方、由读者支持的 Aniimo 资料站，发布在 aniimodata.com。每一页都是静态的：整站都是预先构建好的文件，没有服务端程序、没有数据库、也没有登录，不会存着你的数据。

3. What We Do Not Collect3. 我们不收集什么

We do not ask for, and do not store:我们不索取、也不保存：

  • your name, email address or any other contact details你的姓名、邮箱地址或任何其他联系方式
  • an account, a password or a profile of any kind账号、密码或任何形式的个人资料
  • payment or billing information — the site sells nothing支付或账单信息 —— 本站不卖任何东西
  • anything you type, because there is nothing on the site to type into你输入的任何内容，因为本站没有任何可以输入的地方

4. Information Handled Automatically4. 自动处理的信息

Like any website, ours is served by a hosting provider, and that provider's servers keep standard request logs. These typically record your IP address, the browser and operating system you used, the page you asked for, and the time you asked for it.和任何网站一样，本站由托管商提供服务，托管商的服务器会记录标准的访问日志。这些日志通常包含你的 IP 地址、你使用的浏览器和操作系统、你请求的页面，以及请求的时间。

Those logs belong to the hosting provider and are used to keep the site online and secure. We do not use them to build a profile of you.这些日志属于托管商，用于维持站点在线和保障安全。我们不会用它们给你建立画像。

5. Cookies and Local Storage5. Cookie 与本地存储

AniimoData sets no cookies of its own. If you switch the site's language, that choice is remembered in your browser's local storage — a small value kept on your own device, sent to nobody, and cleared when you clear your browser data.AniimoData 自己不设置任何 Cookie。如果你切换了站点语言，这个选择会记在你浏览器的本地存储里 —— 一个只存在你自己设备上的小值，不会发给任何人，清空浏览器数据时一并清除。

Cookies may also be set by third parties — fonts, advertising and analytics. That is what the next three sections are about.Cookie 也可能由第三方设置 —— 字体、广告、分析统计。接下来三节说的就是这件事。

6. Third-Party Services6. 第三方服务

The site loads its web fonts from Google Fonts. Asking for a font means your browser connects to Google's servers, and Google receives your IP address and the page you were on when you asked. What Google does with that is governed by Google's own privacy policy.本站从 Google Fonts 加载网页字体。请求字体意味着你的浏览器要连上 Google 的服务器，Google 会收到你的 IP 地址，以及你发出请求时所在的页面。Google 拿这些做什么，由 Google 自己的隐私政策管。

7. Advertising7. 广告

AniimoData may display advertising, to cover the cost of running it. Where advertising is shown, it is served by third-party networks:AniimoData 可能会展示广告，用来抵掉维持站点的开销。在展示广告的地方，广告由第三方广告网络投放：

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and elsewhere on the internet.第三方厂商，包括 Google，会使用 Cookie，根据你此前对本站及其他网站的访问来投放广告。Google 使用广告 Cookie，使 Google 及其合作伙伴能够根据你访问本站以及互联网上其他站点的记录，向你投放广告。

You can opt out of personalised advertising in your Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them less relevant to you.你可以在 Google 广告设置里关闭个性化广告，也可以在 aboutads.info 关闭第三方厂商使用 Cookie 做个性化广告。关闭并不会让广告消失，只是让它们与你关系更小。

8. Analytics8. 分析统计

The site runs Google Analytics 4, an analytics service from Google, to see which pages are read and which are not. It is used in aggregate only: page views, the country or region a visit came from, the kind of device and browser it came from, and how the reader arrived. It is never used to identify an individual reader, and there is nothing here to identify you with — no account, no form, no comment.本站使用 Google Analytics 4（Google 提供的分析统计服务），用来看哪些页面有人读、哪些没有。它只以汇总的形式使用：页面浏览量、一次访问大致来自哪个国家或地区、来自什么样的设备和浏览器，以及读者是从哪里过来的。它绝不用来识别某一个读者，本站也没有能识别你的东西 —— 没有账号、没有表单、没有评论。

Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to tell one visit from the next. Google does not log the IP address of a visit, and we do not combine the analytics data with anything else. What Google does with what it collects is governed by Google's own privacy policy.Google Analytics 会在你的浏览器里设置 Cookie，用来区分这一次访问和下一次。Google 不记录访问的 IP 地址，我们也不会把分析数据与别的信息拼在一起。Google 怎么处理它收到的数据，由 Google 自己的隐私政策管。

If you would rather not be counted, the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on switches it off across every site that uses Google Analytics, and blocking third-party cookies or browsing in private mode will also stop it. The site shows no cookie banner, so this paragraph is the notice.如果你不想被计入，可以安装 Google Analytics 停用浏览器插件，它对所有使用 Google Analytics 的网站一并生效；屏蔽第三方 Cookie 或用浏览器的无痕模式也能挡住。本站不弹 Cookie 同意横幅，这一段就是告知。

9. Children's Privacy9. 儿童隐私

This is a game reference site and it is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included — there is no mechanism on the site that would let us.本站是游戏资料站，不面向 13 岁以下的儿童。我们不会在知情的情况下收集任何人的个人信息，儿童也一样 —— 站上没有任何能让我们收集的机制。

10. Your Rights10. 你的权利

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR give you the right to see, correct, delete and move the personal data an organisation holds about you, and to object to how it is used. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to hand over, correct or erase. A request about data held by Google should go to Google.GDPR 等数据保护法律赋予你查看、更正、删除和迁移一家机构所持有的你的个人数据，以及反对其使用方式的权利。因为我们不持有你的任何个人数据，所以没有什么可以交给你、更正或删除。涉及 Google 所持数据的请求，应当向 Google 提出。

11. Changes to This Policy11. 本政策的变更

This policy may be updated — when a third-party service is added, for example. The date at the top of this page is the date of the version you are reading, and a change takes effect when it is posted.本政策可能会更新 —— 比如新增一个第三方服务时。页顶的日期就是你正在读的这一版的日期，改动一经发布即生效。
