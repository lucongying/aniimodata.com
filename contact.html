How to Reach Us怎么找我们

This is the only address the site answers at. A correction is the most useful thing you can send: tell us which entry, what it says now and what it should say, and we can check it against the source and fix it. Mail reaches one person rather than a desk, so give us a few days.本站只有这一个联系地址。最有用的来信是纠错：告诉我们哪一条、现在写的是什么、应该是什么，我们就能去对着来源核一遍再改。邮件到一个具体的人手上，不是工单队列，所以请给我们几天时间。 lucongying99@gmail.com

Corrections内容纠错

A stat, a type, a habitat or an evolution step that is wrong or out of date — the most useful mail we get.数值、属性、栖息地或某个进化步骤写错或过期了 —— 这是对我们最有用的来信。

Copyright版权

Anything of yours that appears here, or a takedown request. Tell us what and where, and we will act on it.本站出现了属于你的内容，或者你要发下架通知。告诉我们是什么、在哪一页，我们会处理。

Everything Else其他一切

A Few Notes几点说明

  • We cannot help with game accounts, purchases, refunds, customer support or event compensation — none of that is ours to do anything about. Those belong with Aniimo's official support channel.游戏账号、充值、退款、客服和活动补偿我们都帮不上 —— 这些不归我们管，请走 Aniimo 官方客服渠道。
  • For a question about the game itself, check the FAQ and the database first. Most of the mail we get is already answered on one of those two pages, and it will be quicker than waiting on us.如果是关于游戏本身的问题，先看看常见问题和数据库。我们收到的邮件里，大部分在这两页上已经有答案了，查一下比自己等回信快。
