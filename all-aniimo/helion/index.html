Helion日耀狮

Holy光 DPS输出 B

Helion has a single form recorded so far. Its stats, type, habitat and evolution requirement are below.日耀狮 目前只记录到一个形态。它的数值、属性、栖息地和进化条件都在下面。

Overview简介

Helion is the light counterpart to Lunara and the species carrying Solar Grace: Holy Storm pulls targets toward its centre while dealing BREAK damage scaled to its level, and with Solar Halo up the final hit adds a 1-second stun; Sun-Kissed restores 20 energy plus 5 more under the same ring. Two of its cards read the ring and two do not, so how much of the kit you get depends on keeping it active.日耀狮是月辉狐的光属性对应物，也是带着太阳之力的物种：圣光风暴一边把目标往中心吸，一边造成按自身等级计算的击破伤害，而身上有【金日之环】时最后一段额外眩晕 1 秒；沐光回复 20 点能量，在同一个环下再多回 5 点。它有两张卡读那个环、两张不读 —— 所以你能拿到多少，取决于你有没有让它一直挂着。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Hustle疾行

Holds a faster run for as long as it is on, and drains stamina the whole time. It is a state, not a dash — letting go is what stops it, and the cost is per second rather than per use. Staying in it to cross a long stretch is the expensive way to travel.开着的时候一直保持更快的移动速度，全程持续消耗体力。它是一个状态，不是一次冲刺 —— 松开才停，代价按秒算，不按次数算。想靠它跑长距离，是最费体力的走法。

Trait特性

Solar Grace太阳之力

When it is not hitting an elemental weakness, its final damage goes up 30% — a trait that pays out on the wrong matchup. Separately, every 4 basic attacks grants Solar Halo, which strengthens the next skill. Both halves point the same way: on this species the basic attack is not filler.在打不出元素克制的时候，最终伤害反而提高 30% —— 这个特性补偿的正是错的对位。另外，每 4 次普攻获得【金日之环】，强化下一次技能。两半指向同一件事：在这个物种身上，普攻不是填充。
