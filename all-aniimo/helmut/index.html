Helmut大眼盔

Dark暗 Break击破

Helmut has 3 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.大眼盔 共有 3 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Helmut has the lowest stat total in the index — the kit that goes with it is defensive, and reads that way: a Defense Mode, two 45-power guard-breaking slams in dark and ice, and the basic attack. It sits at the top of its own tree with nothing above it, so the two stage-3 species below are what those numbers turn into.大眼盔拥有全图鉴最低的种族值总和 —— 配套的技能组是防守向的，从卡面也读得出来：一张防御模式、两发 45 威力的破防重击（暗与冰各一），加上基础攻击。它待在自己那棵树的顶端、上面没有别人，所以它下面那两个三阶物种，就是这组数字最终会变成的样子。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Detection探查视野

Swaps your view for a detective's one that picks out metal objects and cloaked Aniimo nearby. It finds things rather than moving you, and it is the only exploration skill here that answers a question instead of solving a distance.把视野换成探查视野，能看见周围的金属物品和隐身的伊莫。它的作用不是位移，是找东西 —— 这里是唯一一个用来回答问题的探索技能，不是用来解决距离的。

Trait特性

Guardbreak Resonance防破共鸣

The official card reads BREAK +0. That is the figure as the game prints it, reproduced here rather than guessed at — a value would have to be invented to say anything else, and inventing figures is the one thing this site does not do.官方卡上写的是 击破 +0。这是游戏印出来的数字，原样照抄，不替它补 —— 要说成别的数，就得凭空编一个，而编数字正是这个站不做的事。
