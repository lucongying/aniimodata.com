Party队伍

Team Building队伍搭配

Four Aniimo go into a fight and the game sorts every creature into one of five roles, so a party is always missing one of them. This page is about that arithmetic, what each role's bucket actually looks like in the official stats, and the skill cards that pay the rest of the party instead of themselves.一场仗带四只 Aniimo，而游戏把每个物种都归进五种定位之一，所以任何一支队伍都必然缺一整种定位。这页讲的就是这道算术、每种定位在官方数值里到底长什么样，以及那些「不给自己的、给全队的」技能卡。

Four slots, five roles四个位置，五种定位

Two numbers, and they are both the game's: the battle party holds four Aniimo, and the official index files every one of the 86 species under one of five roles. Nothing lines those two up.两个数字，两个都是游戏的：出战队伍是四只 Aniimo，而官方图鉴把 86 个物种每一个都归进五种定位之一。这两个数对不上。

  • Four creatures, not four players. Both numbers happen to be four and they are different things — four is the party you bring into a fight, and four is also how many players fit into one co-op world. The multiplayer figure is sourced over on the FAQ page; this one is the battle party.是四只生物，不是四个玩家。 两个数字恰好都是四，但它们是两件事 —— 四只是你带进战斗的队伍，四是同一个联机世界里能装下的玩家数。联机那个数字的出处写在常见问题页；这里说的是战斗队伍。
  • Nothing first-party prints the four. It comes from the store listings and from the outlets that have played the game, and the official wiki — which does print a role on all 86 species — gives no party size anywhere we have found. Treat it as well attested rather than as official, and as the same number this site's front page already shows.「四」没有任何第一方来源印过。 它出自商店页面和上手玩过的媒体；官方维基确实给 86 个物种每一个都印了定位，但我们翻不到它印过队伍人数。所以请把它当成「多源印证过」而不是「官方说的」，它和本站首页印的也是同一个数。
  • One role benches every fight. Five roles and four seats has no arrangement that covers all five, and the buckets are not the same size: 29 species are filed DPS and 5 are filed Heal. Which one you drop is the whole decision, and the roster makes it for some parties before you get a say.每一场仗都有一种定位坐板凳。 五种定位、四个位置，没有任何排法能全覆盖；而这五档还不一样大：29 个物种归输出，5 个归治疗。「砍掉哪一个」就是全部决策，而对有些队伍来说，名单在你开口之前就已经替你决定了。

Every figure on this page is the game's. The counts and the averages are ours — worked out at build time from the official stat totals and the skill cards, so they cannot drift away from the dataset the rest of the site reads.这页上每一个数字都是游戏的。计数和均值是我们算的 —— 在构建时从官方数值总量和技能卡里算出来，所以它们不可能和站上其它页面读的同一份数据脱节。

What each role brings, in numbers每种定位带来什么，用数字说

Each row is every species filed under that role, averaged across the six stats the official wiki prints. The range beside each average is the spread inside the bucket, and it is printed because that spread is often wider than the gap between two buckets — a mean over five creatures reads as more precision than it has.每一行是归在该定位下的全部物种，按官方维基印的六项数值取平均。每个均值旁边那个区间是这一档内部的极差；之所以要印出来，是因为它经常比两个档之间的差距还大 —— 五只生物算出来的均值，看起来比它实际有的精度要准。

Read across rather than down and the buckets overlap more than they separate: Support averages 103.9 ATK against DPS's 112.6, and the DPS bucket's own spread runs 88 to 130. So the label tells you where a creature sits at the strong end of its bucket, not that everything else is weaker. Four of the five roles lead the index on a different stat — Break on 94.1 BREAK against 48.7 for DPS, Regen on 107.9 REGEN, Heal on 102.6 HP and 87.4 M.DEF, DPS on 112.6 ATK — and that is the actual argument for filling one of each, rather than any claim about the creatures themselves.横着读而不是竖着读，会发现这几档重叠得比它们分开的多：辅助的 ATK 均值是 103.9，输出是 112.6，而输出这一档自己的极差是 88 到 130。所以这个标签告诉你的是「这只在它自己那一档里排在强的那头」，而不是「别的东西都比较弱」。五种里有四种各自在不同的一项数值上领跑 —— 击破领跑 BREAK（94.1，输出是 48.7）、回能领跑 REGEN（107.9）、治疗领跑生命（102.6）和魔防（87.4）、输出领跑 ATK（112.6）—— 这才是「每种定位各带一只」的真正论据，而不是任何关于物种本身的断言。

DPS输出

The only role that leads on ATK, and the widest bucket in the index at 29 species. It is also the lowest for BREAK and the lowest for both defences: the ATK ceiling of 130 is the highest of any role and the HP ceiling of 104 is the lowest. That is what the label buys and what it costs, not a verdict on how long a DPS lasts in a fight.唯一在 ATK 上领跑的定位，也是全图鉴最大的一档，29 个物种。同时它是 BREAK 最低的一档，也是两个防御都最低的一档：ATK 上限 130 是全定位最高，生命上限 104 是全定位最低。这就是这个标签买到的东西和付出的代价，而不是「输出在场上能活多久」的判决。

Break击破

Where the BREAK stat lives. The mean is 94.1 against 48.7 for DPS, and only DPS is a bigger bucket. It is also the role with the lowest ATK ceiling — 100, the only one that does not reach 110 — and the widest P.DEF spread in the index, 52 to 113.BREAK 这项数值住的地方。 均值 94.1，输出是 48.7；只有输出这一档比它更大。它也是 ATK 上限最低的定位 —— 100，唯一一个够不到 110 的 —— 并且物防极差是全图鉴最宽的，52 到 113。

Support辅助

Not a low-damage bucket, which is the thing the label hides. Its ATK averages 103.9 and its ceiling of 120 is the highest in the index after DPS's; what separates the two is the floor, 68 against 88. Support is also the widest bucket by HP, 70 to 113, which is what a label covering both buffers and off-healers should look like.不是一档低伤害的定位，而这正是标签藏起来的那件事。它的 ATK 均值 103.9，上限 120 仅次于输出；把两者分开的是下限，68 对 88。辅助也是生命极差最宽的一档，70 到 113 —— 一个既装增益手又装半个奶妈的标签，本来就该长这样。

Regen回能

REGEN, by name and by number. A mean of 107.9 is the highest of the five, and the bucket's floor of 93 sits above every other bucket's mean. It is the second-smallest bucket at seven species.REGEN，名字和数字对得上。 均值 107.9 是五档里最高的，而且这一档的下限 93 高于其余任何一档的均值。它是第二小的一档，七个物种。

Heal治疗

Five creatures, and the toughest of them on paper. Heal has the highest HP mean (102.6) and the highest M.DEF mean (87.4), and it is the only role whose ATK ceiling stops below 100. Five of 86 is the scarcest label by a wide margin — the next smallest is Regen at seven — and that scarcity is the practical form of the four-and-five problem: the role you are most likely to be missing is the one the index carries fewest of.五只生物，而且是纸面上最硬的一档。 治疗的生命均值最高（102.6）、魔防均值最高（87.4），也是唯一一个 ATK 上限低于 100 的定位。86 分之 5 是最稀缺的标签，差得很远 —— 下一档是回能的 7 只 —— 而这种稀缺就是「五定位四位置」的实际形态：你最容易缺的那种定位，恰好就是图鉴收得最少的那种。

Filling the four把这四个位置填满

Four decisions, in the order they come up. The short version is the rule the FAQ already gives in one sentence — one of each role, and a second creature of a role rather than a fifth damage dealer. What follows is the arithmetic behind that and the two places where the roster decides for you.四个决定，按它们通常出现的顺序。短版本就是常见问题页已经用一句话给过的那条规则 —— 每种定位各一只，要加就加同定位的第二只，而不是第五个输出。下面讲的是那条规则背后的算术，以及名单替你拿主意的两个地方。

  • Fill the four fighting roles first. DPS for the damage, Break to open the window, Support for the buffs and Regen to keep energy moving, with Heal in the last seat if Support cannot hold the party up. Each of those four leads the index on a different stat in the table above, which is what makes the rule a defensible default rather than an arbitrary one.先把四种战斗定位填上。 输出负责伤害、击破负责把窗口打开、辅助负责增益、回能负责让能量转起来，最后一位留给治疗 —— 前提是辅助撑不住全队。上面那张表里，这四种各自领跑不同的一项数值，所以这条规则是站得住的默认解，而不是随便定的。
  • The scarce roles are Heal and Regen. Twelve of the 86 species carry one of those two labels — five and seven. If the creature you want does not exist in your collection yet, that is the shape of the index rather than a gap in your play, and it is why Heal is the seat most often left empty.稀缺的是治疗和回能。 86 个物种里只有十二个挂这两个标签 —— 五个和七个。如果你想要的那只还没进你的收藏，那是图鉴的形状，不是你玩得不好；也正是这个原因让治疗成为最常空着的那一格。
  • Stacking one role is a trade the game charges for in energy. Two DPS and no Regen looks like more damage; the FAQ puts the cost down to energy, and we cannot show that budget — we hold every card's energy cost and no source gives us a recovery rate to weigh it against. What the skill table below does show is where the energy comes from: the cards that hand a teammate energy or energy recovery belong to Regen and Heal creatures, and none of them is a DPS.叠同一种定位，游戏收的过路费是能量。 两个输出不带回能，看起来伤害更高；常见问题页把代价归到能量上，而这份预算我们给不出来 —— 每张卡的能量消耗我们都有，但没有任何来源给过回能速率来和它对账。下面那张技能表能证明的是能量从哪来：把能量或回能交给队友的卡都挂在回能与治疗的物种身上，一张输出的都没有。
  • The elements are a supply problem before they are a tactics problem. Nine elements spread across 86 creatures is a very uneven split, and the strip below is the whole picture. One line of it is short enough to decide a party on its own.属性首先是供给问题，然后才是战术问题。 九种属性铺在 86 只生物上，分布得非常不匀，下面那条带子就是全貌。其中有一行的短，短到能自己决定一支队伍。

Sorted by role, the shape of this table is the finding: every row belongs to a Support, Heal, Regen or Break creature, and the 29 DPS species contribute none of them. Two DPS cards do name the team — Fenmane's Radiant Dart and Pawney's Ironclad Armament — and neither pays the party anything: one is a healing debuff aimed at the enemy and the other is a self-buff whose value happens to depend on the team's timing. A DPS card that handed the party something would appear in the table above; in this index none does.按定位排一下，这张表的形状本身就是结论：每一行都挂在辅助、治疗、回能或击破的物种身上，29 个输出物种一行都没贡献。确实有两张输出的卡点名了队伍 —— 炎鬃的辐射飞镖和爪爪的铸铁武装 —— 但两张都没给队伍任何东西：一张是冲着敌人去的减疗，另一张是自我强化，只是它的价值恰好取决于队伍的节奏。真有输出卡给队伍发东西，它会出现在上表里；在这份图鉴里，一张都没有。

Reading a candidate看懂一只候选

Everything above is about the party. This is about the one creature whose page you have open, and the two traps in the way the game names things.上面讲的都是队伍。这一节讲你正开着的那一只，以及游戏起名方式埋的两个坑。

The six numbers这六项数值

  • HP and ATK are what they look like, and P.DEF and M.DEF are physical and magical defence.生命和攻击就是字面意思，物防和魔防是物理防御和魔法防御。
  • BREAK is a stat, not the Break role. It decides how fast a target's break gauge fills, so it matters most on the creatures whose job is opening that window — which is why the Break bucket leads it and the DPS bucket trails it.BREAK 是一项数值，不是「击破」这个定位。 它决定目标的架势条涨得多快，所以它最要紧的地方正是「负责把窗口打开」的那些生物身上 —— 这也是击破档领跑它、输出档垫底的原因。
  • REGEN is a stat, not the Regen role. It governs how much energy the creature returns, which is why the Regen bucket leads it.REGEN 是一项数值，不是「回能」这个定位。 它管这只生物回多少能量，所以回能档领跑它。
  • None of the six is a rating. No source we hold gives damage as a formula over them, so nothing on this site adds them up or ranks a creature by its total. The role table above is per-stat arithmetic, and it stops there.六项里没有一项是评分。 我们手上的来源没有一份给出「以它们为自变量的伤害公式」，所以站上任何地方都不把它们加起来、也不按总和给生物排名。上面那张定位表是逐项算术，它就停在那里。

Two names, used twice两个名字，各用两次

  • Break is a role and BREAK is a stat. A creature can be filed under Break and carry a low BREAK number; the two sit on the same page and are unrelated.Break 是定位，BREAK 是数值。 一只生物可以归在击破档里而 BREAK 数值很低；两者印在同一页上，但互不相干。
  • Regen is a role and REGEN is a stat. Same shape, same trap.Regen 是定位，REGEN 是数值。 同一个形状，同一个坑。
  • In Chinese the collision is total. The Break role and the BREAK stat are both 击破, and the Regen role and the REGEN stat are both 回能 — the same two characters, with nothing in the words to tell them apart. The English at least capitalises the stat.中文侧这两个名字撞得比英文彻底。 击破既是定位也是 BREAK 数值，回能既是定位也是 REGEN 数值 —— 同样两个字，词语本身没有任何东西能把它们分开。英文至少还能靠大小写区分数值那一侧。
  • Where each one shows up. On a species page the role is the tag beside the name; BREAK and REGEN are two of the six numbers in the stat readout underneath.各自出现在哪。 在物种页上，定位是名字旁边那个标签；BREAK 和 REGEN 是下面数值栏六项里的两项。

This page has no damage model and neither does the rest of the site. Nothing above converts a stat into damage, because no source we hold has the formula.这页没有伤害模型，站上其它页面也没有。上面没有任何东西把数值换算成伤害，因为我们手上的来源没有那份公式。
