Baleetle滚滚郎

Rock土 DPS输出

Baleetle has 2 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.滚滚郎 共有 2 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Baleetle is an earth species built on a single prop: a stone ball. Stone Ball Drift and Stone Ball Cutter roll it forward for 61 and 67 power, with an ice variant alongside and the basic attack behind them. Both of its stage-3 endings keep the ball and add to it, so the ball is the constant right across this family.滚滚郎是一个围绕同一件道具建起来的土系物种：石球。滚石漂移和滚石切割把它向前滚出去，威力分别 61 和 67，旁边还有一个冰属性版本，后面跟着基础攻击。它下面两个三阶终点都保留这颗球再往上加 —— 所以球是这一家子从头到尾的常量。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Hustle疾行

Holds a faster run for as long as it is on, and drains stamina the whole time. It is a state, not a dash — letting go is what stops it, and the cost is per second rather than per use. Staying in it to cross a long stretch is the expensive way to travel.开着的时候一直保持更快的移动速度，全程持续消耗体力。它是一个状态，不是一次冲刺 —— 松开才停，代价按秒算，不按次数算。想靠它跑长距离，是最费体力的走法。

Trait特性

Overloaded Engine聚热岩心

Moving and casting fill an Overload meter, and a full meter gives the next skill 40% bonus damage. Same trait name and same figure as NO.057 — but that card also snaps Sandstorm Spin to top speed, and this one stops at the damage. Compare the cards, not the names.移动和放技能积攒【过载能量】，充满后下一次技能获得 40% 伤害加成。和 NO.057 同名、数字也一样 —— 但那张卡还会让【砂暴大回环】直接进入最高转速，这张只给伤害。要比的是卡，不是名字。
