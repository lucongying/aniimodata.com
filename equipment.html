Equipment装备

Two different things in Aniimo are called equipment: the held item every Aniimo wears into a fight, and the loadout Operation: Egg Heist issues you. This page is the first of them — the 24 held items and what each one actually does, which stat each one rolls, where they drop with the publisher's own odds, and the two layers on top: enhancement to level 15, and the contract that ties one to an Aniimo. The extraction mode's gear gets a section at the end and a pointer.在 Aniimo 里「装备」指两件完全不同的东西：伊莫自己带着上战场的那件携带物，和「抢蛋行动」发给你的一整套装备。这一页讲的是前一件 —— 24 件携带物各自到底干什么、各自滚哪条属性、从哪儿掉以及官方给的几率，再加上叠在上面的两层：升到 15 级，以及把它绑到某只伊莫身上的契约。抢蛋行动那套放在文末，只留一段和一个指路。

One per Aniimo每只伊莫一件

Every Aniimo has exactly one held item slot, and what goes in it works on its own — nothing to trigger and nothing to aim. Each item is two things at once. A main stat, which is a flat bonus to one number: Damage Amp, HP, REGEN, BREAK or healing. And a core effect, which is the sentence deciding how the item wants to be used — Ferocious Fang pays out per level, Nature's Breath pays out for standing still, Avenging Gear pays out for having been hit. Two Aniimo with the same item on differ only in how well each of them can use the core effect, which is why the guide to picking one is a guide to roles.每只伊莫正好有一个携带物格子，装进去的东西自己就会生效 —— 没有要触发的，也没有要瞄准的。每件携带物同时是两样东西。一条主属性，是对某一个数字的固定加成：伤害加深、生命值、能量回复、击破，或者治疗效果。以及一条核心效果，就是那句决定这件东西该怎么用的话 —— 猛兽之牙按等级结算，Nature's Breath 靠站桩结算，复仇发条靠挨打结算。同一件东西装在两只不同的伊莫身上，差别只在各自能用出几分核心效果，这也是为什么「该带哪件」其实是「它是什么定位」的问题。

Getting one onto an Aniimo怎么装到伊莫身上

  1. Reach the Student III Pathfinder title. The slot stays locked until then, and every source we read agrees on the title.把探路者称号练到 见习 III。在那之前格子是锁着的，我们读到的每个来源在这一点上都一致。
  2. Open Aniimo Management — P on PC.打开伊莫管理 —— PC 上是 P 键。
  3. Open Training in the bottom right, then the Held Item tab at the top right.打开右下角的特训，再切到右上角的携带物标签页。
  4. Pick the item and equip it. One per Aniimo, and the stats travel with the item: moving it to another Aniimo costs you nothing but the walk.选中那件装备上去。每只伊莫一件，而属性是跟着装备走的：换给另一只伊莫，你要付的只是走过去的那几步路。

Held items used to be called Carried Items, and they used to have rune slots. They do not any more. The publisher's own launch letter is blunt about it — the random rune slots are gone and the rune system was removed entirely — which makes this the one subject on this site where a large amount of the advice online is not wrong so much as expired. If a guide tells you to farm runes, or to compare rune counts by rarity, it was written against the beta, and the slot it is describing is the one the held item goes in now.携带物以前叫「携带道具」，而且以前有符文孔。 现在没有了。官方自己的上线信写得很直白 —— 随机符文孔已经取消，符文系统整个删除 —— 这也让这里是全站唯一一个「网上一大批攻略不算错、只是过期了」的题材。如果哪篇攻略让你去刷符文、或者按稀有度比较符文数量，那是对着封测写的，而它描述的那个格子，现在装的就是携带物。

Rarity, and what the number actually is稀有度，以及那个数字到底是多少

Rarity is the whole of the difference between two copies of one item: blue Rare, purple Epic, gold Legendary. The core effect does not change with rarity and neither does the main stat's name — a Rare Ferocious Fang and a Legendary Ferocious Fang both roll Damage Amp. Only the size of that bonus moves. That much every source agrees on, which is the part worth carrying into the next paragraph.同一件东西的两份拷贝之间的差别，全部在稀有度上：蓝色稀有、紫色史诗、金色传说。核心效果不随稀有度变，主属性的名字也不变 —— 蓝色的猛兽之牙和金色的猛兽之牙滚的都是伤害加深。动的只有这条加成的大小。这一点所有来源都同意，也是唯一值得带进下一段的东西。

Rarity稀有度 Main stat主属性 Combat power战力

Epic (purple)紫色（史诗） 5% 100

And this is where the sources stop agreeing, so here are both answers. The ladder above is one database's, and it is the only one that prints all three rarities; a second prints 5% at Epic and 10% at Legendary and leaves Rare blank, which is two rows of three agreeing. A third prints a flat +10% with no rarity column at all. And two more print +20% at the top rarity, with +12% for healing where the first one says 6% — which is the same disagreement one sentence further along, because Miraculous Fleece is the one item whose main stat is Enhanced Healing Effect rather than Damage Amp, HP, REGEN or BREAK, and it scales on its own smaller ladder. So the honest answer to "how much is a Legendary item worth" is 10% or 20%, depending on who you ask, and this page is not going to pick for you. What is not in doubt is the direction.分歧就从这里开始，所以两个答案都印。 上面那张阶梯来自其中一个数据库，也是唯一一个把三档全印出来的；第二个库给了紫色 5%、金色 10%，稀有那行空着，三行里对上两行；第三个库干脆不给稀有度那一列，直接写死 +10%。另外还有两个来源把最高档写成 +20%，并把治疗写成 +12%（第一个库写的是 6%）—— 这其实是同一个分歧往前挪了一句，因为神奇暖绒是唯一一件主属性是治疗效果提升而不是伤害加深／生命值／能量回复／击破的携带物，它走的是自己那条小一点的阶梯。所以「一件金色携带物值多少」的诚实答案是 10% 或 20%，取决于你问谁，这一页不替你选。没有争议的是方向。

Twenty-four standard held items, which is the count the publisher's own data files give — they are numbered 551001 to 551024, in sequence, with no gaps and nothing either side of them. The core effects below are quoted, not paraphrased. Both databases lift them straight out of the game's files, which is why several read like a tooltip and why the wording is identical between them: there is no second version of these sentences to average. The main stat is what the item rolls. Where they come from从哪儿来

Held items come from seven places, and only one of them is worth farming. Alphas are the farm — everything else on the list is a one-off, a weekly, a rank reward or a shop, and the guides that cover this say the same thing about all six of them: take them when they come, do not build a plan on them.携带物有七个来路，其中只有一个值得刷。Alpha 才是那个农活 —— 名单上其余六个要么是一次性的、要么是每周一份、要么是称号奖励或者商店，而写这件事的攻略对剩下六个说的一致：碰上就收下，别拿它做计划。

Levelling one up to 15, and the contract on top升到 15 级，以及压在上面的契约

Every held item has a fifteen-level enhancement track, and a maxed item roughly doubles the main stat it started with. There are two ways up the track and they are not equivalent. Feed it other held items — lower or equal rarity, which is what most of the blue drops after Wayfarer III are actually for. Or spend Held Item Cores, which come from chests, Elite Training objectives and Alphas. Cores are the better route for anything you mean to keep, because they do not eat gear you might otherwise have equipped.每件携带物都有一条 15 级的强化线，练满大约能把起始那条主属性翻一倍。往上走有两条路，而且不等价。喂别的携带物 —— 稀有度同级或更低，这也是旅人 III 之后那些蓝装真正的用途。或者花携带物核心，它来自宝箱、特训目标和 Alpha。凡是你打算留着用的东西，核心都是更好的那条路，因为它不吃你本来可能装备的货。

The contract, which is the endgame layer契约，也就是终局那一层

  1. Get a Legendary held item to level 15. That is the level the item's tier 2 effect and the contract screen unlock on — neither is available a level earlier.把一件金色携带物练到 15 级。15 级正是它的二阶效果和契约界面解锁的地方 —— 少一级都开不了。
  2. Equip it on the Aniimo you want to bind it to, and open Training → Held Item → Form Contract.把它装到你想要绑定的那只伊莫身上，打开 特训 → 携带物 → 缔结契约。
  3. Pay the marks the screen asks for. One guide reports ten of them for its example. The main way to get them is reporting Alpha Aniimo you have caught at an Outpost.付界面要的印记。有一篇攻略举的例子是十个。拿到它们的主要办法，是把抓到的 Alpha 伊莫交到前哨站汇报。
  4. Note what you do not need one for: an Aniimo caught as an Alpha already has its enhanced skills, so a contract on it is marks spent to buy something it came with.顺带说清什么时候不需要契约：野外抓到的头目形态伊莫自带技能强化，给它们契约等于花印记去买一个它本来就有的东西。

Two disagreements to read before you commit anything. A contract obviously ties an item to an Aniimo. To *one* Aniimo, or to a whole species? The English guides say species, in as many words — contract one Sherro and every Sherro you own gets the bonus while a Glacy gets nothing. The Chinese guides say the individual, also in as many words — the contract applies to the Aniimo it was made with, and the enhancement is gone the moment you move the item. Nothing we could read settles it, and it is the difference between a contract being a species-wide unlock and a single-Aniimo one, so this page prints both and picks neither. The second is smaller and is probably one fact told two ways: the currency is called Mutation Marks in one place and Mark of the Strong in another, and the marks are named for a region, which suggests there is more than one flavour of them. On one point the sources do agree, and it is the useful one: the main stat works wherever the item is equipped. A contract buys the skill enhancement and nothing else.动手之前有两条分歧要读。 契约当然是把一件装备绑到一只伊莫上。绑到一只，还是绑到一个物种？英文攻略说是物种，而且说得很明确 —— 给一只 Sherro 缔结之后，你手上任何一只 Sherro 都有这份加成，而 Glacy 一点也没有。中文攻略说的是个体，同样说得很明确 —— 契约只对缔结的那只伊莫生效，装备一换手，技能强化就没了。我们读到的东西裁决不了，而这正是「契约到底是一次物种级解锁还是一次单只解锁」的差别，所以这一页两个都印，一个都不选。第二条小一些，多半是同一件事的两种说法：这种货币一处叫变异印记，另一处叫强者之印，而印记是按地区命名的，说明它不止一种口味。有一点所有来源都同意，而且是有用的那一点：主属性在哪儿装备都生效。 契约买到的只有技能强化，别的什么都没有。

The other thing called equipment另一件也叫装备的东西

Operation: Egg Heist — the extraction mode — has a loadout of its own, and it is not a held item. Nothing in it goes on an Aniimo. The guides that cover the mode describe three gear slots for the player: an Amplifier, which raises the damage you deal and the damage you take; a Protective Unit, which does the opposite; and a Backpack, which sets how much you can carry out. Chips go into them, the whole set is bought with Eggshell Coin in the mode's own shop, and all of it has durability, which is a thing no held item has. It shares the word with the rest of this page and nothing else.「抢蛋行动」是个撤离玩法，它有一套自己的装备，而那不是携带物。那套里没有任何一件是装到伊莫身上的。写这个玩法的攻略描述的是玩家自己的三个装备位：一个增幅器，它同时放大你打出去的伤害和你吃到的伤害；一个防护单元，做的是相反的事；以及一个背包，决定你能带出去多少。芯片插在它们里面，整套东西用蛋壳币在那个玩法自己的商店里买，而且它们全都有耐久 —— 这是携带物一件都没有的东西。它和这一页其余部分共用的只有一个词。

The mode itself — when it opens, its ranks, and what a run pays — is on the progression page. The coins, tickets and what the Egg Heist shop stocks are in the currency table on the items page.
