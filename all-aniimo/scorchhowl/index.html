No. 003

Scorchhowl狼斗士

Fire火 DPS输出 C

Scorchhowl has 5 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.狼斗士 共有 5 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Scorchhowl keeps the fire starter skeleton and hangs three cards on it: Burn Breaker, which at full charge spends every drop of energy you have left for 84 power, a Lightning Kick as a second dodge-strike, and a 170-power Falling Star that costs nothing. That last card is what makes the species forgiving — the finisher works on an empty bar, so there is never a turn where the species has nothing to do.狼斗士保留了火系初始的骨架，并在上面挂了三张卡：燃尽强击，满蓄力时把你剩下的能量全部花光、打出 84 威力；作为第二个回避反击的雷光踢；以及一发不耗能量的 170 威力流星坠落。最后这一张才是这个物种宽容的地方 —— 收尾技在空能量条上也能打，所以它永远不会有「什么都做不了」的回合。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Mobility探索技能

Hustle疾行

Holds a faster run for as long as it is on, and drains stamina the whole time. It is a state, not a dash — letting go is what stops it, and the cost is per second rather than per use. Staying in it to cross a long stretch is the expensive way to travel.开着的时候一直保持更快的移动速度，全程持续消耗体力。它是一个状态，不是一次冲刺 —— 松开才停，代价按秒算，不按次数算。想靠它跑长距离，是最费体力的走法。

Trait特性

Scorching Flames炽焰

Deals 25% more damage to anything it elementally counters. The 25% is a flat figure and the official text does not say whether it multiplies into the rest of the damage formula, so the number is repeated here and nothing further is claimed for it.对受到元素克制的目标，造成的伤害提高 25%。这个 25% 是定值，而官方文本没有说它会不会再乘进伤害公式的其他部分 —— 所以这里只照抄数字，不多作解释。

Skills技能

Split the way the official page splits them: Combat holds the skills you cast, Innate the basic attack. The list is the species', not the form's — a form does not change it.分法和官方页面一致：战斗技能是你会主动放的那几个，固有技能里是基础攻击。这份表属于物种，不属于形态 —— 换形态不会换掉它。

That makes the energy bar the multiplier: the same skill fired on half a bar and on a full one are two different attacks — so the card is really a question of when you are willing to spend everything at once.蓄力后向前发出强力轰击；在最大蓄力阶段会花光全部剩余能量，并且每多消耗 10 点能量就额外造成 30% 伤害。这就把能量条变成了倍率：同一次技能，半条能量和满条能量打出来是两个不同的攻击 —— 所以这张卡真正问的是：你什么时候愿意一次把它全部花掉。
