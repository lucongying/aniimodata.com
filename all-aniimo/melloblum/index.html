Melloblum魅乐薇

Grass草 Support辅助

Melloblum has 2 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.魅乐薇 共有 2 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Melloblum is the support end of the same fork: three rose cards that damage and heal at the same time, a Blasting Roses carrying its own +35% crit rate, and a Rose Garden support at no cost. The attack-and-heal cards are the ones that define it — they let it contribute damage without ever giving up a turn of healing.魅乐薇是同一个分叉里偏辅助的那一端：三张玫瑰卡同时造成伤害和治疗，一张自带 +35% 暴击率的爆裂玫瑰，以及不耗能量的玫瑰园。真正定义它的是那些「打一下顺带回一口」的卡 —— 它们让它能在不掉治疗回合的前提下参与输出。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Phantom Opening魅影破绽

Stacks of Rose Mark come from teammates' basic attacks, not its own — up to 10, and each takes 3 energy off its skill costs. At full stacks that is 30 energy cheaper per skill, which is enough to change what the sheet can afford; the catch is that it only builds while the rest of the team keeps swinging.【玫瑰印记】来自队友的普攻，不是它自己的 —— 最多 10 层，每层让它的技能少花 3 点能量。满层就是每个技能便宜 30 点，足够改变这套技能表能打出什么；前提是队伍里其他人在持续普攻。

Skills技能

Split the way the official page splits them: Combat holds the skills you cast, Innate the basic attack. The list is the species', not the form's — a form does not change it.分法和官方页面一致：战斗技能是你会主动放的那几个，固有技能里是基础攻击。这份表属于物种，不属于形态 —— 换形态不会换掉它。

So the trigger is the team's crit rate rather than this species' own output — a support card whose timing is set by whoever else is attacking.一朵玫瑰爆炸并对目标施加标记。这个标记会在目标受到 3 次暴击后绽放，绽放时基于施放者 5% 生命值治疗附近所有友方。所以触发条件是队伍的暴击率，不是这个物种自己的输出 —— 这是一张节奏由队友决定的辅助卡。
