Irisalis彩虹鸢尾

Grass草 DPS输出

Irisalis has a single form recorded so far. Its stats, type, habitat and evolution requirement are below.彩虹鸢尾 目前只记录到一个形态。它的数值、属性、栖息地和进化条件都在下面。

Overview简介

Irisalis is the second of the game's two Legendary Aniimo and the attacking half of that pair: grass, 130 ATK, 540 total, and a kit that is really one idea — put copies of itself on the field and let them shoot. Irisalis Shadow and Florae Descent each leave a 30-second double, the two coexist, and the trait turns every double into a 15-power beam whenever the species casts its signature skill or lands a basic-attack finisher. Nothing here goes through a Wild Surge: it is a Legendary with 99 Irisalis Petals between you and it, and the page carries no habitat because there is no wild spawn to name.彩虹鸢尾是游戏里两只传说伊莫中的第二只，也是这对里偏输出的那一只：草元素、130 攻击、总属性 540，整套技能其实只有一个意思 —— 把自己复制到场上去打。鸢影和花灵降诞各留一具 30 秒的分身，两具可以共存；而只要它放了招牌技能、或者普攻收尾命中，特性就会让场上的每一具分身各射一发 15 威力的光束。这里没有 Wild Surge 那条路：它是 Legendary，你和它之间隔着 99 枚 Irisalis Petals；这页也不写栖息地，因为没有野外刷新点可写。

Mobility探索技能

Fly Lv.3飞行 Lv.3

Fly Lv. 3, and that level is the entire card: no speed, no duration, no energy cost. The English wiki heads this card Pathfinding instead of naming a movement state, and the level is where the information stops — it is a rating of how this species travels, not a state you turn on.飞行 Lv.3，而这个等级就是这张卡的全部：没有速度、没有持续时间、没有消耗。英文维基给它的标题是 Pathfinding，不是某个移动状态的名字；信息到等级为止 —— 它评的是这个物种怎么移动，不是一个能打开的状态。

Trait特性

Bloom Cluster聚芳

Three clauses in one line. Whirling Blossom Rain, or the last hit of a basic attack, makes every clone on the field fire a 15-power beam; each clone hit banks 1 Dance Power, and at 9 the next skill costs 50% less EP. Then the death clause: when it goes down it turns back into a flower and blooms again, restoring itself, once every 180 seconds. The Dance Power half is a discount you steer rather than one you wait for — clones are summoned by two different cards, so how many beams a single cast fires is a placement question, not a fixed number.一句话三件事。放疾旋花雨、或者普攻收尾命中时，场上每一具分身都会射出一发 15 威力的光束；分身每命中一次积 1 点舞力，攒到 9 点，下一个技能能耗 减半。剩下的半句是死亡条款：力竭后它变回花朵再次绽放、把自己救回来，每 180 秒一次。舞力那一半是靠自己控出来的折扣，不是等出来的 —— 分身由两张不同的卡分别召唤，所以一次施放能射出几发，是排布问题，不是固定数字。

Skills技能
