Sheldon智多星

Water水 DPS输出

Sheldon has a single form recorded so far. Its stats, type, habitat and evolution requirement are below.智多星 目前只记录到一个形态。它的数值、属性、栖息地和进化条件都在下面。

Overview简介

Sheldon keeps Shelly's two skills and the basic attack, so the second stage adds stats and nothing else. The line only opens up at the third: Sherro adds a water slash, a light slam, a lightning slam, and a Torrent Blade that reaches its full size only when cast near water.智多星保留了贝壳星的两个技能和基础攻击，所以第二阶只加数值。这条线要到第三阶才打开：星骑士加上一道水系斩击、一发光系重击、一发雷系重击，以及一发只有在水边才能完全展开的激流刃。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Tidal Heart逐浪之心

In water terrain, or for a while after absorbing water, its Water damage is up 20% for 10 seconds. Worth knowing that the identically-named trait on NO.055 gives 25% for 20 seconds — same name, better numbers. Compare the cards, not the names.在水环境里，或者吸水后的一段时间内，水元素伤害提高 20%，持续 10 秒。请注意 NO.055 身上那条同名特性给的是 25%、20 秒 —— 名字一样，数字更好。要比的是卡，不是名字。

    A shell strike that picks up +30% Power in extra damage and +15% damage amp for 20 seconds if there is water nearby to activate the puddles it hits. That is this family's pattern spelled out on one card: the movement skill lays the puddles down and the damage skill spends them — so read it as a combo piece rather than as a standalone hit.一次贝壳砸击；如果附近有水、能激发命中范围内的水滩，就额外造成 30% 威力的伤害，并获得 +15% 伤害加深，持续 20 秒。这张卡把这个家族的模式写全了：位移技能负责铺水滩，伤害技能负责花掉它 —— 所以它是连招里的一环，不是单独的一次攻击。

    Rolls forward on stamina, ramming what it hits and giving stamina back as it goes — at most once every 3 seconds. It finishes by leaving a puddle behind, and that matters more than it looks: the family's other cards read puddles. So the roll is a movement tool and the thing that lays down the resource at the same time.消耗体力向前滚动，撞到的目标受伤，一路上还会回复体力 —— 最多每 3 秒回一次。滚动结束时会在原地留下一个水滩，而这一点比看上去重要：这个家族的其他卡都要读水滩。所以这一滚既是位移，也是铺资源的手段。
