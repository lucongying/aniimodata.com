Celestis星乐蒂

Dark暗 DPS输出 A

Celestis has a single form recorded so far. Its stats, type, habitat and evolution requirement are below.星乐蒂 目前只记录到一个形态。它的数值、属性、栖息地和进化条件都在下面。

Overview简介

Celestis ties for the smallest kit in the index at three cards: a 61-power bomb, a 74-power glide, and the basic attack. Both of its skills are magic, so it fights from range and never has to close the gap — which means the only real decision the species asks you to make is which of its two spells gets the 20 energy.星乐蒂以三张卡并列图鉴里最小的技能组：61 威力的一击、74 威力的滑翔冲击，加上基础攻击。它的两个技能都是魔法，所以它是远程作战、从来不用贴身 —— 也就是说，这个物种真正要你做的决定只有一个：这 20 点能量花在哪一个法术上。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Grand Sorcerer魔法大师

For 5 seconds after it casts anything, its basic attacks deal an extra 6 Power on hit. The window is short and the figure is flat — it does not scale with ATK — so what this rewards is staying in the fight after a skill rather than firing one off and backing out.释放技能后的 5 秒内，普攻命中会额外造成一次 6 威力的伤害。窗口短，数字也是定值 —— 不随攻击成长 —— 所以它奖励的是技能之后继续贴着打，而不是放完就撤。
