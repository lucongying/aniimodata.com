Tubster大号鸥

Wind风 Break击破

Tubster has 3 forms. The base stats, the trait and the skill list are the same on all of them — what changes is the type, where it lives, and what it takes to evolve.大号鸥 共有 3 个形态。基础数值、特性和技能在所有形态上完全一致 —— 变的是属性、栖息地，以及进化要付的代价。

Overview简介

Tubster is the BREAK arm: its own cards are Pause Gust, which fires two arcs that drag targets along and explode where they cross, Gale Guard for defence, and Ceasefire Drum, all sitting beside the family's borrowed tornadoes. It carries the lowest attack and the highest defence of the three siblings, which is the trade this role makes — health-bar damage given up for the guard bar.大号鸥是偏击破的那一支：它自己的卡是停顿疾风（射出两道把目标一路拖走的弧线，交汇处爆炸）、防御用的疾风守护、以及停火鼓，旁边还是那几张从家族借来的龙卷。三兄弟里它的攻击最低、防御最高 —— 这就是这个定位做的交换：把血条伤害换成护盾条。

One scale on all six axes, 0–140 — the same one on every species page.六个轴共用一套刻度 0–140，本站每一页的物种都是这套。

Trait特性

Victory Concerto胜利协奏

Three effects on one card. While shielded, BREAK efficiency +40%. Triggering a BREAK plays Concerto, cutting the whole team's stamina cost by 30% — a combat trigger paying out in exploration currency. And with 3 Movement-series buffs active, every Movement effect is 30% stronger. Read that last clause carefully: "Movement" here is a *musical* movement, the Chinese card's 乐章, not movement across the ground — it names a family of buffs belonging to this species' band, not the exploration skills.一张卡上叠了三件事。有护盾时击破效率 +40%；造成击破时吹响【协奏曲】，全队体力消耗降低 30% —— 一个战斗里的触发，付的是探索那边的账。身上有 3 个乐章系列增益时，所有乐章效果再提高 30%。最后一条要读准：这里的「Movement」是乐章的「乐章」，不是走路的「移动」；它指的是这个物种所属乐队的一套增益，和探索技能没有关系。
