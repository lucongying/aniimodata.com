Walkthrough流程

Quests任务

The main story in order with the level each step expects, the side quests worth the detour, and the steps people actually get stuck on.主线按顺序排开并标上每一步要的等级、值得绕一趟的支线，以及真正把人卡住的那几步。

How quests actually work任务系统实际上怎么运作

The game explains none of this, and most quest frustration traces back to one of them.下面这几条游戏一条都没讲，而大部分「任务卡住了」都出在它们身上。

  • Everything lives in the Log. Open it and read the Journey tab. The main story, the regional side quests and every clue you have picked up sit in the same list — there is no separate side-quest board, and the tab you have not looked at is usually where the thing you want is.一切都在 Log 里。 打开 Log，看 Journey 标签页。主线、地区支线、还有你路上捡到的所有线索，全在同一张列表里，没有单独的支线面板。你要找的东西，通常就在你还没点开的那个标签页。
  • The markers are unreliable, and often missing. Plenty of quests draw nothing on the map until you track them by hand, and some only mark their later steps once you are partway through. If an objective looks unreachable, re-track it from the Log before you start walking.地图标记不可靠，而且经常根本没有。 不少任务在你手动追踪之前，地图上什么都不画；有些要等你做到一半，才标出后面的步骤。目标看着根本到不了时，先从 Log 里取消追踪再重新追一次，别急着上路。
  • Some entries are clues, not quests. Entries headed Clue or Rumor are not handed to you by an NPC — tracking one is what starts the quest. Rumor: A Broken Helmet? is the way into the Russet Highlands legend, not a note about it.有些条目是线索，不是任务。 标着 Clue 或 Rumor 的条目不是哪个 NPC 交给你的，追踪它本身才是任务的开始。Rumor: A Broken Helmet? 是丹岩高地那条传说的入口，不是关于它的笔记。
  • Twining is a dialogue tool. Several quests will not advance unless you are Twined with a particular Aniimo: a Helmut to read Helmspeak, a Celestis to cross a barrier, an Emberpup-line Aniimo to talk to the pack, an Iris to dance. When an NPC ignores you, the question is usually what you are wearing.Twine 是对话工具。 有好几个任务必须和特定 Aniimo 处于 Twine 状态才会推进：Helmut 用来听懂 Helmspeak，Celestis 用来穿过结界，Emberpup 那条线用来和狼群说话，Iris 用来跳舞。NPC 不理你的时候，问题通常出在你身上挂着谁。
  • Time of day is a gate. A number of quests and quest steps only work at night. The day-and-night switch unlocks through the main story, and once you have it, several quests that looked broken start working.时间是道闸。 相当一部分任务和任务步骤只在夜里能做。昼夜切换跟着主线解锁，拿到之后，几个原本看着像坏掉的任务会自己好。

The promotion quests are where a run stops真正卡住进度的是晋升任务

Six promotions stand between the start of the game and the top of the ladder, and they are the reason progress dead-ends. Four of them are among the twenty-two missions above; the other two do not appear in that list at all, and they are in the table below anyway, because the ladder does not stop where the mission list does. Each one wants a character level and a finished Elite Training chapter before Dr. Lewis will sit you down for the assessment — and each one raises both your rank and your level cap. You cannot out-level your rank, so if your team has stopped growing, this is the fix rather than more catching. Experience earned while you are capped is banked, not lost, and lands the moment the cap lifts. The last cap a promotion moves is 60, and that is also the last title the game's own tables print — nothing the studio has published goes higher.从开局到梯子顶端之间站着六次晋升，进度断在这里，原因也在这里。其中四次就在上面那 22 个任务里；另外两次根本不在那张表上，我们照样收在下面这张表里 ——因为梯子并不停在任务表结束的地方。每个晋升都要求一个角色等级加一章打完的精英训练，Dr. Lewis 才会坐下来考你；而每个晋升同时抬你的学员等级和等级上限。你超不过自己的学员等级，所以队伍练不动了，该来解决这个，而不是接着抓。卡在上限期间拿到的经验会存着，不会丢，上限一开就到账。晋升能抬到的最后一个上限是 60，那也是官方自己公布的表格点到的最后一个头衔 —— 官方发布过的东西里，再往上没有了。

Side quests worth the detour值得绕一趟的支线

Optional, regional, and uneven. Most pay Glimmer, Credits, Fame and Knowledge and can wait; the ones below are the exceptions, because each ends in something you keep — an Aniimo, an evolution, a permanent unlock — rather than a purse.支线是地区性的、可有可无的，质量也参差。大多数给 Glimmer、Credits、Fame 和 Knowledge，放着也行；下面这些是例外，因为它们最后给的是能留下的东西，一只 Aniimo、一条进化线、一个永久解锁，而不是一笔钱。

The fragrance journeys香气之旅

A collection rather than a story, and the closest thing here to a long game. Elvira, in the Sea of Flowers, hands out a branch per region and each branch ends in a perfume. There are ten perfumes across eight regional branches — Forest of Falling Stars, Beast Fang Ridge, Echoback Landing, Blitzwood, Berylline Causeway, Rosetower Woods, Russet Highlands and Driftwise Meadow — and nothing forces an order, so you can take them as you reach each region.与其说是剧情，不如说是一套收集，也是这个游戏里最像长线玩法的东西。花海（Sea of Flowers）的 Elvira 按地区发分支，每个分支最后给你一瓶香水。一共十瓶香水、八个地区分支：Forest of Falling Stars、Beast Fang Ridge、Echoback Landing、Blitzwood、Berylline Causeway、Rosetower Woods、Russet Highlands、Driftwise Meadow。分支之间互不依赖，也不排顺序，走到哪个地区做哪个就行。

  • Each branch is a real quest, not a fetch. The Forest of Falling Stars one, given by Perfumer Dael at the Celestis outpost, wants a Celestis to get you through the barrier, ten ordinary Starines and three Large ones — two you pick up around the outpost and one you trade for — and it finishes with a festival cutscene. It pays the Peace of Mind perfume.每个分支都是一条真任务，不是跑腿。 比如 Celestis 前哨站的调香师 Dael 给的 Forest of Falling Stars 那条，要你变成 Celestis 穿过结界、凑十颗普通 Starine 和三颗 Large Starine（两颗在前哨站附近捡、一颗要换），最后还有一段庆典演出。它的报酬是 Peace of Mind 这瓶香水。
  • The branches are gated by other quests. The Echoback Landing branch will not open until Everlasting Echoback Song is done, and the Rosetower Woods one sits behind the Flowers and Thorns chapter. Do the regional legend first and the fragrance branch follows.分支被别的任务锁着。 Echoback Landing 那条要等 Everlasting Echoback Song 做完才开，Rosetower Woods 那条压在花与刺章节后面。先把地区传说清掉，香气分支自然会跟上。
  • The set closes at Tideblossom Coast. That final hand-in is the end of the chain, and it is the one worth planning around rather than stumbling into.整套在 Tideblossom Coast 收口。 最后那一次交付才是这条链的终点，值得提前规划，别走到那儿才发现前置没做。

Where people get stuck卡住人的那几步

The blockers that come up over and over, and what actually clears them.反复被问到的几个卡点，以及真正能解开它们的做法。

  • The objective has no marker at all. This is the normal state of several quests, not a bug to wait out. Untrack and re-track the quest from the Log's Journey tab, then read the objective text instead of watching for an arrow — a fair number of objectives describe the route in words and never draw it.目标连个标记都没有。 好几个任务本来就是这种状态，不是等一等就会好的 bug。从 Log 的 Journey 标签页取消追踪再重新追踪，然后读目标那行字，别盯着箭头看。有相当一部分目标是用文字描述路线的，从来不画出来。
  • The Sword of Bravery will not come out. You need a Helmut in the party and Twined, and the dialogue is a courage test: answer as the story's warrior would — the unyielding heart, the exhausted Helmut, and yes to pulling the sword — rather than with the cautious answer. Pulling it evolves the Helmut and summons Infergon, so be ready for a Lv 50 fight.Sword of Bravery 拔不出来。 队伍里要有 Helmut 并且处于 Twine 状态，那段对话是个勇气测试。按故事里那个战士的方式回答：谈谈不屈的心、说说那只筋疲力尽的 Helmut、最后答应拔剑，别选那个谨慎的答案。拔出来会让 Helmut 进化，同时招来 Infergon，所以先做好打 50 级的准备。
  • The Echonite trail dies partway. The blue resonance runs to the next node, not to the quest marker, which is why following the marker loses it. Check the clock is still Nighttime, look up — several nodes sit on the ribs and ledges above you — and re-strike the last Echonite you lit to restart the trail.Echonite 的共鸣链断在半路。 蓝色共鸣是连着下一个节点的，不是连着任务标记，跟着标记走反而会跟丢。先确认时间还在 Nighttime，然后抬头看，好几个节点在你上方的肋骨和崖沿上。断了就回头再敲一次刚点亮的那个 Echonite，链条会重新接上。
  • The Voxel Cube hunt stalls short of nine. The first cube is fixed at the glitching sign outside ChillBeanz; the other eight come in any order. The markers for the last three only appear once you have six, so six is the number to push to.Voxel Cube 收到九个之前卡住。 第一个固定在 ChillBeanz 门口那块花屏招牌，另外八个顺序随意。最后三个的标记要等你先拿到六个才会出现，所以六个是那道坎。
  • Secret of the Golden Helmet never shows up in the Log. It is a hidden scene in the Russet Highlands, not a trackable quest, which is exactly why it is missed. The helmet will not come off unless you are Twined with a Cloak-ability Aniimo.Secret of the Golden Helmet 从来不在 Log 里出现。 它是丹岩高地的一处隐藏场景，不是能追踪的任务，也正因为它不在列表里才那么容易被漏掉。队伍里没有带 Cloak 能力的 Aniimo，那顶头盔摘不下来。
  • Lumenfly Valley pushes you back at the barrier. The valley only admits Flutternyms, so Twine with one before you approach — and note that Nicole stays behind, so you are on your own in there.在 Lumenfly Valley 的结界前被弹回来。 这个山谷只放 Flutternym 进去，所以靠近之前先 Twine 成 Flutternym。另外注意 Nicole 不会跟进来，里面只有你自己。
  • Warrior has not moved on. It is not stuck: it advances one step per server reset and takes three days, and every step is at night. Set the world clock and come back after the reset rather than re-talking to it.Warrior 推不动。 它没卡住，只是每过一天服务器重置才推进一步，一共三天，而且每一步都在夜里。把世界时间调好，等重置之后再回来，别反复找它对话。
  • Come out Floret will not complete. It wants all six regional Iris forms kept, so releasing one you already caught puts you back to hunting it.Come out Floret 交不了。 它要求六种地区 Iris 形态都留在手上，所以把你已经抓到的那只放生了，就得重新去刷。

What repeats会重来的那些

Quest-shaped content that comes back, and when — the things to do on a day when the story is gated.剧情被等级卡住的那天，该做的其实是这些。

  • Daily Activity. Five tasks, 700 points on offer against a 500-point threshold, so the spare 200 is your room to skip one on a busy day. It opens once Alpha Turbo is caught; open it with F4. It resets at 4:00 AM server time — UTC+8 on APAC, UTC+1 on Europe, UTC-5 on America — and packs you have not claimed do not carry over.日常活跃度。 五个任务共 700 点，门槛是 500 点，那多出来的 200 点就是你忙的时候可以少做一项的余量。抓到 Alpha Turbo 之后开启，按 F4 打开。每天服务器时间凌晨 4 点重置（亚太 UTC+8、欧洲 UTC+1、美洲 UTC-5），没领的礼包不会顺延到第二天。
  • Elite Training. The courses every promotion is gated behind, and the thing to grind when one will not open. Core courses are the ones the promotions name; the elective paths pay separately and fill the level gap faster than catching does.精英训练。 每个晋升都压在它上面，晋升开不了的时候该刷的就是这个。核心课程是晋升点名要的那些；选修路线单独结算，补等级缺口比抓宠快。
  • Homeland jobs. Daily quests from your Homeland, rerollable a limited number of times a day. They are the reason to keep the Homeland's element roles staffed rather than treating it as decoration.家园委托。 每天从家园接，可以重掷有限的次数。这才是把家园各属性岗位填满的理由，别只把它当摆设。
